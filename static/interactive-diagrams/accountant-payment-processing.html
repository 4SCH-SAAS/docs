💡 Best Practice: Always count cash carefully before recording payment. Issue manual receipt first, then record digitally immediately - don't batch at end of day.
⚠️ Security Note: Store cash securely in the safe until bank deposit. Never leave cash unsecured, even temporarily.

💡 Reconciliation Best Practices:
  • ✓ Reconcile at least monthly (weekly is better)
  • ✓ Keep deposit slips organized chronologically
  • ✓ Use clear payment references for easier matching
  • ✓ Resolve discrepancies immediately - don't let them pile up
  • ✓ Document all adjustments with explanations
⚠️ Common Discrepancies:
  • Deposit in bank but not in 4SCH: Forgot to record payment
  • Payment in 4SCH but not in bank: Cash not deposited yet
  • Amount differences: Recording error or incorrect deposit
  • Timing differences: Payment recorded but deposited next month
