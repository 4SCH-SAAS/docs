💡 Online Payment Tips:
  • ✓ Ensure you have sufficient funds before starting
  • ✓ Keep your OTP/PIN ready
  • ✓ Use a stable internet connection
  • ✓ Save the payment receipt immediately
  • ✓ Verify invoice shows "PAID" after payment
⚠️ Important: If payment is deducted but invoice still shows "Unpaid", don't pay again! Contact school office with your transaction reference. The issue usually resolves within 24 hours.

💡 Bank Transfer Best Practices:
  • ✓ Always include invoice number in narration/description
  • ✓ Include student name in transfer description
  • ✓ Save bank alert and screenshot immediately
  • ✓ Send proof to school office same day
  • ✓ Follow up after 24-48 hours if not reflected
⚠️ Important Narration Format:
Always use: "Invoice [Number] - [Student Name]"
Example: "Invoice INV-2026-0234 - Amara Okonkwo"
This helps the accountant match your payment quickly.

💡 Cash Payment Tips:
  • ✓ Prepare exact amount if possible
  • ✓ Visit during office hours (avoid rush times)
  • ✓ Keep manual receipt safe - it's your proof
  • ✓ Verify accountant records payment in system immediately
  • ✓ Check portal later to confirm "PAID" status
⚠️ Important: Always insist on a receipt immediately. If accountant says "I'll give it later," politely wait. Never leave without a receipt for cash payment!

💡 Payment Plan Tips:
  • ✓ Request payment plan early - don't wait until after due date
  • ✓ Be honest about your financial situation
  • ✓ Propose realistic installment amounts you can afford
  • ✓ Stick to agreed payment dates to maintain trust
  • ✓ Communicate immediately if you'll miss a payment
⚠️ Important: Payment plans are a privilege, not a right. Schools are more likely to approve if you have a good payment history. Always honor your commitments to maintain this benefit.
