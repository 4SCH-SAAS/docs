💡 Creating Effective Assignments:
  • ✓ Write clear, specific instructions
  • ✓ Set realistic deadlines (give students enough time)
  • ✓ Specify format requirements (PDF, Word, handwritten, etc.)
  • ✓ Attach reference materials or examples
  • ✓ State grading criteria clearly
⚠️ Best Practice: Create assignments at least 3-5 days before due date to give students adequate preparation time. Avoid creating assignments with same-day deadlines.

💡 Effective Feedback Practices:
  • ✓ Be specific - "Good grammar" vs "Excellent use of complex sentences"
  • ✓ Balance positive and constructive comments
  • ✓ Provide actionable suggestions for improvement
  • ✓ Reference specific parts of their work
  • ✓ Be encouraging even when work needs improvement
⚠️ Grading Consistency: Grade all submissions for one assignment in the same sitting when possible. This ensures consistent application of standards and fair grading across all students.

💡 Assignment Management Tips:
  • ✓ Check statistics regularly to monitor progress
  • ✓ Send reminders 1-2 days before deadline
  • ✓ Extend deadlines fairly if majority struggling
  • ✓ Avoid major edits after students start submitting
  • ✓ Archive completed assignments for future reference
⚠️ Deleting Published Assignments: Be very careful when deleting published assignments with student submissions. All student work and grades will be permanently lost. Consider archiving instead.
