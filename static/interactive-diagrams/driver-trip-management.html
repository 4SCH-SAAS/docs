💡 Best Practices:
  • ✓ Start trip when you begin your route (enables parent tracking)
  • ✓ Always confirm student identity before marking present
  • ✓ Wait 2-3 minutes at each stop for late students
  • ✓ Mark absent students so parents are notified
  • ✓ Safety is priority - never rush
⚠️ Safety Note: If a student is not at the pickup point, mark them absent and notify parent if possible. Do not wait beyond reasonable time as it delays other students.

💡 Drop-off Safety Protocol:
  • ✓ Always verify parent/guardian identity before handover
  • ✓ For young students, wait until parent arrives
  • ✓ Perform final bus check - ensure no student left behind
  • ✓ If parent doesn't show, contact school immediately
  • ✓ Never leave young students unattended
⚠️ Critical: ALWAYS perform a final bus check before completing the trip. Walk through the entire bus to ensure no student is left behind or sleeping.

⚠️ EMERGENCY PROTOCOL:
  • Step 1: Call Emergency Services (112/767)
  • Step 2: Ensure student safety
  • Step 3: Call school immediately
  • Step 4: Report in app only after situation is secure
  • Never delay emergency response to use the app!
💡 Common Issues to Report:
  • Vehicle: Mechanical problems, tire issues, fuel problems
  • Route: Road closures, heavy traffic, accidents blocking route
  • Student: Illness, behavior issues, missing student
  • Other: Weather delays, schedule conflicts

  • ✓ Apply for leave well in advance (at least 3 days for planned leave)
  • ✓ Provide clear reason for leave request
