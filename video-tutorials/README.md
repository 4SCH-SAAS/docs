# 4SCH Video Tutorials

A comprehensive video tutorial library for 4SCH School Management System. Learn everything from getting started to advanced features through professional, accessible video content.

---

## Overview

The 4SCH Video Tutorials library contains **21 professional videos** organized into 4 series, covering all aspects of the platform. Whether you're a school administrator, teacher, parent, or student, you'll find videos tailored to your role and needs.

### Key Features

- **Role-Based Learning Paths**: Customized videos for each user type
- **Comprehensive Coverage**: From basic setup to advanced features
- **Accessibility First**: All videos include captions and descriptions
- **Mobile-Friendly**: Optimized for phones, tablets, and computers
- **Regular Updates**: Content updated as new features are released

## How to Use This Library

### Watching Videos

1. **On the Web**: Visit the 4SCH Help Center or this video library
2. **In-App Help**: Access videos through the Help (?) icon in 4SCH
3. **Mobile**: Download videos through the 4SCH mobile app
4. **YouTube**: Full playlist available on [4SCH YouTube Channel](https://youtube.com/@4sch)

### Features

- **Captions**: All videos include English captions for accessibility
- **Chapters**: Larger videos are divided into chapters for easy navigation
- **Transcripts**: Full text transcripts available for every video
- **Downloadable**: Most videos can be downloaded for offline viewing
- **Mobile Optimized**: Videos stream on all devices

### Search & Discover

Use the metadata to find videos by:
- **Topic**: Search by feature (attendance, grades, fees, etc.)
- **Role**: Filter by your user role
- **Skill Level**: Find beginner, intermediate, or advanced content
- **Series**: Browse by series or topic

## Accessibility

### For Viewers with Hearing Impairments
- All videos include full captions
- Captions are synchronized with audio
- Download caption files (VTT format) separately if needed

### For Viewers with Vision Impairments
- Audio descriptions available for complex visual content
- Full text transcripts for all videos
- High contrast interface options

### Technical Requirements
- **Minimum Browser**: Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+
- **Internet Speed**: 2+ Mbps for smooth streaming
- **Screen Size**: Optimized for phones (360px+), tablets, and monitors
- **Captions**: Automatically displayed; turn on/off with CC button

## FAQ

### Can I download videos for offline viewing?
Yes! Most videos can be downloaded from the Help Center or mobile app. File sizes are optimized for storage.

### Are videos available in other languages?
Currently, all videos are in English with English captions. Translated versions are planned for Q3 2024.

### How often are videos updated?
Videos are reviewed and updated as features change. Check the "Last Updated" date on each video.

### Can I embed videos on my school website?
Yes! Videos are available with embeddable links. Contact support@4sch.com for embedding permissions.

### Why do some videos seem long?
Longer videos are divided into chapters. Use chapters to navigate to specific topics or watch the entire video for comprehensive learning.

### Do I need to watch videos in order?
The Introduction Series (01-03) should be watched in order. Other series can be watched as needed for your role.
