# Video Script: Quick Start Guide

**Duration:** 7 minutes  
**Target Audience:** First-time users of any role  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Log in to 4SCH for the first time
- Navigate the main dashboard
- Locate key features relevant to your role
- Customize your profile and preferences
- Know where to find help

---

**[VISUAL]** 4SCH logo with "Quick Start Guide" subtitle, clock icon

**[MUSIC]** Encouraging, upbeat background music

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Getting started with 4SCH is simple. This quick start guide will have you up and running in minutes, whether you're an administrator, teacher, parent, or student."

## [00:15-00:45] Prerequisites & Login (30 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Showing login page on mobile and web, username/password fields

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Before you begin, make sure you have:
- Your username or email address
- Your initial password (usually provided by your school administrator)
- A web browser or the 4SCH mobile app

To log in, visit www.4sch.com and enter your credentials. First-time users will be prompted to change their password for security."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Login: www.4sch.com"
- "Use Email or Username"
- "Change Password on First Login"

## [00:45-01:30] Your First Login (45 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Screen recording of login process, password change dialog

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"When you log in for the first time, 4SCH asks you to create a new password. This is required for security - use a combination of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Your password should be at least 8 characters long.

Next, you may see a tutorial overlay. You can skip this and access it later from Help, or follow along to learn the basic navigation."

**[VISUAL]** Dashboard loads, initial setup wizard appears

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"After login, you'll land on your role-specific dashboard. The layout and options you see depend on your user role."

## [01:30-02:30] Dashboard Overview (60 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Highlighting different dashboard sections

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Let's explore the main dashboard structure. Every user sees:

The Navigation Menu on the left shows all available features for your role. Click any icon to access that section.

The Top Bar includes your user profile, notifications, and settings. You can access your account from here.

The Main Content Area displays your role-specific dashboard with key metrics and quick actions.

For Administrators, you'll see school statistics, recent activities, and quick links to user management.

For Teachers, you'll see your class list, today's schedule, and pending attendance.

For Parents, you'll see your child's dashboard with attendance and grades.

For Students, you'll see your timetable, assignments, and grades."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Left Menu - Navigation"
- "Top Bar - Account & Notifications"
- "Main Area - Role-Specific Dashboard"

## [02:30-03:30] Setting Up Your Profile (60 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Screen recording of profile settings page

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Next, complete your profile. Click the profile icon in the top-right corner and select 'My Profile' or 'Account Settings'."

### Update Your Information
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Update your:
- Full name and preferred name
- Email address and phone number
- Profile photo (helps others recognize you)
- Notification preferences
- Language preference

Make sure your contact information is current so others can reach you and you receive important notifications."

**[VISUAL]** Showing profile fields being filled in

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Take time to upload a profile photo - it makes communication more personal and helps build community."

---

## [03:30-04:15] Customizing Notifications (45 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Notification settings panel with toggles and options

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"4SCH keeps you informed through notifications. Customize what you receive and how.

Go to Settings > Notifications and configure:
- Which events trigger notifications (attendance, grades, messages, fees)
- Delivery method (in-app, email, SMS)
- Timing (immediate or daily digest)

You can turn off notifications you don't need to reduce email overload, but we recommend keeping important alerts active."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Customize by Role"
- "Choose Delivery Methods"
- "Daily Digest Option"

---

## [04:15-05:00] Finding Key Features (45 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Walking through menu navigation for different roles

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Here's a quick map of where to find common features:

For Attendance - Teachers go to Academics > Attendance. Administrators go to Reports > Attendance Reports.

For Grades - Teachers go to Academics > Grades. Parents and Students go to Dashboard > My Grades.

For Messaging - All users go to Communication > Messages or click the chat icon.

For Reports - Go to Reports section (available based on your role).

For Settings - Click your profile icon > Settings or School Settings (admin only)."

**[VISUAL]** Menu items being highlighted

Download from:

Log in with the same credentials as the web version. Your data syncs automatically across all devices."
