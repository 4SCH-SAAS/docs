# Video Script: User Roles Explained

**Duration:** 8 minutes  
**Target Audience:** New users trying to understand their role  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Understand the 7 primary user roles in 4SCH
- Learn what each role can and cannot do
- Identify which role applies to you
- Know how to request role changes

---

## [00:00-00:20] INTRO (20 sec)

**[VISUAL]** 4SCH logo with "User Roles Explained" title card  
**[MUSIC]** Professional, informative background music

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Every user in 4SCH has a specific role that determines what they can see and do. This video explains all seven user roles and helps you understand your responsibilities within the system."

---

## [00:20-01:00] Why Roles Matter (40 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Animated icons showing different users, lock/unlock symbols for permissions

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"4SCH uses roles to protect data security and ensure everyone only accesses what they need. Your role determines:
- What information you can view
- What actions you can perform
- Who you can communicate with
- What reports you can generate

This protects student privacy and prevents accidental changes to important school data."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Security & Privacy Protection"
- "Clear Responsibilities"
- "Data Access Control"

---

## [01:00-02:15] School Administrator (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Admin dashboard with full menu options, settings panel

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"School Administrators are the system managers. They control the overall configuration and security of 4SCH for their entire school."

### Responsibilities
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Administrators can:
- Create and manage user accounts for all roles
- Configure school settings and academic calendar
- Set up fee structures and payment methods
- Manage student enrollment and transfers
- Create backup courses and edit school information
- Generate comprehensive reports
- Control system-wide permissions and security settings
- Manage admin approvals for workflows"

### What They Can't Do
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"For data integrity, administrators cannot:
- Mark attendance (that's teachers' responsibility)
- Enter grades (teachers and class coordinators do this)
- View private parent-teacher messages
- Delete historical records without proper audit"

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "System Configuration"
- "User Management"
- "Financial Oversight"
- "Reporting & Analytics"

---

## [02:15-03:30] Teachers (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Teacher mobile app interface, marking attendance, entering grades

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Teachers are the primary daily users. They manage academic activities and communicate with students and parents."

### Responsibilities
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Teachers can:
- Mark student attendance daily
- Create and assign homework
- Enter grades and exam results
- Conduct assessments and quizzes
- Create timetables and class schedules
- Message parents about student progress
- View student academic records
- Submit attendance reports"

### What They Can't Do
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Teachers cannot:
- Access financial/fee information
- Create new user accounts
- Change school-wide settings
- View other teachers' classes (unless assigned)
- Delete or modify historical grades"

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Daily Attendance"
- "Academic Management"
- "Parent Communication"
- "Class Administration"

---

## [03:30-04:45] Class Coordinators (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Class coordinator dashboard, class management tools

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Class Coordinators manage administrative duties for their assigned classes. They work between teachers and administrators."

### Responsibilities
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Class Coordinators can:
- Manage their class roster and student assignments
- Review and approve attendance reports
- Enter grades and generate report cards
- Manage class-specific communications
- Generate class performance reports
- Approve student transfers within class
- Coordinate with teachers in their class
- Create and manage class announcements"

### What They Can't Do
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Class Coordinators cannot:
- Create new users or assign roles
- Access financial systems
- Modify school-wide settings
- Override teacher-entered grades without approval"

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Class Management"
- "Grade Administration"
- "Class Reporting"
- "Student Coordination"

---

## [04:45-05:45] Parents (60 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Parent mobile app showing child's dashboard, fees, messages

### Responsibilities
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Parents can:
- View their child's attendance records
- Check grades and report cards
- Pay school fees online
- Receive notifications about absences and grades
- Message teachers about their child
- View announcements from school
- Update emergency contact information
- Download receipts for fee payments"

### What They Can't Do
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Parents cannot:
- View other students' information
- Mark attendance
- Change grades
- Access school financial data
- View teacher-only information"

**[VOICEOVER]:**

- View their timetable and class schedule

- Check exam results and grades
- View school announcements
- Message teachers with academic questions

### What They Can't Do
**[VOICEOVER]:**

- View other students' grades or information

## [06:45-07:30] Other Support Roles (45 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Grid showing Finance Officer and Librarian roles

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"4SCH includes two additional specialized roles:

Finance Officers manage all financial transactions, fee collections, and generate financial reports. They can view payment histories, create invoices, and reconcile accounts - but cannot modify student or teacher data.

Librarians manage library resources, check-outs, and inventory. They track which students have borrowed materials and can generate library reports."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Finance Officer - Financial Management"
- "Librarian - Resource Management"
