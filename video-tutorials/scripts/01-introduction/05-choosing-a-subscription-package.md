# Video Script: Choosing a Subscription Package

**Duration:** 7 minutes
**Target Audience:** New School Admins who have just signed in for the first time
**Learning Objectives:**
- Tell the difference between **Recurring** and **Prepaid** subscription packages
- Pick a package from the catalog and complete payment confidently
- Recognise when a **Custom Package** has already been assigned by your Super Admin
- Find your current plan, your bills, and your receipts at any time

"There are two ways your school ends up on a subscription plan in 4SCH.

The first is **self-serve**. You open the Subscriptions page yourself, browse the available packages, pick the one that fits your school, and pay for it through your school's preferred payment provider.

The second is when **a custom plan has already been assigned to you** by your 4SCH support contact. This is common for new schools who agreed pricing with the team before going live, and for very large schools whose needs don't quite fit the standard packages.

Both paths land you in the same place: a school that can actually use every feature you're entitled to. Let's look at each."

## [01:00-02:30] Path A: Self-Serve From the Catalog (90 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- School Admin dashboard
- Click sidebar → **Settings → Subscriptions**
- Page loads with a grid of packages
- Highlight a tab/filter selector at the top: **Recurring | Prepaid**
- Hover over a **Recurring** package card — tooltip shows "billed every cycle, auto-renews"
- Click the **Prepaid** tab — cards re-arrange to show one-off packages
- Each card shows: package name, price, billing cycle in days, feature list, and a **Subscribe** button

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"To browse the catalog yourself, sign in as the School Admin, then click **Settings → Subscriptions** in the sidebar.

You'll see a grid of available packages, with two tabs at the top: **Recurring** and **Prepaid**.

A **Recurring** package bills your school every billing cycle and renews automatically — for example, a monthly plan that charges the same amount on the same day every month.

A **Prepaid** package is a one-off purchase for a fixed number of days — for example, a 90-day pack that covers your school for one school term, with no automatic renewal at the end.

Each card shows you the package name, the price, the billing cycle in days, and the list of features the plan includes. Take your time — the differences between plans usually come down to user limits, included modules, and how much storage you get.

Some packages may be marked **Trial** — these give you a short window to try a higher-tier plan at no cost. Trial plans automatically end on their expiry date, so set a reminder to pick a paid plan before then."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Settings → Subscriptions"
- "Recurring = renews automatically"
- "Prepaid = one-off, no auto-renew"
- "Trial = free, time-limited"

## [02:30-04:00] Subscribing and Paying (90 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Click **Subscribe** on a chosen recurring package
- A confirmation modal summarises the package, price, and billing cycle
- Click **Continue to Payment**
- Page redirects to the connected payment provider (e.g. Paystack) with the school's amount already filled in
- Complete payment with a test card
- Provider redirects back to 4SCH with a green "Payment Successful" banner
- Page automatically reloads to show the new **Current Plan** card at the top
- Bell-icon notification: "Your subscription is active until [date]"

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"Found the plan you want? Click **Subscribe** on its card. A confirmation window appears showing exactly what you're about to pay for and the billing cycle. Double-check it, and click **Continue to Payment**.

4SCH hands you over to your school's chosen payment provider — Paystack, Flutterwave, or Stripe, depending on what's connected in your **Payment Configuration** settings. The amount is pre-filled, so just enter your card details and complete the payment as you normally would.

When the payment goes through, your provider sends you back to 4SCH with a green **Payment Successful** message, and 4SCH does three things automatically:

- Your subscription becomes active, and your **end date** moves forward by the billing cycle
- Any premium features that were locked while you were on a trial or free plan unlock immediately
- A receipt appears in your payment history, ready to download whenever you need it

If your internet drops between the payment provider and 4SCH, don't worry — 4SCH will still recognise the payment automatically within a minute or two. Just refresh the Subscriptions page."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Subscribe → Confirm → Pay → Done"
- "Receipt saved automatically"
- "Safe on a flaky connection"

:::tip Before you click Subscribe
If you don't see a payment provider option at all when you click **Subscribe**, your Super Admin still needs to connect a provider in **Settings → Payment Configuration**. Ask them to do that first — it only takes a couple of minutes.
:::

## [04:00-05:30] Path B: Custom Plan Already Assigned for You (90 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Same **Settings → Subscriptions** page, but this time:
  - A large card at the top reads "Current Plan: **Sunshine Academy Custom Plan** — assigned by 4SCH support"
  - The package cards below are read-only / greyed out with a small lock icon
  - A "Talk to your support contact" link sits at the bottom right
- Click the **Current Plan** card → expands to show feature list, billing cycle, and the date the plan was assigned

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"If your 4SCH support contact has already set up a custom plan for your school, you'll see it the moment you open **Settings → Subscriptions**. There's a big **Current Plan** card at the top of the page with your school's plan name and the words **assigned by 4SCH support** underneath.

The packages in the catalog below it will look greyed out — that's intentional. Because your plan was set up specifically for you, switching it requires the 4SCH support team's help. If you need anything changed — adding more users, extending the end date, switching to a recurring schedule — use the **Talk to your support contact** link at the bottom right of the page.

Click your **Current Plan** card to expand it. You'll see exactly what features are included, your billing cycle in days, and the date the plan was assigned. This is the same information your support contact has on their side, so you're looking at a single source of truth.

A custom plan still uses the same renewal and payment behaviour as any other plan — the only difference is that you didn't pick it from the catalog yourself."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Current Plan card = the plan your school is on"
- "Catalog greyed out = changes go through support"
- "Use 'Talk to support' link to request changes"

## [05:30-06:30] Bills, Receipts, and Renewal Reminders (60 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Click **Settings → Subscriptions → History** tab
- A list of past bills with status badges: **Paid** (green), **Pending** (yellow), **Overdue** (red)
- Hover a row → tooltip showing the amount, payment provider, and download-receipt icon
- Click the receipt icon → PDF preview opens in a new tab
- Pan back to dashboard → highlight the small expiry-warning banner that appears when your plan is close to renewal

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"Every subscription invoice — paid, pending, or overdue — lives in **Settings → Subscriptions → History**.

Each row shows the bill date, the amount, which payment provider was used, and a status badge. Click the small receipt icon on any **Paid** row to download a PDF receipt for your records.

A small reminder about renewals: 4SCH shows an **expiry warning banner** on your School Admin dashboard a few days before your current plan ends. You can also see your exact end date any time on the **Current Plan** card. Renewing early is always safe — you don't lose any days from the plan you're already on; 4SCH just adds the new days on top."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Subscriptions → History"
- "Paid · Pending · Overdue"
