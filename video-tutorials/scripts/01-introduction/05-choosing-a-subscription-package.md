# Video Script: Choosing Your Subscription Package

**Duration:** 7 minutes
**Target Audience:** Newly signed-in School Admins who need to lock in (or recognise) the subscription package their school will run on
**Learning Objectives:**
- Understand the two ways a package is set on your account
- Self-serve a preset package from the catalog and complete payment
- Recognise when a custom package has been assigned for you
- Know where to view your current plan, bills and receipts
- Understand what happens at expiry and how the grace period works

## [00:20-01:00] Two Ways a Package Is Set (40 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Split-screen graphic, two cards:
- Left: **"You choose it yourself"** with a hand-tapping-a-card icon — "Self-serve from the catalog"
- Right: **"Already assigned for you"** with a gift-box icon — "Custom package set by your 4SCH support contact"

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"There are two ways a subscription package is set on a 4SCH account.

The first is **self-serve**. You open the package catalog yourself, compare the preset packages, and click Subscribe on the one that fits your school. You pay online and the plan starts immediately.

The second is when a **custom package has already been assigned to you** by your 4SCH support contact. This happens when you've agreed a tailored deal with our team — perhaps a discounted rate, a longer trial, or a feature combination that isn't in the public catalog. In that case, your current plan is already in place when you sign in.

Let's look at both."

## [01:00-03:00] Path A: Self-Serving from the Preset Catalog (120 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- School Admin dashboard
- Cursor clicks **Settings** in the left menu, then **Subscription**
- The Subscription page opens — a tab switcher at the top says **Recurring** and **Prepaid**
- Show the **Recurring** catalog: a row of cards, each with a package name, monthly price, and feature limits (number of students, number of staff, modules included)
- Cursor hovers a card — the **Subscribe** button appears
- Click **Subscribe** on the Silver card
- A confirmation dialog: "Subscribe to Silver — ₦15,000 / month"
- Click **Proceed to Payment**
- The Paystack checkout window opens
- Demo card entered, payment confirmed
- Browser returns to 4SCH with a green banner: "Subscription active. Thank you!"
- The Subscription page now shows the Silver card as **Current Plan** with a new end date

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"To pick a package yourself, sign in and go to **Settings → Subscription**.

You'll see two tabs at the top: **Recurring** and **Prepaid**. Recurring packages renew automatically each month or term. Prepaid packages are pay-as-you-go — you pay once for a fixed period, and you'll be reminded when it's time to renew.

Each package shows three important things at a glance:
- The **price** — monthly, termly, or for the prepaid period
- The **student and staff limits** — for example, up to 500 students, up to 30 staff
- The **modules included** — things like Fees, Exams, Continuous Assessment, Online Classes, Library, Transport

Compare the cards side by side. When you've found one that fits your school, click **Subscribe**.

A confirmation dialog appears showing the package name and the amount. Click **Proceed to Payment**, and Paystack — or Flutterwave, or Stripe, depending on what your school admin has set up — opens in a new window.

Enter your payment details and complete the payment. As soon as it goes through, the window closes and you're back in 4SCH with a green confirmation banner. Your new plan is active straight away, and the Subscription page now shows the package you chose marked as **Current Plan** with the end date filled in.

That's it — you're live."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Settings → Subscription"
- "Tabs: Recurring · Prepaid"
- "Subscribe → Pay → Active immediately"

:::tip Paying for a school in Nigeria
4SCH's most common payment option in Nigeria is **Paystack**, which accepts cards, bank transfer, and USSD. **Flutterwave** is also supported. Your accountant can configure which gateways are switched on under **Settings → Payment Configuration**.
:::

## [03:00-04:30] Path B: A Custom Package Has Been Assigned for You (90 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- A different school admin signs in for the first time
- The dashboard shows a friendly banner at the top: **"Your school is on the *Custom — Federal Pilot* plan, expires 31 Dec 2026"**
- Cursor clicks **Settings → Subscription**
- The Subscription page opens
- Instead of the full catalog, it shows a **Current Plan** card prominently at the top — package name, included modules, student/staff limits, expiry date
- Below the card, the catalog area shows preset packages with their **Subscribe** buttons greyed out, and a soft message: *"Your school is currently on a custom plan assigned by your 4SCH support contact. Contact support to change plans."*

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"If your 4SCH support contact set up a custom package for you when they provisioned your school, you'll see things slightly differently.

Right at the top of the dashboard, a friendly banner tells you which plan your school is on — the name, and when it expires. There's nothing for you to choose, because the plan is already active.

If you open **Settings → Subscription**, you'll see your custom plan displayed as the **Current Plan** at the top of the page, with all of its included modules, limits and the expiry date laid out clearly.

You'll still see the preset package catalog below your current plan — but the Subscribe buttons on the preset packages are greyed out, and a short message explains why: your school is on a custom plan, and any change has to go through your 4SCH support contact.

If you'd like to switch to a preset package, or change the terms of your custom one, send an email to your support contact — they'll handle it on the back end and you'll see the updated plan reflected next time you refresh."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Banner: 'Your school is on the [Plan Name] plan'"
- "Custom plan → contact support to change"

## [04:30-05:45] Where to Find Bills, Receipts and Renewals (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Still on **Settings → Subscription**, scroll down past the Current Plan card
- Section titled **Billing History** appears — a table with columns: Date, Description, Amount, Status, Action
- Click the **Download** action on one row — a PDF receipt opens with the school logo, plan name, amount and a transaction reference
- Switch back to the page, click the **Receipt** action on a different row to see it inline
- For a recurring plan: highlight the **Auto-renew** toggle near the top

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"Now that your subscription is set up, the **Settings → Subscription** page is also where you come back for everything billing-related.

Scroll down past your current plan and you'll find the **Billing History** table. Every payment your school has ever made for 4SCH is listed here: the date, what it was for, how much it cost, and whether it's marked Paid.

For any paid bill, click the **Download** button in the Actions column — a printable PDF receipt opens with your school logo, the plan name, the amount and a transaction reference. Keep these for your records — they're handy at year-end and when filing for tax purposes.

If you're on a **recurring plan**, look at the top of the page for the **Auto-renew** toggle. When it's switched on, 4SCH automatically generates the next invoice as your end date approaches, and your stored payment method is charged. Switch it off if you'd rather pay manually each cycle.

If you're on a **prepaid plan**, you'll see a reminder banner appear as you approach the end date. Click **Renew** any time to top up for another period."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Settings → Subscription → Billing History"
- "Download receipts as PDFs"
- "Recurring: Auto-renew toggle · Prepaid: Renew button"

## [05:45-06:30] What Happens at Expiry (45 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- A calendar with two dates highlighted: today and the expiry date
- Animation showing a yellow warning banner appearing on the dashboard a few days before expiry
- After expiry: the dashboard shows a soft, friendly banner: "Your subscription expired on ___. You're in your grace period — pay now to avoid interruption."
- Cursor clicks the **Pay Now** button in the banner; the payment flow opens

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"4SCH gives you plenty of warning before your subscription runs out.

A yellow warning banner appears on your dashboard a few days before the expiry date. You can click straight from the banner to renew without having to navigate anywhere.

If the expiry date passes and the bill hasn't been paid, you enter a short **grace period**. During the grace period your school can still sign in and everything keeps working normally — there's just a friendly reminder banner at the top.

If the grace period ends without payment, access to premium features is paused until the next invoice is settled. Your data is never deleted — it's all there waiting as soon as you renew."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Warning banner before expiry"
- "Grace period: everything still works"
- "Your data is safe — renew any time"
