# Video Script: Teacher's Complete Guide

**Duration:** 20 minutes (4 chapters)  
**Target Audience:** Teachers  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Mark attendance efficiently
- Create and manage assignments
- Enter grades and exam results
- Communicate with parents and students

---

## CHAPTER 1: Getting Started & Dashboard (5 min)

### [00:00-00:20] INTRO
**[VISUAL]** Teacher dashboard on mobile and web
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Welcome, teachers! This guide shows you how to use 4SCH for daily teaching tasks - from marking attendance in seconds to grading assignments and communicating with parents. Let's get started!"

### [00:20-02:00] First Login & Dashboard Tour
**[VISUAL]** Login process → Dashboard walkthrough
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Access 4SCH from the mobile app or web browser. Your admin will provide login credentials - typically your email address and a temporary password. Change this password immediately after first login.

Your dashboard shows today's classes, pending assignments to grade, recent announcements, and quick stats. The 'My Classes' section lists all classes and subjects assigned to you. Today's Schedule shows your timetable. Tap any class to mark attendance, view students, or post assignments."

### [02:00-03:30] Understanding Your Class List
**[VISUAL]** Class list with student details
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Tap a class to see your student roster. Each student card shows their photo, name, roll number, and attendance percentage. Tap any student for detailed information - contact details, academic performance, attendance history, and parent information.

Use the search bar to quickly find students by name or roll number. Filter by gender or attendance status if needed. The class strength counter shows total enrolled vs present today."

### [03:30-05:00] Mobile App vs Web Portal
**[VISUAL]** Side-by-side comparison
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"The mobile app is perfect for daily tasks - attendance, viewing schedules, and quick messaging. Download from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

The web portal offers advanced features like detailed grading, report generation, and bulk operations. Access it from any browser at your school's URL.

Both sync instantly - mark attendance on your phone during class, then enter detailed grades from your laptop at home. Your choice of device for each task."

## CHAPTER 2: Attendance Management (4 min)

### [05:00-06:30] Marking Daily Attendance
**[VISUAL]** Attendance marking workflow on mobile
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Marking attendance takes just seconds. From your dashboard, tap today's class. Click 'Mark Attendance'.

You'll see your complete student list. Tap each present student - they turn green. Absent students remain unmarked or tap to mark absent explicitly. Use 'Mark All Present' for full attendance, then unmark absent students.

Add remarks for specific students if needed - Late, Sick Leave, On Duty. Click Submit. Parents receive instant notifications about their child's absence. The system timestamps your submission."

### [06:30-07:30] Viewing Attendance History
**[VISUAL]** Attendance reports and trends
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"View attendance history from the Class menu → Attendance Report. Filter by date range to see patterns. The system highlights students with low attendance - those below 75% appear in red.

Generate attendance sheets for your records or parent-teacher meetings. Export as PDF or Excel. Monthly attendance summaries show class averages and trends - useful for identifying students who need intervention."

### [07:30-09:00] Handling Late Arrivals & Corrections
**[VISUAL]** Editing attendance
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If a student arrives late after you've marked attendance, no problem. Go to Attendance → View Today's Attendance. Find the student and tap Edit. Change from Absent to Present or Late, add a remark, and save.

Administrators set permissions for editing past attendance. Typically, teachers can edit today's and yesterday's records. For older corrections, contact your admin who can make changes with proper documentation.

Pro tip: Some schools use the 'Late' status separately from Present to track punctuality without affecting attendance percentages."

---

## CHAPTER 3: Assignment & Grading (6 min)

### [09:00-11:00] Creating Assignments
**[VISUAL]** Assignment creation form
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Assign homework from the Assignments menu → Create Assignment. Enter the assignment title - 'Chapter 5 Exercise' or 'Essay on Climate Change'.

Write clear instructions in the description field. Set the subject and class. Choose a due date - students see countdown timers.

Attach reference materials - PDFs, images, or links to educational videos. Set maximum points for grading - 10, 20, or 100, your choice.

Decide if you want file submissions, text submissions, or both. Enable late submissions if you allow them, optionally with penalty points. Click Publish.

Students receive instant notifications and see the assignment in their app with all details and attachments."

### [11:00-13:00] Reviewing Student Submissions
**[VISUAL]** Submissions review interface
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"View submissions from Assignments → My Assignments → Select Assignment. You'll see submission status: Submitted, Pending, or Late.

Click any submission to review. Students can upload images of handwritten work, PDFs, or type text responses. View attachments directly in the app.

Grade each submission by entering points earned out of maximum points. Add feedback comments - praise good work or guide improvements. Mark as 'Needs Revision' if you want resubmission.

Click Save & Next to move to the next student seamlessly. When done, click Publish Grades. Students receive notifications with their scores and your feedback instantly."

### [13:00-15:00] Entering Exam Grades
**[VISUAL]** Grade entry for exams
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"For formal exams, your admin sets up exam structures with subjects and maximum marks. Access from Exams → Grade Entry.

Select the exam, class, and subject. You'll see a grid with student names and mark fields. Enter marks for each student - the system validates against maximum marks set.

Add remarks for individual students if needed: 'Excellent progress', 'Needs improvement in algebra', etc. Use bulk actions to mark absent students if they missed the exam.

Save frequently to avoid data loss. Once complete, click Submit for Verification. Admins review and publish results. Students and parents can then view detailed mark sheets and rankings."

## CHAPTER 4: Communication & Best Practices (5 min)

### [15:00-16:30] Messaging Parents
**[VISUAL]** Messaging interface
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Communicate with parents from the Messages menu. Click New Message, select recipients - individual parents, entire class parents, or specific students.

Compose your message about homework reminders, performance updates, or event notifications. Attach files if needed - study materials or consent forms.

Messages deliver as push notifications to parent apps and email. Parents can reply directly, creating a conversation thread. You receive notifications of parent replies.

Use announcement mode for one-way broadcasts like exam schedules or holiday notices. Regular messages allow two-way communication for discussions about student progress."

### [16:30-18:00] Teacher Productivity Tips
**[VISUAL]** Tips overlay with demonstrations
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Maximize efficiency with these tips. Mark attendance during the first five minutes of class while students settle - saves time and ensures accuracy.

Create assignment templates for recurring homework types - save commonly used instructions and reuse them.

Use the gradebook's sort and filter features to identify struggling students early. Address issues before they become major problems.

Schedule weekly time to review analytics - which topics have low test scores? Adjust your teaching accordingly.

Enable mobile notifications for parent messages so you respond promptly, building trust with families."

### [18:00-19:30] Classroom Management Features
**[VISUAL]** Additional features showcase
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Beyond basics, 4SCH helps with classroom management. Post class announcements about upcoming tests, project deadlines, or syllabus changes.

Share study materials - upload notes, presentations, or video links that students access anytime. Organize by topics for easy reference.

View student profiles for holistic understanding - academic history, attendance patterns, previous teacher remarks. This context helps you support each student better.

Track your own statistics - classes taught, assignments created, average grading time. Reflect on your efficiency and areas for improvement."

### [19:30-20:00] OUTRO
**[VISUAL]** Resources and next steps
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"You're now equipped to use 4SCH effectively! Access detailed help from the app's Help section, watch feature-specific tutorials, or email support@4sch.com with questions. Happy teaching!"
