# Video Script: School Driver's Complete Guide

**Duration:** 12-15 minutes (3 chapters)  
**Target Audience:** School Drivers/Transportation Staff  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Manage daily trips and routes
- Mark student attendance during pickups/dropoffs
- Communicate with parents about delays and issues
- Track vehicle maintenance and safety records

---

## CHAPTER 1: Trip Management & Dashboard (4-5 min)

### [00:00-00:20] INTRO
**[VISUAL]** Driver dashboard with trip information
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Welcome, school drivers! 4SCH helps you manage daily trips efficiently, ensure student safety, and communicate transparently with parents. This guide covers everything you need for smooth daily operations - from trip schedules to attendance marking and emergency communications."

### [00:20-01:45] First Login & Driver Dashboard
**[VISUAL]** Login process → Dashboard walkthrough
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Download the 4SCH Driver App from Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Your school admin provides login credentials. Launch the app, enter credentials, and you're ready to go.

Your dashboard shows today's trips at a glance: Morning pickup route with all assigned stops, afternoon dropoff route, and any special trips scheduled. Each trip shows assigned vehicle, expected start time, and student count.

Quick stats display: Students on your route today, completed pickups, pending dropoffs, and any alerts - student absence, special passenger, or parent notes.

The dashboard updates in real-time as you complete each stop. This gives your school visibility into transportation operations and helps coordinate with office staff."

### [01:45-03:15] Understanding Trip Routes & Schedules
**[VISUAL]** Route details and stop information
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Your assigned routes appear in the Trips menu. Tap any trip to see complete details: pickup and dropoff stops in order, arrival times for each stop, and students assigned to each stop.

Each stop shows location address, estimated arrival time, and students waiting there - by name and photo for quick identification.

The app provides GPS-guided navigation to each stop. Follow the map route or use your GPS device for directions - the app integrates with Google Maps for real-time traffic updates and optimal routing.

If traffic delays you, the app tracks actual arrival times and automatically notifies parents that their child's pickup will be delayed. Transparency reduces parent anxiety."

### [03:15-04:30] Checking Special Instructions & Alerts
**[VISUAL]** Special instructions and passenger alerts
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Before starting your route, review all alerts and special instructions in the Trip Details section. Are any students absent today? The app shows if a student is sick or not coming.

Special instructions appear for specific students: 'John's mother will pick him instead today', 'Sarah needs to go to clinic after school', 'Ahmed has swimming class - dropoff at sports complex'.

Safety alerts flag important information: Medical conditions, allergies, behavioral notes, or emergency contacts. Read these carefully - they ensure you handle each student appropriately.

Parent notes might include: 'Please ensure my child drinks water', 'Call me if running late', or 'Use alternative route due to traffic'. These personalized requests help you provide better service."

## CHAPTER 2: Student Attendance & Pickup/Dropoff (4-5 min)

### [04:30-06:15] Marking Student Attendance at Pickups
**[VISUAL]** Attendance marking workflow during trips
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"At each pickup stop, mark which students you've picked up. Open the app and navigate to the current trip. The app shows a checklist of students assigned to this stop.

As each student boards, tap their name - they turn green showing they're picked up. If a student doesn't show up after waiting a set time, mark them absent and call their parent immediately - they may have overslept or forgotten school today.

Add remarks if needed: 'Student arrived late', 'Parent requested early pickup', 'Student is sick but came anyway', etc. These remarks help the school understand attendance patterns.

Once all students at this stop are accounted for, tap 'Complete Stop'. The app calculates time spent at the stop and moves to the next location. This real-time tracking keeps the school informed of your progress throughout the route."

### [06:15-07:45] Handling Unexpected Situations
**[VISUAL]** Alert and communication features
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Unexpected situations happen: Traffic jam makes you 30 minutes late, a student is ill and needs to go to the hospital, or bad weather requires route changes.

When facing delays, use the app's 'Report Delay' feature. Enter the delay reason and estimated new arrival time. Parents of affected students receive instant notifications: 'Your child's dropoff will be delayed by 30 minutes due to heavy traffic.'

For health emergencies, immediately contact the school through the app and call parents directly. The app's emergency contact feature shows all relevant phone numbers - school, parents, emergency contacts.

For safety concerns - if you need to deviate from route due to accident or hazard - use the app to notify the school immediately. Document what happened for records."

### [07:45-09:30] Dropoff Procedures & Parent Handoffs
**[VISUAL]** Dropoff workflow and signature/confirmation
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"At each dropoff stop, use the same attendance marking process. Tap students as they get off the vehicle. If a student is not picked up by parent or authorized person, do not let them off - call school immediately for instructions.

The app prompts you to confirm the authorized person picking up each student. Match the person to authorized list - if someone unexpected comes to pick up a student, verify with the school before releasing them.

Some schools require photo/signature confirmation for high-security dropoffs. Take a quick photo of the person picking up the student or note their ID. This protects the child and documents who they were released to.

Once all students are dropped off at a stop, mark the stop complete. By end of day, all students have been successfully delivered home. This accountability protects students and documents your responsibility."

### [09:30-10:30] Communication During Trips
**[VISUAL]** In-app messaging with parents and school
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"The Messaging feature lets you communicate with school and parents during routes. If a parent messages asking about pickup time, you can respond directly: 'Currently at stop 3, will reach your location in 10 minutes.'

For important updates, use broadcast messages to all affected parents: 'Due to heavy traffic, all dropoffs will be 20 minutes late. Students are safe.'

The school office can also message you with updates: 'Student John is absent today, don't pick him up', or 'Please proceed directly to clinic with student Sarah after school.'

All messages are logged with timestamps. This creates transparency and protects everyone - if disputes arise, you have documentation of all communications."

## CHAPTER 3: Daily Records & Safety Best Practices (3-4 min)

### [10:30-11:45] Completing Daily Trip Reports
**[VISUAL]** Trip completion and reporting interface
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"At the end of each trip, complete the Trip Report. The app summarizes your route: students picked up, attendance status, any incidents, and time taken.

Review the summary for accuracy. Did you pick up all assigned students? Are there discrepancies that need noting? Any damage to vehicle or safety concerns?

Add end-of-trip notes: Vehicle condition, fuel level, maintenance needs noticed, or behavioral issues with students. These records help the school track vehicle health and plan maintenance.

Submit the report. It goes to the transportation manager and school office. They can review your performance, address any issues, and plan next day operations."

### [11:45-13:00] Safety & Best Practices
**[VISUAL]** Safety tips overlay with demonstrations
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Safety is paramount. Always follow traffic rules - speed limits, red lights, and safe driving practices. Your passengers are school's most valuable assets.

Maintain vehicle cleanliness and safety: no sharp objects lying around, working seatbelts, good ventilation, and emergency supplies in the vehicle.

Know every student's name and parents' contact details. Use the app to review this before routes so you recognize students and can contact parents quickly if needed.

Never deviate from approved routes without school permission. If you take a different route, inform the school before doing so.

Keep the vehicle maintained. Report any mechanical issues immediately - faulty brakes, warning lights, or unusual sounds. A well-maintained vehicle ensures student safety.

Be professional and courteous with students and parents. You represent the school. Positive interactions build trust and make daily operations smooth."

### [13:00-13:30] Vehicle Maintenance & Fuel Tracking
**[VISUAL]** Maintenance log and fuel tracking
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Track vehicle maintenance from the Vehicle section. Log all maintenance performed - oil changes, tire rotations, repairs, or inspections. Include dates and costs.

The system reminds you of upcoming maintenance based on mileage or time intervals. This prevents breakdowns during student routes.

Log fuel purchases - amount, cost, and mileage. This helps calculate fuel efficiency and budget for fuel expenses. Any unusual fuel consumption can indicate vehicle problems.

Document any vehicle damage - scratches, dents, or broken components. Photograph damage if significant. This protects you by documenting pre-existing conditions versus new damage."

### [13:30-15:00] Tips for Efficiency & Parent Relations
**[VISUAL]** Tips and best practices
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Start and finish routes on time consistently. Parents plan their day around pickup/dropoff times. Reliability builds trust.

Use the app's arrival time estimates when communicating with parents. If you're running late, alert them immediately rather than keeping them guessing.

Treat students with kindness and respect. They feel safe when drivers are friendly and patient. Happy students mean happy parents and fewer complaints.

Learn students' names and preferences. Some like quiet, others are chatty. Adapt to their comfort - this small effort builds better relationships.

Keep the vehicle neat and comfortable. Clean interior, good air conditioning, and safe seating make the ride pleasant for students.

Communicate any issues early - if a student is disruptive, has health issues, or has family problems affecting behavior, inform the school so they can support the student.

Document everything through the app. Good records protect you and help the school improve transportation services."

### [15:00-15:00] OUTRO
**[VISUAL]** Support resources and safe driving message
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"You're now equipped to manage daily routes safely and efficiently with 4SCH. Remember, you're trusted with our school's most precious cargo - students' safety is your top priority. For help with the app, contact your school admin or email support@4sch.com. Drive safely!"
