# Video Script: School Accountant's Complete Guide

**Duration:** 15-18 minutes (4 chapters)  
**Target Audience:** School Accountants/Finance Staff  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Manage school financial dashboard
- Configure and manage fee structures
- Process and track payments
- Generate financial reports and analytics

---

**[VISUAL]** Login process → Dashboard walkthrough

**[VISUAL]** Year/session selector interface

You can run reports for specific years or compare multiple years to identify trends - is collection improving? Are certain grades defaulting more? This historical analysis guides financial strategy."

## CHAPTER 2: Fee Management (4-5 min)

### [04:30-06:00] Creating & Managing Fee Structures
**[VISUAL]** Fee structure configuration interface
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Set up fee structures from Fees → Manage Fees → Fee Categories. Create distinct categories for different fee types your school charges: Tuition, Exam Fees, Library Fees, Sports Fees, Transport Fees, Activity Fees - whatever applies to your school.

For each category, enter the amount in local currency. Decide if the fee applies to all grades or specific grades - for example, transport might differ by location.

Specify payment terms: Is this a single yearly fee or divided into installments? Set installment schedule with due dates. January payment, March payment, July payment - whatever your school's calendar dictates.

Make fees optional or mandatory. Optional fees appear separately so parents know they can skip them. Mandatory fees are standard expectations.

Save the fee structure. Once saved, you can view or edit at any time. Changes apply to new enrollments; existing students keep their original fee structure unless you specifically reassign."

### [06:00-07:30] Assigning Fees to Students
**[VISUAL]** Bulk fee assignment workflow
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Assign fees to students from Fees → Student Fee Assignment. Select a grade and class - all students in that class appear.

Choose which fee categories apply - typically all fees for that grade apply to all students, but you can customize. Select the fee structure just created. Click 'Assign Fees to Selected Students'.

The system creates individual fee accounts for each student based on assigned categories and installment schedule. Each student now has a personalized fee schedule showing what they owe and when.

You can assign different fees to different grades. Maybe Grade 11 students pay higher exam fees due to WAEC/JAMB exams. Assign higher exam fees to Grade 11, standard fees to other grades.

For scholarship students or financial aid recipients, you can override fees - reduce or waive specific categories. This flexibility accommodates various school policies."

### [07:30-09:15] Managing Fee Installments & Adjustments
**[VISUAL]** Fee adjustment and installment management
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"From Fees → Fee Adjustments, you can modify student fees. Select a student and view their complete fee breakdown.

Add extra charges if needed - additional exam fees, late penalties, or special program costs. The system recalculates totals immediately.

Grant discounts or waivers - reduce fees by percentage (15% scholarship) or amount (₦50,000 full scholarship). Reason codes help track why discounts were given - scholarships, financial hardship, employee benefits, etc.

For students leaving school mid-year, prorate their fees. A student joining in May pays prorated amounts for remaining months instead of full annual fees.

All adjustments create audit trails - what was changed, by whom, when, and why. This transparency is essential for financial accountability and parent disputes resolution."

### [09:15-10:30] Fee Payment Plans & Reminders
**[VISUAL]** Payment plan configuration and reminder settings
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Configure payment reminders from Settings → Payment Reminders. Set automatic reminders to send to parents at specific intervals: 7 days before due date, on due date, 3 days after due date - whatever frequency your school prefers.

Customize reminder messages: 'Tuition fees are due on March 15. Pay now to avoid penalties.' Include school contact information and payment instructions.

You can send reminders via in-app notifications, email, or SMS (if SMS gateway is configured). Parents receive multiple notifications creating urgency and reducing overdue balances.

Set up escalation reminders for severely overdue accounts - 30 days overdue triggers a stricter message. This automated system reduces manual follow-up work."

## CHAPTER 3: Payment Processing & Tracking (4-5 min)

### [10:30-12:00] Online Payment Processing
**[VISUAL]** Payment gateway integration and processing
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Parents pay fees through multiple channels configured in your system. Online payment through the parent app links to payment gateways - Paystack, Flutterwave, or Remita depending on your school's setup.

Parents select 'Pay Now' in the app, choose which fee to pay, and select payment method: debit card, bank transfer, USSD, or mobile money. Payment gateways handle security with PCI-DSS compliance.

The payment processes instantly. Parents receive confirmation with transaction ID and receipt. Your 4SCH system simultaneously receives payment notification, records the transaction, and updates student fee balance automatically.

This real-time reconciliation eliminates manual data entry, reduces errors, and ensures transparency. Parents see their updated balance immediately - no delays or discrepancies."

### [12:00-13:30] Processing Manual/Bank Payments
**[VISUAL]** Manual payment entry and bank reconciliation
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Some parents prefer paying via bank transfer or cash. Record these payments manually from Payments → Add Payment.

Enter student name or ID, amount paid, date, and payment method. You can add payment reference or transaction number for tracking.

For bank transfers, match incoming deposits to payments. Download bank statements, then match deposits to student accounts. The system shows matched vs unmatched transactions.

For cash payments, verify the source. Did a parent bring cash? Record the receipt immediately - date, amount, who received it, and from whom. Never delay cash receipts - record immediately for transparency and security.

All manual entries appear in reports as clearly as online payments. Maintain proper documentation for audits."

### [13:30-15:00] Payment Tracking & Exception Handling
**[VISUAL]** Payment status views and exception handling
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"From Payments → View Payments, see all transactions - date, student, amount, method, and status. Filter by student, date range, payment method, or status.

Some payments may be Pending - awaiting bank confirmation. These auto-resolve when banks process transfers. Completed payments show a green checkmark.

Failed payments appear in red - these need investigation. Common causes: insufficient funds, card declined, or technical issues. Contact parents to reprocess with correct information.

You can generate payment receipts anytime. Parents requesting receipts for their records - generate from the Payments section. These official receipts show student name, amount, date, and purpose.

For disputes - parent claims they paid but the system shows outstanding - investigate transaction history. Look at bank records, payment gateway confirmations, and payment logs. Document findings for resolution."

### [15:00-15:45] Handling Defaulters & Collection Strategies
**[VISUAL]** Defaulter list and collection management
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"From Reports → Outstanding Fees, see students with overdue balances. The system flags accounts 30+ days overdue as critical.

For serious defaulters, some schools require proof of payment arrangement before allowing student continuation. You can tag such accounts for follow-up.

Generate dunning notices - formal demand letters requesting payment. Include student name, fee details, amount owed, and due date. Print or email these to parents.

Create collection action plans: Contact defaulters on specific dates, escalate to school leadership if unresponsive, explore installment arrangements, or consider account suspension.

Document all collection attempts - calls made, emails sent, promises given, payments received. This protects the school in disputes and tracks collection effectiveness over time."

## CHAPTER 4: Reports & Financial Analysis (3-4 min)

### [15:45-17:00] Generating Financial Reports
**[VISUAL]** Reports generation interface and output samples
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"4SCH's Reports module provides comprehensive financial analytics. From Reports → Collection Report, see overall collection status: Target vs Actual collected, percentage achievement, and trends over time.

Fee-wise breakdown shows which categories have highest collection - is tuition collected well but sports fees lagging? This identifies problem areas.

Grade-wise breakdown shows which classes have poorest collection. Use this to target communication - maybe Grade 12 has only 60% collection while Grade 9 has 95%.

Student-wise report lists every student with fee breakdown, amount paid, outstanding, and payment status. Export as Excel for further analysis or to share with school leadership.

Schedule reports to generate automatically - weekly, monthly, or termly. Receive reports via email so you always have current information without manual generation."

### [17:00-17:45] Payment Method Analytics
**[VISUAL]** Payment method breakdown and trend analysis
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Understand how parents prefer paying from the Payment Method Analytics. What percentage use online payment vs bank transfer vs cash? This guides communication strategy.

If most parents use online payment, ensure the payment gateway is always available. If many prefer bank transfer, ensure bank details are visible and correct.

Track payment method trends over time. If cash collections are declining but online increasing, plan accordingly. Some schools phase out cash collection due to operational burden.

Identify seasonal patterns. Do more parents pay just before deadline? Does collection spike on specific dates? Use this intelligence to optimize reminder timing."

### [17:45-18:00] Dashboard Alerts & Notifications
**[VISUAL]** Alert system and notification settings
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Configure alerts for critical financial events. Alert when a large payment is received, when an overdue account reaches 60 days past due, or when daily collection exceeds target.

These alerts keep you informed of important developments without constantly monitoring the dashboard. Respond quickly to issues - investigate failed payments, follow up on defaulters.

Dashboard shows top defaulters prominently - the few accounts with largest outstanding balances. Focus on these high-value accounts for maximum collection impact.

You can customize which alerts you receive based on role - head accountant gets all alerts, junior staff gets subset. Proper role configuration ensures right people get right alerts."

**[VISUAL]** Support resources and best practices
**[VOICEOVER]:**

For detailed help, access the in-app Help section or contact support@4sch.com with technical questions. Happy accounting!"
