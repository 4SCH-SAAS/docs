# Video Script: Student's Complete Guide

**Duration:** 10-12 minutes (3 chapters)  
**Target Audience:** Students  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Navigate the student dashboard effectively
- Access timetable and manage schedules
- Submit assignments and view grades
- Use communication features with teachers

---

## CHAPTER 1: Dashboard & Timetable (3-4 min)

### [00:00-00:20] INTRO
**[VISUAL]** Student dashboard on mobile and web
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Welcome, students! Welcome to 4SCH - your complete academic companion. This guide shows you everything you need to succeed: access your timetable, submit assignments, check grades, and communicate with teachers. Let's dive in!"

### [00:20-01:30] First Login & Dashboard Overview
**[VISUAL]** Login process → Dashboard walkthrough
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Start by downloading the 4SCH Student App from Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Your school will provide login credentials - usually your email or student ID and a temporary password. Change your password immediately after first login.

Your dashboard is your command center. At the top, your profile showing name, class, roll number, and average grade. Below that are quick stat cards: Today's attendance percentage, upcoming assignments, and important announcements from your school.

The dashboard gives you everything at a glance - what you need to focus on today, what's coming up, and critical information from your teachers and school administration."

### [01:30-03:00] Understanding Your Timetable
**[VISUAL]** Timetable interface showing weekly schedule
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Your timetable is in the Timetable menu. See your complete weekly schedule with all subjects, teachers, timings, and sometimes classroom locations.

Tap any class to see the teacher's name, subject details, and room number. Set personal reminders - the app can alert you 5 minutes or 15 minutes before each class starts. This helps you prepare and never miss important lectures.

You can switch between weekly and daily views depending on what you prefer. Color-coded subjects make it easy to spot subjects at a glance. If there's a schedule change or special class, you'll get a notification immediately."

### [03:00-04:00] Mobile App vs Web Portal
**[VISUAL]** Side-by-side comparison of mobile and web
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"The mobile app is your go-to for quick access during the day - check timetable, view assignments, and submit work on the go.

The web portal on a computer offers a fuller view for detailed tasks like reviewing feedback on assignments, comparing grades across exams, or downloading study materials.

Both sync instantly - submit an assignment on your phone, and your teacher sees it immediately. Check grades on the web, and the mobile app reflects the update right away. Use whichever device makes sense for each task."

---

## CHAPTER 2: Assignments & Grades (4-5 min)

### [04:00-05:30] Viewing & Submitting Assignments
**[VISUAL]** Assignment list and submission workflow
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Find all your assignments in the Assignments menu. You'll see three tabs: Pending - homework due soon, Submitted - work you've turned in, and Graded - assignments with scores and feedback.

For each assignment, you see the title, description, due date with countdown timer, and any attachments from your teacher - PDFs, images, or video links. Download and review these materials to understand what's expected.

To submit, tap the assignment and click 'Submit Work'. You can upload images of handwritten work, type text responses, attach files, or a combination. Take clear photos of handwritten work - good lighting and centered images help teachers grade accurately.

Click Submit. Your teacher gets a notification immediately. You can resubmit before the deadline if you want to improve your work - your teacher grades the latest version."

### [05:30-07:00] Late Submissions & Revision Requests
**[VISUAL]** Late submission status and feedback interface
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Miss the deadline? Some teachers allow late submissions - usually with reduced marks. Check the assignment details to see if late submissions are enabled and what penalty applies.

After your teacher grades your work, they often add feedback comments. Read these carefully - they're personalized guidance on what you did well and where to improve. Some teachers may request a revision, asking you to resubmit with improvements.

If revisions are requested, you have a set timeframe to resubmit. This is an opportunity to learn and improve your grade. Take it seriously - it shows teachers you're committed to learning."

### [07:00-09:00] Understanding Your Grades & Performance
**[VISUAL]** Grades interface and analytics
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"View all your grades in the Grades menu. See assignments, quizzes, and formal exam results with scores, feedback, and dates.

Each grade shows points earned out of maximum - for example, '18 out of 20' with a percentage. Teacher feedback comments appear below the score. Some teachers include encouraging remarks or specific improvement suggestions.

Your dashboard shows your average grades by subject. Track your performance over time - are you improving in Math? Is English your strong suit? Understanding your strengths and weaknesses helps you plan study time effectively.

Some schools publish class averages and rankings. You can compare your performance anonymously - see where you stand relative to class standards. This motivates you to put in the work needed."

### [09:00-10:00] Formal Exams & Report Cards
**[VISUAL]** Exam results and report card
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"For formal exams, results appear in the Exams menu once your teachers complete grading and the school publishes them. You'll see your complete mark sheet - all subjects, marks obtained, maximums, and percentages.

Review how you performed in each subject. If results disappoint in specific areas, reach out to your teacher for extra support or tutoring. Many teachers offer revision sessions or extra resources.

Your school may share class rank - this shows where you stand academically. Use this information constructively - celebrate improvements and identify areas for focused effort next term."

## CHAPTER 3: Communication & Tips (3-4 min)

### [10:00-11:15] Messaging Your Teachers
**[VISUAL]** Messaging interface
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Need clarification on homework? Have a question about an assignment? Message your teacher from the Messages menu. Click 'New Message' and select your teacher or class.

Write your question clearly and professionally. For example: 'Hi Sir/Ma, I'm confused about the quadratic formula application in question 3 of today's homework. Could you clarify?' Include specific details so teachers understand exactly what you need help with.

Teachers get notifications of your message and can reply during their available time. You'll see their response instantly. Keep messages respectful and on-topic - it builds a better relationship with your teachers.

You can also receive important announcements from your class teacher or school in this section - exam schedules, project deadlines, or special instructions."

### [11:15-12:00] Student Success Tips
**[VISUAL]** Tips overlay with demonstrations
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Set reminders for all assignment deadlines - don't miss submissions due to forgetfulness. Start assignments early, not the night before.

Review teacher feedback on submitted work - this is where you learn what to improve. Don't ignore comments or corrections.

Check your timetable every morning - it prevents showing up late or missing classes. Enable notifications for schedule changes.

Use the Grades section to identify where you need help. Are you scoring low in specific subjects? Seek extra tutoring or teacher support early.

Download study materials your teachers share. They post notes, presentations, and reference materials to help you prepare for exams and understand concepts better.

Communicate professionally with teachers. Clear, respectful questions show maturity and get better responses than casual messages."

### [12:00-12:00] OUTRO
**[VISUAL]** Support resources and next steps
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"You're now ready to make the most of 4SCH! Stay organized, submit on time, engage with your teachers, and watch your academic performance improve. For help, check the app's Help section or ask your school administrator. Good luck with your studies!"
