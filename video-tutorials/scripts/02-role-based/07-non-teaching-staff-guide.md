# Video Script: Non-Teaching Staff Complete Guide

**Duration:** 10-12 minutes (3 chapters)  
**Target Audience:** Non-Teaching Staff (Office, Support, Maintenance)  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Navigate the staff dashboard
- Perform role-specific tasks and workflows
- Collaborate with other staff members
- Support overall school operations

### [01:45-03:30] Understanding Your Role & Permissions
**[VISUAL]** Role-based feature access and permissions
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Your role determines which features you can access. Administrative staff can view student records, manage enrollment, and process applications. They cannot grade assignments or enter exam marks - those are teachers' responsibilities.

Logistics staff manage supplies, inventory, and requisitions. They can create purchase orders and track deliveries but cannot access student academic information.

Maintenance staff manage facility requests, track repairs, and log maintenance activities. They have access to facilities management but not student or financial systems.

Support staff might assist with customer service, help parent inquiries, or manage communications. Their permissions are customized to support functions.

This role-based access ensures everyone has the tools they need while maintaining security and data privacy. If you need access to features not available, request from your administrator."

## CHAPTER 2: Role-Specific Tasks & Workflows (3-4 min)

### [04:30-06:00] Administrative Staff Tasks
**[VISUAL]** Admin task workflows - enrollment, records, approvals
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Administrative staff handle student enrollment and record management. From the Students menu, view all enrolled students with complete profiles - name, class, parent contact information, and academic history.

Process new enrollment applications: Review submitted forms, verify documents, create student accounts, and generate login credentials for parents and students. The system tracks application status - submitted, approved, or pending.

Maintain student information: Update contact details, address changes, or parent information. Use bulk operations to update multiple records at once - change class assignments when students are promoted, update contact data from forms.

Print certificates, transcripts, and official documents. The system generates these automatically from stored data. Review before printing to ensure accuracy.

Generate administrative reports: Enrollment by grade, student demographics, contact information lists for communication. Use these for planning and communication."

### [06:00-07:30] Logistics & Supply Management
**[VISUAL]** Supply order and inventory management
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Logistics staff manage school supplies and inventory. From the Supplies menu, view current inventory levels - how much chalk, paper, cleaning supplies, or stationery the school has in stock.

Create purchase requisitions when inventory runs low. Specify what's needed, quantity, and urgency. Submit for approval by your supervisor.

Once approved, create purchase orders to send to vendors. Track order status: Ordered, Shipped, Delivered, or Received. Update inventory when deliveries arrive.

Maintain vendor information - contact details, pricing, and past orders. This helps you make informed purchasing decisions and manage vendor relationships.

Generate inventory reports showing what's stocked, what's running low, and spending by category. Use this data for budgeting and planning purchases."

### [07:30-09:00] Maintenance & Facility Management
**[VISUAL]** Maintenance requests and facility tracking
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Maintenance staff manage facility requests and repairs. Staff throughout the school submit facility requests - broken chairs, leaking taps, faulty lights, or HVAC issues.

From the Maintenance menu, see all pending requests with details: location, issue description, when reported, and priority level. Assess each request and assign to yourself or a team member.

Update request status as work progresses: Acknowledged, In Progress, Completed. Add notes about what was done: 'Replaced broken chair', 'Repaired leaky faucet in Lab 2', 'Serviced air conditioner'.

Track parts used and labor hours for budgeting purposes. Schedule preventive maintenance - regular inspections and servicing to prevent breakdowns.

Generate maintenance reports showing request volume, resolution time, and areas needing attention. These reports help plan maintenance budgets and identify facility problems."

### [09:00-10:30] Collaboration & Communication
**[VISUAL]** Internal messaging and task assignment
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"The Collaboration menu enables communication with colleagues. Use internal messaging to coordinate with other staff members - 'Can you handle the Monday shipment delivery?', 'The lab equipment needs urgently repair', 'We're out of paper - please order more'.

Assign tasks to team members. A supervisor can assign maintenance requests to staff, or an admin can delegate document preparation. Assigned staff receive notifications and can update task status.

Share documents and files through the system. Upload lesson schedules, training materials, or reference documents that colleagues need. Organize by folder for easy access.

View team calendars showing who's working, leave schedules, and important deadlines. Coordinate work effectively to ensure all tasks are covered."

## CHAPTER 3: Best Practices & Collaboration (3-4 min)

### [10:30-11:15] Productivity & Efficiency Tips
**[VISUAL]** Tips overlay with workflow demonstrations
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Prioritize your daily tasks. Review your task list first thing in the morning - what's urgent? What's deadline-driven? What can wait? Handle urgent items first.

Use bulk operations to save time. Processing multiple documents? Batch operations update many records at once instead of individually.

Automate routine tasks if possible. Set up automatic purchase orders for regularly needed supplies. Use templates for recurring documents. These small efficiencies add up.

Communicate clearly and frequently with colleagues. If a task is delayed or problematic, inform relevant people early so they can adjust plans.

Keep records organized. File documents properly, name files descriptively, and maintain version control. This helps you find things quickly and prevents duplicate work.

Update the system in real-time. Don't delay logging information. Real-time data helps the whole school operate efficiently."

### [11:15-12:00] Collaboration Best Practices & Support
**[VISUAL]** Teamwork scenarios and problem resolution
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Collaboration makes the school function smoothly. If a teacher needs student records, provide them promptly. If maintenance needs supply budget information, gather it quickly. Support each other's work.

Document your work in the system. When you complete a task, mark it done and add notes. Your documentation helps others understand what was done and prevents duplicate effort.

Escalate issues appropriately. If a student enrollment issue is unclear, don't guess - contact your supervisor for guidance. If a maintenance problem is beyond your capability, call a professional.

Maintain confidentiality of student and staff information. You have access to sensitive data - grades, contact information, health data. Protect this information and only share with authorized people.

Provide feedback and suggestions for improvement. If a process is inefficient or the system could work better, tell your administrator. Continuous improvement benefits everyone.

For technical issues, contact your school's IT support or email support@4sch.com. Be specific about the problem - what were you trying to do? What error did you get? Clear descriptions help support teams solve issues faster.

Professional development opportunities: Ask about training on advanced features. As you become comfortable with basics, learning advanced functions increases your value and productivity."

Your role is essential - administrative staff keep records accurate, logistics staff ensure supplies available, maintenance staff keep facilities functioning. Together, you create an environment where teachers can teach and students can learn. For help, check in-app resources or contact your school administrator. Thank you for your dedicated service!"
