# Video Script: Parent's Complete Guide

**Duration:** 13 minutes (3 chapters)  
**Target Audience:** Parents/Guardians  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Monitor child's attendance and academic progress
- Pay school fees online
- Communicate with teachers
- Manage multiple children accounts

---

## CHAPTER 1: Getting Started (4 min)

### [00:00-00:20] INTRO
**[VISUAL]** Parent app interface
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Welcome, parents! Stay connected to your child's education with the 4SCH parent app. Monitor attendance, view grades, pay fees online, and message teachers - all from your smartphone. Let's explore!"

### [00:20-01:30] Installing & First Login
**[VISUAL]** App store download → Login process
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Download the 4SCH Parent App from Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Search '4SCH Parent'.

Your school provides login credentials when your child enrolls - typically your email address and a password. Launch the app, select your school from the list, and enter credentials.

On first login, you're prompted to change your password. Choose a strong password combining letters, numbers, and symbols. Enable biometric login - fingerprint or face unlock - for convenient access."

### [01:30-03:00] Dashboard Overview
**[VISUAL]** Dashboard tour with feature highlights
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Your dashboard shows everything at a glance. At the top, your child's profile with photo, name, class, and section.

The Attendance Card shows current month's attendance - total days, present, absent, and percentage. Tap for detailed history.

Fee Status Card displays total fees, amount paid, and pending balance with a quick 'Pay Now' button.

Recent Assignments shows upcoming and submitted homework. Latest Announcements from school and teachers appear in the feed.

Quick action buttons give one-tap access to timetable, exam results, messages, and profile."

### [03:00-04:00] Managing Multiple Children
**[VISUAL]** Child switcher interface
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If you have multiple children in the school, all appear in one account. Tap the profile icon at top-left to see your children list. Select any child to view their information.

Each child has their own dashboard, attendance, fees, and messages. Switch between children anytime. Notifications show which child they're about - 'John was absent today', 'Sarah submitted assignment'.

The app remembers your last viewed child for convenience."

---

## CHAPTER 2: Monitoring Academic Progress (5 min)

### [04:00-05:30] Checking Attendance
**[VISUAL]** Attendance screens and calendar view
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Monitor attendance from the Attendance menu. Calendar view shows color-coded days - green for present, red for absent, orange for late.

Tap any date to see detailed information: time marked, subject-wise attendance if applicable, and teacher remarks like 'Sick Leave' or 'School Event'.

Monthly and weekly summaries show trends. If attendance drops below 75%, the app alerts you to take action - contact the school or address issues with your child.

Enable push notifications to receive instant alerts when your child is marked absent. Some schools also send SMS alerts."

### [05:30-07:00] Viewing Grades & Results
**[VISUAL]** Grades and report cards
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"View assignments and exam results from the Academics menu. The Assignments section shows all homework - upcoming, submitted, and graded.

Tap any assignment to see: the task description, attachments from teacher, due date, your child's submission with upload time, and grade received with teacher feedback.

For formal exams, go to Exam Results. Select the exam to view the complete mark sheet - all subjects, marks obtained, maximum marks, and percentage. See class average and your child's rank if the school shares rankings.

Download or share report cards as PDFs - useful for scholarship applications or record keeping."

### [07:00-09:00] Timetable & Study Materials
**[VISUAL]** Timetable and resources section
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Access your child's timetable from the Timetable menu. Weekly view shows daily classes with subject, teacher, and timing. Some schools include room numbers.

Set reminders for specific classes or use the app's auto-reminder feature for subjects your child struggles with.

Study Materials section contains resources shared by teachers - notes, presentations, video links, and reference documents. Filter by subject or topic. Download materials for offline study or revision before exams."

---

## CHAPTER 3: Fees & Communication (4 min)

### [09:00-10:30] Paying School Fees Online
**[VISUAL]** Fee payment workflow
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Pay fees conveniently from the Fees menu. You'll see complete fee breakdown - tuition, exam fees, library fees, sports fees, etc.

View installment schedule with due dates. Overdue amounts appear in red with late fee penalties if applicable.

Tap 'Pay Now'. Select payment method: debit card, bank transfer, or mobile money. The app uses secure payment gateways - Paystack, Flutterwave, or Stripe.

Enter payment details and confirm. You receive instant confirmation with a digital receipt. Download or email receipts for your records. The school's finance office sees the payment immediately - no manual verification delays."

### [10:30-12:00] Messaging Teachers & School
**[VISUAL]** Messaging interface
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Communicate with teachers directly from the Messages menu. View conversations by teacher or class.

To send a new message, tap New Message, select the recipient - your child's class teacher, subject teacher, or school admin. Compose your message about academic concerns, absence notifications, or general inquiries.

Attach files if needed - medical certificates, consent forms, or documents. Send and track when teachers read your message.

Teachers reply through the system, creating threaded conversations. Enable notifications to receive alerts for new replies. The app time-stamps all messages for reference."

### [12:00-13:00] Best Practices & Tips
**[VISUAL]** Tips overlay with examples
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Get the most from 4SCH with these tips. Check the app daily, even briefly - stay updated on attendance, new assignments, and school announcements.

Set up notifications for important events: absent alerts, fee due reminders, exam results published, and teacher messages.

Use the app to have informed conversations with your child - discuss assignments, review test performance together, and celebrate improvements.

For urgent matters, call the school directly. Use app messaging for non-urgent communication, allowing teachers to respond during their available time.

Regularly review the fee status to avoid surprises. Plan payments around installment due dates."

### [13:00-13:00] OUTRO
**[VISUAL]** Support resources
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Thank you for using 4SCH to stay involved in your child's education! Access FAQs in the app's Help section or contact your school's admin for assistance. Your engagement makes a difference!"
