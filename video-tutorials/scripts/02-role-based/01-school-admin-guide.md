# Video Script: School Administrator Complete Guide

**Duration:** 22 minutes (5 chapters)  
**Target Audience:** School Administrators  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Complete system setup from scratch
- Manage students, teachers, and staff
- Configure fees and academic structure
- Generate reports and analytics

---

**[VISUAL]** Login screen → Dashboard tour

Your dashboard is your command center. At a glance, you see total students, active teachers, today's attendance percentage, and fees collected this session. Quick action buttons let you add students, generate reports, or post announcements instantly.

IMPORTANT: Change your default password immediately. Click your profile icon, select 'Change Password', and set a strong, unique password."

Fees manages structures, invoices, and payments.
Reports provides analytics and exports.

**[VISUAL]** Settings → School Profile screen
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Before anything else, complete your school profile. Go to Settings → School Profile.

Upload your school logo - this appears on report cards and parent communications. Enter your school name, address, contact numbers, email, and website. Set your school code for official documentation.

Add your school motto and principal's information. These details automatically populate on certificates and official documents. Click Save when complete."

## CHAPTER 2: Academic Structure Setup (5 min) [04:00-09:00]

### [04:00-05:00] Creating Academic Sessions
**[VISUAL]** Academics → Session Years → Add New
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Academic sessions represent school years. Go to Academics → Session Years, click Add New Session.

Enter the session name, like '2024-2025'. Set the start date as your first day of school, and end date as the last day. Check 'Default Session' to make this your active year - this is what students and teachers see.

You can have multiple sessions in the system, but only one is active at a time. This is crucial for historical records and reporting."

### [05:00-06:30] Setting Up Classes & Sections
**[VISUAL]** Classes creation workflow
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Now create your classes. Go to Academics → Classes → Add New Class.

For a secondary school, you might create Class 7 through Class 12. Enter the class name and select the medium of instruction - English, for example.

Next, add sections within each class. Select your newly created class, click Add Section. Sections could be A, B, C, or specialized streams like Science, Commerce, or Arts. Set a capacity if you want to limit enrollment. 

Repeat this for all your classes. You can always add more sections later as enrollment grows."

### [06:30-08:00] Creating Subjects
**[VISUAL]** Subjects → Add Subject workflow
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Subjects are the foundation of academic management. Go to Academics → Subjects → Add Subject.

Enter the subject name - Mathematics, English, Physics, etc. Select the subject type: Core subjects are mandatory for all students; Elective subjects are optional.

Assign subjects to specific classes. For instance, Mathematics might be assigned to all classes, while Advanced Chemistry only to Class 11 and 12 Science sections.

Set subject codes for report cards - MATH101, ENG201, etc. Add the maximum marks if standardized across all exams."

### [08:00-09:00] Subject-Teacher Assignment
**[VISUAL]** Assigning teachers to subjects
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Once teachers are added (we'll cover that next), assign them to subjects. Go to Academics → Subject Assignment.

Select a class and section, then choose each subject and its teacher. Teachers will only see classes and subjects assigned to them. You can assign multiple teachers to one subject for different sections, or multiple subjects to one teacher.

This assignment controls access - teachers can only mark attendance and enter grades for their assigned classes."

**[CHAPTER END MARKER]**

## CHAPTER 3: User Management (5 min) [09:00-14:00]

### [09:00-10:30] Adding Students
**[VISUAL]** Students → Add Student form walkthrough
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Time to enroll students. Go to Students → Add Student.

Fill in personal details: full name, date of birth, gender, religion if applicable, and upload a photo. Enter admission number and admission date.

Select the class and section. Choose the session year - typically your current active session.

Add parent/guardian information: father's name, mother's name, contact numbers, email addresses, and residential address. These create parent login accounts automatically.

Enter medical information if needed - blood group, allergies, emergency contact. Set transport details if your school offers it. Click Submit to enroll the student.

The system generates login credentials for both the student and parents, sent via email."

### [10:30-12:00] Bulk Student Import
**[VISUAL]** Students → Import → Excel template
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"For existing schools migrating to 4SCH, manually entering hundreds of students isn't practical. Use bulk import instead.

Go to Students → Import Students. Download the Excel template. Open it in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

Fill in student data following the format exactly: admission number, name, date of birth, class, section, parent details. The template includes instructions and examples.

Save your completed file, return to 4SCH, and upload it. The system validates data and shows any errors. Fix errors in your Excel file and re-upload. Once validated, click Confirm Import.

Students and parent accounts are created in seconds, not hours."

### [12:00-13:00] Adding Teachers
**[VISUAL]** Staff → Teachers → Add Teacher
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Add teaching staff from Staff → Teachers → Add Teacher.

Enter personal details: name, date of birth, gender, photo, contact information. Fill in employment details: employee ID, joining date, qualification, and designation like Senior Teacher or Head of Department.

Set login credentials using their email address. Generate a strong password or let them set it on first login.

Save the teacher profile. Next, assign subjects as we covered earlier - this controls what they can access and manage."

### [13:00-14:00] Adding Non-Teaching Staff & Parents
**[VISUAL]** Staff → Non-Teaching Staff
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Non-teaching staff include accountants, admission officers, librarians, and transport coordinators. Go to Staff → Non-Teaching Staff → Add Staff.

Fill in personal details and select their role. Each role comes with predefined permissions - Accountants access fee management, Transport Coordinators handle routes and drivers.

Customize permissions if needed from the Permissions tab. Create login credentials and save.

Parents are created automatically when you add students. To manage them, go to Parents → All Parents. Here you can edit contact information or reset forgotten passwords."

**[CHAPTER END MARKER]**

## CHAPTER 4: Fee Management (4 min) [14:00-18:00]

### [14:00-15:30] Creating Fee Structures
**[VISUAL]** Fees → Fee Structure → Add Fee Type
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Set up your school's fee structure from Fees → Fee Types.

Create different fee types: Tuition Fee, Admission Fee, Exam Fee, Library Fee, Sports Fee, etc. For each fee type, set if it's compulsory or optional, and if it's refundable.

Next, assign fee amounts to classes. Go to Fees → Assign Fees. Select a class, choose fee types that apply, and enter amounts. Class 10 might have higher tuition than Class 7, for example.

Set payment installments if you allow fee payment in parts - First Term, Second Term, Third Term. Define due dates for each installment to enable automated reminders."

### [15:30-16:30] Generating Invoices
**[VISUAL]** Fees → Generate Invoices workflow
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Once fee structures are set, generate invoices. Go to Fees → Generate Invoices.

Select the session, class, and installment. Click Generate. The system creates invoices for all students in that class automatically.

View individual invoices from Students → Select Student → Fees Tab. Here you see total fees, amount paid, and balance pending. Download PDF invoices to print or email to parents."

### [16:30-17:30] Recording Payments
**[VISUAL]** Fee payment recording process
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"When parents pay fees, record them in the system. Go to Fees → Collect Fee.

Search for the student, select their pending invoice. Enter the amount received and payment mode - Cash, Cheque, Online Transfer, or Card. Add a receipt number for your records.

Click Submit. The system updates the student's fee balance instantly, generates a receipt, and sends a confirmation notification to parents. You can print the receipt or email it directly."

### [17:30-18:00] Online Payment Setup
**[VISUAL]** Settings → Payment Gateway configuration
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Enable online payments to let parents pay from the mobile app. Go to Settings → Payment Gateways.

4SCH supports Paystack, Flutterwave, and Stripe. Enter your gateway credentials - API keys from your payment provider account. Set transaction charges if applicable.

Enable the gateway and save. Parents can now pay fees directly from the parent app using debit cards or bank transfers. Payments sync automatically to student accounts."

**[CHAPTER END MARKER]**

## CHAPTER 5: Reports & Best Practices (4 min) [18:00-22:00]

### [18:00-19:30] Generating Reports
**[VISUAL]** Reports module tour
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"4SCH's reporting module gives you powerful insights. Go to Reports → Attendance Reports.

Filter by class, section, and date range. View attendance percentages, absent students, and trends over time. Export as PDF or Excel for presentations or records.

Fee collection reports show total collected vs pending by class, payment method breakdown, and defaulter lists. Academic reports display exam results, subject-wise performance, and student rankings.

All reports can be scheduled to generate automatically and email to you weekly or monthly."

### [19:30-20:30] Daily Administrator Checklist
**[VISUAL]** Dashboard with checklist overlay
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Successful school management with 4SCH follows a routine. Every morning, check your dashboard for key metrics, review pending approvals for leave requests or admissions, and monitor attendance reports.

Weekly, review fee collection status, generate reports for leadership meetings, check staff attendance, and post announcements for upcoming events.

Monthly, generate financial reports, review academic performance trends, backup your database from Settings, and plan activities for the next month."

### [20:30-21:30] Security & Best Practices
**[VISUAL]** Security settings overview
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Protect your school data with these best practices. Use strong passwords and change them quarterly. Enable two-factor authentication from Settings → Security.

Assign role-based permissions carefully - teachers shouldn't access fee records, accountants shouldn't edit academic data. Review user activity logs monthly to spot unusual access.

Backup your database weekly. Go to Settings → Backup & Data. Schedule automatic backups to cloud storage. Test restoring from backup annually to ensure data integrity."

**[VISUAL]** 4SCH resources screen
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Congratulations! You now have the knowledge to fully manage your school with 4SCH. Refer to our detailed documentation at docs.4sch.com, watch specific feature tutorials, or contact support@4sch.com for assistance.

Next, train your teachers and staff with role-specific guides. Thank you for choosing 4SCH!"
