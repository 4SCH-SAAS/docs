## Production Specifications

### Video Format
- **Resolution:** 1080p (1920×1080) minimum, 1440p (2560×1440) preferred
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps
- **Codec:** H.264
- **Bitrate:** 8000-12000 kbps
- **Container:** MP4
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9

### Audio Format
- **Sample Rate:** 48 kHz
- **Bit Depth:** 16-bit
- **Bitrate:** 192 kbps
- **Channels:** Stereo
- **Format:** AAC
- **Voiceover:** Professional quality, noise-free

### Subtitle/Captions
- **Format:** SRT or VTT
- **Timing:** Synchronized with voiceover
- **Accuracy:** 100% match with script
- **Readability:** 17-20 characters per line max

### Thumbnails
- **Dimensions:** 1280×720 pixels
- **Format:** PNG or JPG
- **Text:** Bold, readable, on-brand
- **Images:** Relevant feature screenshot + text
- **Consistency:** Unified design across series

## Branding & Visual Standards

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** 4SCH brand blue
- **Secondary:** Complementary accent colors
- **Backgrounds:** Clean, professional
- **Text Overlays:** High contrast for readability

### Typography
- **Font:** Professional sans-serif (Arial, Helvetica, or similar)
- **Sizes:** Hierarchical (title > subheading > body)
- **Weight:** Regular for body, bold for emphasis
- **Color:** High contrast against background

### Logo & Branding
- **Logo:** Appear in intro/outro
- **Watermark:** Subtle corner watermark on main content
- **Lower Thirds:** Professional graphics for speaker/title identification
- **Consistency:** Apply same style across all 20+ videos

### Transitions & Effects
- **Transition Duration:** 0.3-0.5 seconds
- **Style:** Smooth fades, wipes, or dissolves
- **Frequency:** Not overused, only to separate sections
- **Effects:** Subtle animations for UI highlights, no distracting movements

## SEO & Discoverability Strategy

### Platform Considerations

**YouTube**
- Title format: "[4SCH Feature] Complete [Role] Guide | Tutorial"
- Description: 200-300 words with timestamps and links
- Tags: 10-15 relevant keywords
- Playlist organization by series and role
- Cards/end screens: Link to related videos
- Subtitles: Auto-generated + manual review

**Video Site/Documentation**
- Embedded video players
- Transcripts for accessibility
- Related article links
- Download script PDFs

### SEO Best Practices
- **Title:** Keyword in first 60 characters
- **Description:** Start with most important info
- **Tags:** Mix broad and long-tail keywords
- **Timestamps:** Include in description for longer videos
- **Captions:** Improves SEO and accessibility
- **Related Content:** Link to documentation

### Keyword Strategy
- Primary keywords: "4SCH [feature]"
- Long-tail keywords: "[feature] tutorial for [role]"
- Comparison keywords: "vs manual processes"
- Problem-solution: "How to [accomplish task]"

## Analytics & Measurement

### Key Metrics to Track
- **View count:** Total views per video
- **Watch time:** Average watch duration
- **Engagement:** Likes, comments, shares
- **Retention:** Where viewers drop off
- **Click-through:** Links clicked in description
- **Search terms:** How people find videos
- **Audience:** Demographics and device usage
- **Conversions:** Views to sign-ups/trials

### Success Benchmarks
- Average watch time: 70%+ of video length
- Engagement rate: 2%+ (likes/comments relative to views)
- Click-through rate (CTR): 3%+ on description links
- Completion rate: Varies by length (expect 50-80%)

### Regular Review Schedule
- Weekly: Basic metrics (views, watch time)
- Monthly: Detailed analytics, engagement analysis
- Quarterly: Series performance, planning updates
- Annually: Strategic assessment, content calendar

## Accessibility Considerations

### Captions & Subtitles
- ✓ 100% accurate verbatim captions
- ✓ Sync with voiceover timing
- ✓ Include sound descriptions (e.g., [notification beep])
- ✓ Speaker identification
- ✓ SRT format for compatibility

### Visual Accessibility
- ✓ High contrast text on backgrounds
- ✓ Zoom level 100-150% for readability
- ✓ Color not sole means of information
- ✓ Sufficient pause time for on-screen text reading
- ✓ Audio descriptions for complex visuals (optional)

### Audio Accessibility
- ✓ Clear, professional voiceover
- ✓ Captions for all dialogue
- ✓ Sound descriptions for non-speech audio
- ✓ No rapid flashing/seizure triggers

### Cognitive Accessibility
- ✓ Clear, simple language
- ✓ Logical flow and structure
- ✓ Short sentences and paragraphs
- ✓ Consistent terminology
- ✓ Visual guides and highlights

## Important Notes for Production Teams

1. **Demo Environment:** Use realistic but clearly-marked demo/test data. Use actual 4SCH interface screenshots where possible.

2. **Nigerian Context:** Include Nigerian names, currency (NGN), locations, and examples throughout. This increases relevance for target audience.

3. **Accessibility First:** Always include accurate captions. Test videos for accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA minimum).

4. **Consistency:** Maintain consistent visual style, music selections, and voiceover tone across all videos in a series.

5. **Quality Over Speed:** Better to release fewer high-quality videos than many poor-quality videos.

6. **User Feedback:** Incorporate feedback from early users. Are there common questions not covered? Update future videos.

7. **Maintenance Plan:** As 4SCH updates features, update relevant scripts rather than re-recording entire videos when possible.

8. **Analytics Monitoring:** Track which videos get views, how long people watch, where they drop off. Use this to improve future content.
