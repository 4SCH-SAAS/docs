**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Next, review the promotion mappings. The system automatically pairs each class with its new level. JSS1A promotes to JSS2A, JSS2A to JSS3A, and so on. Final year students graduate to Alumni status.

If you need custom mappings - perhaps combining two classes or splitting one - you can adjust here. But the automatic mappings are usually correct.

Confirm the mappings, and you'll see the final confirmation screen. This shows the scope of the promotion: 2,456 students across 10 classes. 41 students graduating. A warning reminds you that promotions can't be reversed, so ensure everything is correct.

Click 'Execute Promotion.' The system processes all 2,456 students, updating their records, assigning them to new classes, and generating promotion certificates. It takes about 90 seconds.

When complete, the success screen shows the results: 2,415 promoted, 41 graduated, 10 not promoted due to withdrawal or expulsion. All students have been moved to their new classes for the new academic year."
