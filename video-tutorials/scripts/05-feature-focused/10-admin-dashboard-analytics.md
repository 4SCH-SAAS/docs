### Key Metrics & KPIs (1:30 - 4:00)

**[VISUAL]**
- Zoom into KPI cards
- Highlight each metric as discussed:
  - Total Students card (showing 450 students)
  - Total Teachers card (showing 35 teachers)
  - Fee Collection status (showing 70% collection rate)
  - Gender distribution chart
  - Class-wise enrollment graph

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Let's break down what these numbers mean and why they matter.

**Total Students** shows your current enrollment - this is all active students across all classes. In this example, we have 450 students. This number updates in real-time as you admit or discharge students.

**Gender Distribution** gives you a breakdown of male and female students. Here we see 55% boys and 45% girls. This helps you plan gender-specific facilities, sports programs, or dormitory space.

**Total Teachers and Staff** shows your human resources. This includes full-time teachers, part-time teachers, and non-teaching staff. Use this to calculate your student-teacher ratio - ideally 20:1 or better.

Now, the **Fee Collection Status** - this is one of your most important metrics. It shows three categories:

- **Fully Paid** (green) - 70% - Students who've paid all fees
- **Partially Paid** (yellow) - 20% - Students who've paid some fees
- **Unpaid** (red) - 10% - Students with no payments yet

What does this tell you? A healthy school maintains 75-85% collection rate by mid-session. If your unpaid percentage exceeds 20%, it's time to send payment reminders.

The dashboard also shows **Class-wise Enrollment** - helping you identify which classes are over or under-capacity. If JSS 1 has 60 students while JSS 2 has only 30, you might investigate why enrollment dropped.

**Attendance Trends** - if configured, shows daily, weekly, or monthly attendance rates. A sudden drop in attendance might indicate a problem that needs attention - perhaps exam stress, illness outbreak, or transportation issues."

**[VISUAL]**
- Alert panel highlighted
- Show different types of alerts:

  - Yellow alert: "Fee collection below target (70%)"
  - Blue info: "Exam results pending for 3 classes"
- Click on alert to see details
- Alert action buttons: "Renew Now" or "Dismiss"

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Your dashboard includes an intelligent alert system that flags issues before they become problems.

**Red alerts** are urgent - like 'License expires in 5 days'. Click 'Renew Now' to go directly to subscription renewal. Don't ignore these.

**Yellow warnings** need attention soon - like 'Fee collection below 75% target'. This doesn't require immediate action but should be on your radar.

**Blue information** alerts are informational - like 'Exam results pending for 3 classes'. These remind you of tasks to complete.

The system prioritizes alerts by urgency. Address red alerts first, then yellows, then blues.

You can dismiss alerts once resolved, or set reminders to check them later. But be strategic - don't dismiss important financial or compliance alerts until they're truly resolved."

### Best Practices (7:30 - 8:30)

**[VISUAL]**
- Split screen showing:
  - Daily dashboard check routine
  - Weekly review session
  - Monthly reports
- Calendar marking key review times
- Checklist appearing on screen

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Let me share some best practices for using your dashboard effectively.

**Start Each Day with Your Dashboard**  
Make it your morning routine. Check today's attendance, see if there are urgent alerts, review any overnight announcements. Takes 2 minutes, keeps you informed all day.

**Weekly Deep Dive**  
Every Monday, spend 15 minutes on deeper analysis:
- Compare this week's attendance with last week
- Check fee collection progress toward monthly target
- Review upcoming exams and events
- Identify any trends - attendance dropping? Collections slowing?

**Monthly Review**  
At month-end, generate comparative reports:
- Student enrollment trends
- Fee collection vs target
- Attendance patterns
- Staff performance metrics

Use these insights in your monthly staff meetings. Data drives better decisions than guesswork.

**Set Up Alerts**  
Configure alert thresholds based on your school's standards. If you expect 90% attendance, set alerts to trigger at 85%. If fee collection should be 80%, alert at 75%.

**Export and Share**  
Most dashboard widgets allow exporting to PDF or Excel. Share monthly dashboards with your board, investors, or parent association. Transparency builds trust.

Remember: Your dashboard is only valuable if you act on what it tells you. Numbers without action are just numbers."
