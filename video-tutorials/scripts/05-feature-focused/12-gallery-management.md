**[VISUAL]**

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Creating an album is your first step. Think of albums as folders that organize photos by event or theme.

Let me create an album for our Independence Day celebration. I'll click Create Album, and a form appears.

First, I give the album a clear, descriptive name. Instead of just 'Photos', I'll name it 'Independence Day Celebration - October 2024'. This tells everyone exactly what's inside.

Next, I add a description. This might be: 'Celebrating Nigeria's independence with our students. The day featured patriotic songs, the raising of the national flag, student speeches about national pride, and a special assembly.' Parents love these details – they give context to the photos.

Then I select the session year – in this case, 2024/2025. This is crucial for organization. At year-end, you'll have clear separation between this year's events and last year's events.

I set the event date – October 1st, 2024 – which helps with chronological sorting.

Finally, I choose visibility settings. Should this album be:
- **Public** – Anyone can see it
- **Students Only** – Just your students
- **Parents Only** – Just parents
- **Staff Only** – Just teachers and staff
- **Private** – Only admin

For Independence Day, I'll make it Public so everyone in the community can enjoy it.

I can also mark it as 'Featured' – meaning it appears prominently on the homepage. Perfect for celebrating important events.

Click Save, and the album is created and ready for photos."

**[VISUAL]**
- Open the newly created album
- Show upload area with drag-and-drop interface
- Demonstrate dragging files into the upload area
- Show progress bar as files upload
- Display uploaded photos in grid layout

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Now that the album exists, let's add photos. I click Upload or Add Photos to open the upload interface.

I can upload two ways. Method one is drag and drop – I'll select my Independence Day photos from my computer and drag them right into the upload box. Watch how easy that is.

Method two is clicking Browse Files to select photos manually. Either way works great.

Before uploading, a quick reminder about file sizes. Photos should ideally be:
- Between 1920 and 3840 pixels wide
- Under 5MB each – usually 2-3MB is perfect
- In JPG or PNG format
- Clear and well-lit – avoid blurry or very dark images

For videos, use MP4 format, keep them under 100MB, and limit them to 1-5 minutes for best viewing experience.

As the photos upload, I see a progress bar. The system can handle multiple photos at once – upload 20, 30, even 50 photos together. Don't close your browser until it completes.

Once uploaded, I can add details to each photo. I'll add a caption: 'Chukwudi leads the national anthem while students stand at attention.' This helps viewers understand what they're seeing.

I can also add tags like 'independence day, ceremony, national flag, students' – these help people find photos later when searching.

For photos that really capture the event's essence, I can mark them as Featured. These become the album cover and appear first. One or two featured photos per album is ideal.

Everything saves automatically. That's it – my Independence Day album is live and ready for the community to enjoy!"

"Here's where good organization saves you time. I have many albums now – sports days, graduations, field trips, graduations – and they could become a jumbled mess.

The key is organizing by session year. When someone wants to find 'Graduation 2024', they filter to the 2024/2025 session, and there it is – clean and easy.

Let me show you the gallery view. At the top, there's a Session Year filter. Watch what happens when I click it and switch from 2024/2025 to 2023/2024. Best practices for organization:

**By Session Year** – Always assign the correct academic year when creating albums. This is non-negotiable. It keeps everything organized.

**By Event Type** – Within a session, organize major events into separate albums:
- Independence Day Celebration - October 2024
- Inter-House Sports Day - October 2024
- World Teachers' Day - October 2024
- End of Term Prize Giving - December 2024

**By Term** – Some schools prefer organizing by school term first, then events within that term.

**Archive Old Sessions** – At year-end, your 2023/2024 albums still exist but are safely separated. They're not cluttering the current view.

When I look at the gallery now, I see it's structured beautifully:
- Current session's events are prominent
- Previous session is archived

This simple organization saves countless hours of confusion down the line."

When I edit any album, I can adjust who sees it. Suppose I have a sensitive staff training event – I'd set visibility to Staff Only. No parent or student access.

For school events celebrating achievements – like when Ngozi won the debate competition – I'd set it to Public. Everyone sees it, celebrates with us.

- **Parents Only** – Parent meetings, PTA events

Now, displaying on your website. Your 4SCH gallery has a beautiful widget that shows on your school homepage. When visitors land on your website, they immediately see featured albums – maybe your latest Independence Day photos or the recent graduation ceremony.

### Best Practices & Tips (6:45 - 7:30)

**[VISUAL]**
- Show checklist appearing on screen
- Montage of good vs. poor quality photos
- Timeline showing upload cadence
- Show featured album selection on homepage

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Let me share best practices that separate good galleries from great ones.

**Quality Over Quantity** – Thirty excellent photos beat two hundred mediocre ones. After an event, review all photos. Delete blurry images, remove duplicates, keep only your best shots. Your gallery will shine brighter.

**Get Permission First** – Before uploading any student photos, ensure you have parental consent. This is essential and non-negotiable. It protects students and builds trust.

**Capture a Story** – Arrange photos to show event progression. For Independence Day: Opening ceremony → Students singing anthem → Flag raising → Awards ceremony → Closing. This narrative makes the gallery engaging.

**Upload Promptly** – Don't wait weeks. Upload photos within 48 hours while the event is fresh. Parents are excited right after the event and want to see photos immediately.

**Write Great Captions** – 'Students at event' is boring. 'Chukwudi proudly stands with his team after winning the relay race' tells a story parents love.

**Keep Folders Organized** – Use consistent naming. 'Sports Day - March 2025' not 'Photos March'. Clear names save searching time later.

**Maintain Featured Albums** – Update your homepage featured albums monthly. Keep them fresh and recent. Unfeature old events after 2-4 weeks.

**Monitor Storage** – Check your gallery storage regularly. If you're running low, compress large videos or delete duplicates. Most schools get plenty of space, but good maintenance keeps things running smoothly.

**Respect Privacy** – If a parent requests their child's photos be removed, honor that immediately. If a photo shows a student in distress, don't publish it. Gallery management is a responsibility."
