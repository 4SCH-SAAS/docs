# Video Script: Issuing Certificates

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Target Audience:** School Admins, Principals, and Class Teachers issuing certificates to students or staff
**Learning Objectives:**
- Build a reusable certificate **template** with the design canvas
- Issue student certificates one-by-one or in bulk for a whole class
- Issue staff certificates (employment letters, awards)
- Download, print, or email certificates as PDFs

## [00:15-01:30] Building a Certificate Template (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Sidebar → **Certificate Templates → Add Template**
- Form:
  - **Name** (e.g. "End-of-Term Award")
  - **Type** — Student / Staff
  - **Page size** (A4 / Letter), **Orientation** (Portrait / Landscape)
- Click **Save & Design**
- The Design Canvas opens — a blank A4 with a side panel of draggable elements:
  - Background image upload (school's award border)
  - Text blocks
  - Logo
  - Signature image
  - Date stamp
  - QR code (auto-fills with a verification link)
  - **Form-field placeholders**: {student_name}, {class}, {session}, {issued_date}, {certificate_number}
- Drag elements onto the canvas, double-click any text block to edit and apply font/colour
- Click **Save Design** → template appears in the list with a small preview thumbnail

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"Open **Certificate Templates → Add Template**. Give the template a clear name like 'End-of-Term Award' or 'Leaving Certificate', pick whether it's for **Students** or **Staff**, choose the page size and orientation, and click **Save & Design**.

The design canvas opens — a blank page with a side panel of drag-and-drop elements. Upload your school's certificate border as a **background image**, drop a **logo** at the top, add **text blocks** for the wording, and drag a **signature image** to the bottom for the principal's signature.

The clever bit is the **form-field placeholders** in the side panel — `{student_name}`, `{class}`, `{session}`, `{issued_date}`, `{certificate_number}`. Drag these onto the canvas wherever you want the real value to appear, and 4SCH will fill them in for each individual certificate at the moment you issue it.

Click **Save Design** when you're done. The template appears in the list with a small thumbnail preview, ready to use as many times as you like."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Certificate Templates → Add Template"
- "Student or Staff type"
- "Drag-and-drop design canvas"
- "Placeholders: {student_name}, {class}, {session}, …"

**[VISUAL]**

  - Student picker — multi-select with checkboxes; "Select All" at the top of the list

- Spinner → success page with one row per student, each with **Download PDF** and **Email to Parent** buttons

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"To issue certificates to students, open **Certificates → Issue Certificate**.

Pick the template you built, filter to a class or section if it helps, and tick every student who should receive this certificate. Use **Select All** if it's a whole class or year group.

Any **extra fields** your template needs — for example a specific Achievement description, or a different Issued Date — appear underneath. Fill them in once and they apply to every certificate in this batch.

Click **Preview** to spot-check one student's certificate before generating, then click **Generate**. 4SCH renders one PDF per selected student, fills in all the placeholders with each student's real details, and lists them on a results page.

From there you can **download each PDF individually**, **email it to the parent** with one click, or use **Download All as ZIP** to grab the whole batch — perfect for graduation day when you want to print fifty certificates in one go."

## [03:30-04:30] Practical Tips (60 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Tip cards appearing one by one:
  - 🎯 Build templates once, then reuse — don't redesign per event
  - 🆔 The auto-generated **{certificate_number}** placeholder makes each certificate uniquely traceable
  - 🔍 The **QR code** element automatically embeds a verification URL — anyone scanning it can confirm the certificate is genuine
  - 📅 Set **{issued_date}** to a fixed date for the batch (e.g. ceremony date) instead of "today"
  - 👁️ Always **Preview** the first certificate before generating fifty
  - 📂 Save your school's official signature image in **School Settings → Branding** so it's available across every template

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"A few habits that make this module shine.

Build templates **once** — a school typically only needs five or six (end-of-term, end-of-year, sports award, leaving certificate, employment letter, service award). Re-use them, don't redesign every event.

Use the auto-generated `{certificate_number}` placeholder so every certificate you issue has its own unique reference number — invaluable when a parent calls a year later asking for a duplicate.

The **QR code** element automatically embeds a verification URL. Anyone scanning the QR with their phone is taken to a 4SCH page confirming the certificate is genuine and was issued by your school.

Set `{issued_date}` to a fixed batch date — typically your ceremony date — rather than 'today', so all the certificates in the batch share a consistent date.

Always **Preview** the first certificate before generating fifty — it's the cheapest way to catch a typo before you've printed an entire batch.

And save your school's official signature image once in **School Settings → Branding** so it's available in the side panel of every template you build."
