"Recording an expense is straightforward. Go to Expenses from the main menu, then click 'Add Expense'.

Let's record an electricity bill. 

**Title**: Be specific - 'Electricity Bill - January 2026'. This helps when searching later.

**Category**: Select 'Utilities' from your predefined categories.

**Amount**: Enter ₦45,000. The system uses Nigerian Naira as default currency.

**Date**: When was this expense incurred? We'll use January 31, 2026.

**Payment Method**: How did you pay? Options include Cash, Bank Transfer, Cheque, Mobile Money.

**Reference Number**: Enter the invoice or receipt number - INV-12345. This creates an audit trail.

**Description**: Add details if needed - 'Monthly electricity bill for school compound and dormitories.'

**Attach Receipt**: Upload a photo or PDF of the actual receipt. This is critical for accountability. Click 'Upload' and select your file.

### Best Practices (7:00 - 8:00)

**[VISUAL]**
- Checklist appearing on screen
- Before/after examples of good vs poor expense entries
- Receipt attachment examples

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Here are best practices for effective expense management:

**1. Record Daily**: Don't wait until month-end. Record expenses the same day or next day while details are fresh.

**2. Always Attach Receipts**: No receipt, no record. This is non-negotiable for audit compliance.

**3. Be Specific in Titles**: Instead of 'Supplies', write 'Textbooks for JSS 1 Mathematics - 50 copies'. Future you will thank current you.

**4. Use Reference Numbers**: Invoice numbers, receipt numbers, payment voucher numbers - these create an audit trail.

**5. Review Weekly**: Spend 10 minutes each Friday reviewing the week's expenses. Catch errors early.

**6. Separate Personal from School**: Never mix personal and school expenses. Use separate accounts and cards.

**7. Set Approval Workflows**: For large expenses (above ₦50,000), require approval from principal or board before payment.

**8. Reconcile Monthly**: Match your expense records with bank statements. Every expense should have a corresponding bank debit.

**9. Budget Realistically**: Don't set budgets too tight or too loose. Use historical data from previous years.

**10. Train Your Team**: If multiple people record expenses, ensure they all follow the same process.

Remember: Good expense management isn't about restricting spending - it's about spending wisely and accounting for every naira."
