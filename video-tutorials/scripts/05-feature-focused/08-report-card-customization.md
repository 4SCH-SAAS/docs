**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Step two: Customize the header. Add your school logo, name, and motto. Choose header colors matching your brand. Include contact information so parents can reach you. See changes preview in real-time - this helps you get the look right."

**[SCREEN RECORDING - Step 3: Configure Content Sections]**

- Drag sections to reorder

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Step three: Arrange content sections. You can include: Academic Performance, Competencies, Behavior, Attendance, Comments, and custom sections. Drag to reorder them as you prefer. Toggle off sections you don't need. Your school might want Character Development prominent, so drag it higher."

**[SCREEN RECORDING - Step 4: Add School-Specific Metrics]**

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Step four: Add custom sections reflecting your school's values. If your school emphasizes leadership and service, create that section. Define specific metrics - Class Leadership, Community Service, Environmental Awareness - and how they're rated. These custom metrics show parents what your school values."

**[SCREEN RECORDING - Step 5: Customize Academic Performance Display]**

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Step five: Customize how academic performance displays. Do you show just final grades or also breakdown by continuous assessment and exam? Show class averages so parents see how their child compares to peers? Include grade scale so parents understand what A, B, C means? Choose what information is most relevant."

**[SCREEN RECORDING - Step 6: Design Appearance & Colors]**

- Configure section header styles
- Choose fonts: Professional, Modern, Traditional
- Set text sizes for readability
- Preview how it looks printed vs digital

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Step six: Design the appearance. Choose colors matching your school brand. Select fonts - perhaps professional Times New Roman or modern Arial depending on your school's identity. Adjust text sizes for readability. Preview how it looks both on screen and printed - printing often needs larger text."

## [04:45-05:00] Best Practices (15 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Tips appearing on screen

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"A few quick tips for effective report card customization."

**[ON-SCREEN BULLET POINTS]**
1. **Keep It Clear:** More isn't always better. Focus on information parents and students actually need.

2. **Brand Consistency:** Ensure colors and fonts match your school's overall brand identity.

3. **Test Thoroughly:** Preview all report card variations before deploying to ensure quality.

4. **Annual Review:** Review your template yearly. Update to reflect curricular changes or school evolution.

5. **Accessibility:** Ensure sufficient contrast between text and background for readability, especially for vision-impaired stakeholders.

## Production Notes

**Camera/Screen Recording:**
- Record at 1920x1080 resolution minimum
- Show template builder interface clearly
- Zoom in on design customization details
- Display sample report cards prominently
- Show color and font selection clearly

**Graphics & Animations:**
- Animate color selection showing live preview changes
- Use before/after comparisons for design changes
- Create smooth transitions between sections
- Highlight customizable elements as they're being adjusted

**Audio:**
- Background music: 20-30% volume during voiceover
- Voiceover pace: Professional, conversational (130-150 words per minute)
- Sound effects: Soft success chime when template is saved, notification for feedback received

**Text Overlays:**
- Use 24pt minimum font size
- Display step numbers prominently
- Keep overlays on screen for 3-5 seconds
- Use consistent color scheme

**Timing Cues:**
- Pause on sample report card previews for 3-5 seconds
- Allow color selections to display visibly
- Show layout comparisons side-by-side
- Sync visual transitions with voiceover mentions
