**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Step one: Select invoice criteria. What type of invoice? Term fees for the current term, additional charges, or special fees? Which students? All of them, a specific form, or students in a particular boarding status? The system shows you'll invoice 324 students."

**[SCREEN RECORDING - Step 2: Configure Invoice Items]**

- System auto-populates fees based on student category

**[VOICEOVER]:**

You can override specific items if needed. See the calculation: Tuition ₦300,000, Registration ₦50,000, total ₦350,000."

**[SCREEN RECORDING - Step 3: Set Payment Terms]**

## [04:35-05:00] Best Practices (25 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Tips appearing with supporting graphics

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"A few quick best practices for maximum efficiency."

**[ON-SCREEN BULLET POINTS]**
1. **Batch Invoicing:** Invoice all students at once rather than piecemeal - more efficient and fair.

2. **Scheduled Delivery:** Send invoices at optimal times - perhaps day one of term when parents expect them.

3. **Payment Methods:** Accept multiple payment methods - online, bank transfer, check - to increase payment rates.

4. **Reminders:** Enable automatic reminders before and after due dates to improve collections.

5. **Backup Copies:** Save invoice PDFs both digitally and maintain printed records for compliance.

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Pro tips: Invoice in batches - all students at once. Schedule delivery for the start of term when parents expect invoices. Accept multiple payment methods to make it easy for parents. Enable automatic reminders - they dramatically improve payment rates. And maintain records both digital and printed for compliance."

## Production Notes

**Camera/Screen Recording:**
- Record at 1920x1080 resolution minimum
- Zoom in on invoice details for clarity
- Show calculation examples clearly
- Capture status dashboard legibly

**Graphics & Animations:**
- Animate progress bars during generation
- Use checkmarks for successful operations
- Highlight important invoice sections (Amount, Due Date)
- Show currency symbols (₦) clearly

**Audio:**
- Background music: 20-30% volume during voiceover
- Voiceover pace: Professional, conversational (130-150 words per minute)
- Sound effects: Subtle notification for successful generation, gentle paper shuffle effect

**Text Overlays:**
- Use 24pt minimum font size
- Display step numbers prominently
- Keep overlays on screen for 3-5 seconds
- Use consistent color scheme

**Timing Cues:**
- Pause on invoice previews for clear viewing (3-5 seconds)
- Show progress bars completing fully
- Allow payment dashboard to display with time for absorption
- Sync visual transitions with voiceover mentions
