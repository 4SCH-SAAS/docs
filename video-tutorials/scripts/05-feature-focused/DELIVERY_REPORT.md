## Format Specifications (All Scripts)

### Video Specifications
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Full HD) minimum
- **Frame Rate:** 30 fps
- **Format:** MP4, H.264 codec
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 widescreen

### Audio Specifications
- **Sample Rate:** 44.1 kHz
- **Bit Depth:** 16-bit
- **Channels:** Stereo
- **Background Music Volume:** 20-30% during voiceover
- **Voiceover Pace:** 130-150 words per minute
- **Tone:** Professional, conversational

### Text Overlay Standards
- **Minimum Font Size:** 24pt
- **Display Duration:** 3-5 seconds per overlay
- **Color Scheme:** Consistent with school branding
- **Positioning:** Clear and unobstructed

### Timing Standards
- **Step Display Time:** 2-3 seconds minimum
- **Chart/Graph Display:** 3-5 seconds
- **Transition Speed:** Smooth, 0.5-1 second
- **Voiceover Sync:** Precise timing with visuals
