### What are Class Announcements? (0:30 - 1:15)

**Visual Cues:**
- Split screen: show announcement examples
- Highlight key benefits with icons:
  - Real-time delivery
  - Parent notifications
  - Schedule for later
  - Multiple recipients

**Voiceover Script:**
"Class Announcements are instant messages you can send to your entire class or specific student groups. Your announcements reach students and their parents simultaneously through the app and email. You can send them immediately or schedule them for a specific date and time. This is perfect for homework reminders, schedule changes, event notifications, and important class updates."

**Production Notes:**
- Show 2-3 example announcements on screen
- Use smooth transitions between benefit icons
- Pause briefly after each benefit (1 second)

---

### Step 1: Accessing the Feature (1:15 - 2:00)

**Visual Cues:**
- Screen recording: Click menu → Communications
- Highlight "Announcements" option
- Show dashboard with announcement panel open

**Voiceover Script:**
"First, let's navigate to the Announcements feature. From your dashboard, open the main menu and select 'Communications.' You'll see several options here—click on 'Announcements' to get started. The announcements panel will open, showing any previous announcements and a button to create a new one."

**Production Notes:**
- Zoom in on menu items for clarity
- Use arrow cursor to highlight clickable elements
- Match voiceover timing with on-screen actions
- Speed up non-critical transitions

---

### Step 2: Creating an Announcement (2:00 - 3:30)

**Visual Cues:**
- Screen recording: Click "New Announcement"
- Show form with fields:
  - Title
  - Message body
  - Recipient selection (class/group)
  - Attachment option
  - Send immediately vs. schedule

**Voiceover Script:**
"Click 'New Announcement' to start creating your message. You'll see a straightforward form with several sections. First, add a clear title—this appears in notifications and email subjects. Next, write your message in the body field. Keep it concise but informative.

Now, select your recipients. You can choose to send to your entire class, a specific group of students, or individual students if needed. If you want to include a document or image—like a PDF of homework or an event flyer—use the attachment option.

Finally, decide when to send it. You can send immediately, or use the 'Schedule' option to send at a specific date and time. This is great for scheduling reminders a few hours before an important event."

**Production Notes:**
- Record screen actions slowly and deliberately
- Pause briefly (2-3 seconds) when introducing each new section
- Highlight the attachment feature with a subtle animation
- Show example inputs in gray text for guidance
- Keep message examples realistic and classroom-appropriate

---

### Step 3: Preview and Send (3:30 - 4:15)

**Visual Cues:**
- Show preview screen
- Display how announcement appears to students
- Display how announcement appears to parents
- Highlight "Send" button
- Show confirmation message

**Voiceover Script:**
"Before sending, always preview your announcement. The system shows you exactly how it will appear to students and parents. This is your chance to catch any typos or formatting issues. Once you're satisfied, click the 'Send' button. You'll receive a confirmation that your announcement was delivered successfully, and you'll see it appear in your announcements history."

**Production Notes:**
- Display side-by-side preview of student and parent views
- Use checkmark animation when confirming send
- Show success notification prominently
- Play subtle success sound effect

---

### Best Practices (4:15 - 4:50)

**Visual Cues:**
- Slide-based section with best practice tips
- Icons for each tip:
  - Clock icon (timing)
  - Checkmark icon (clarity)
  - Bell icon (frequency)
  - Document icon (documentation)

**Voiceover Script:**
"Here are some best practices to get the most out of Class Announcements:

First, send announcements during reasonable hours. Avoid late-night messages unless it's truly urgent.

Second, be clear and concise. Parents and students appreciate brief, actionable messages.

Third, avoid over-communicating. Reserve announcements for important updates. Too many messages can lead to notification fatigue.

Finally, save important announcements. The system keeps a full history, which is great for documenting communications with parents."

**Production Notes:**
- Display one tip at a time
- Keep tip text readable (16pt minimum)
- Use subtle transitions between tips
- Background music remains consistent
