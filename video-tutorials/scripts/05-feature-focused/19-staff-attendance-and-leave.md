# Video Script: Staff Attendance & Leave

**Duration:** 7 minutes
**Target Audience:** School Admins, Accountants, and Principals managing staff attendance and leave
**Learning Objectives:**
- Record daily staff attendance in seconds
- Browse attendance by day, by month, or by individual staff
- Set up leave types and yearly leave allowances (leave masters)
- Submit, review, approve, and reject leave requests
- See how leave automatically flows into payroll

## [00:20-01:30] Daily Staff Attendance (70 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Sidebar → **Staff → Staff Attendance**
- Page shows today's date and a list of every active staff member with three status buttons next to each name:
  - **Present** (green)
  - **Absent** (red)
  - **On Leave** (blue, auto-set when there's an approved leave covering today)
- Top of page: **Mark All Present** quick action
- Tick a few rows individually, then click **Save**
- Confirmation toast: "Attendance saved for [date]"
- Page reload shows the same screen with the date pinned and the ticks persisted

**[VISUAL DETAIL]**
- One row shows the staff member already marked **On Leave** with the leave reason on a hover tooltip — the row's status buttons are locked

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"Recording daily staff attendance takes seconds.

Open **Staff → Staff Attendance**. You'll see today's date at the top and every active staff member listed below with three quick-tap buttons: **Present**, **Absent**, or **On Leave**.

In most schools the answer for nearly everyone is **Present**, so the **Mark All Present** shortcut at the top gets you 90% of the way in one click. From there, just tap **Absent** for anyone who isn't in, and click **Save**.

You'll notice that anyone who has an approved leave covering today is already marked **On Leave** automatically — the row is locked so you can't accidentally overwrite their leave status. Hover over their row to see the leave reason as a tooltip."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Staff → Staff Attendance"
- "Mark All Present, then adjust"
- "Approved leave = locked On Leave row"

## [01:30-02:30] Browsing Attendance History (60 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Top of attendance page → tab switcher: **Daily | Monthly | Per Staff**
- Click **Monthly** → calendar grid for the current month with totals per day at the bottom
- Click **Per Staff** → search for a staff member → their full monthly grid appears with green/red/blue dots per day and totals at the right: **Present X · Absent Y · Leave Z**

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"For history, switch to the **Monthly** or **Per Staff** tab at the top.

The **Monthly** view shows the whole month at a glance — every day across every staff member, with totals at the bottom of each day so you can spot a day where attendance dipped unusually.

The **Per Staff** view is the one accountants love — pick a staff member, see their entire month as a grid of coloured dots, with their totals on the right ready to feed straight into a payroll discussion.

Both views have an **Export to CSV** icon if you need the raw data for a spreadsheet."

## [02:30-03:45] Setting Up Leave Types (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Sidebar → **Staff → Leave Types** (also labelled **Leave Masters**)
- List of existing leave types: Annual Leave, Sick Leave, Casual Leave, Maternity, Paternity, Study, Unpaid
- Each row shows: name, annual allowance (days), **Paid** (yes/no), session year
- Click **Add Leave Type** → form:
  - Name
  - Annual Allowance (days)
  - Paid toggle (Paid → doesn't affect payroll; Unpaid → reduces "Affected by Leave" payroll components)
  - Session year (auto-selected)
- Save → new leave type appears in the list

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"Before you can approve a leave request, the school needs **leave types** set up. This is a one-time job at the start of each session year.

Open **Staff → Leave Types**. You'll usually see the common defaults already: Annual, Sick, Casual, Maternity, Paternity, and an **Unpaid** category.

To add your own, click **Add Leave Type** and fill in three things:

- A clear **name** that staff will see when requesting leave
- An **annual allowance** in days — how many of this type each staff member gets per session year
- A **Paid** toggle — Paid leave doesn't affect payroll, Unpaid leave reduces every salary component you flagged as **Affected by Leave** back in the Payroll Settings video.

Each leave type is scoped to the current session year, so you naturally start fresh each new year."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Staff → Leave Types"
- "Annual allowance in DAYS"
- "Paid vs Unpaid = the payroll impact toggle"

## [03:45-05:15] Staff Requesting Leave (90 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Switch to a Staff member's account
- Sidebar → **My Leave → Request Leave**
- Form:
  - **Leave Type** dropdown (shows their remaining balance next to each option, e.g. "Sick Leave — 8 days remaining")
  - **From** and **To** date pickers
  - **Reason** textarea
  - Optional **Attachment** (e.g. a medical certificate)
  - Live preview: "You are requesting 3 days of Sick Leave from 12 Aug to 14 Aug"
- Click **Submit Request**
- Status appears on the staff's **My Leave → Requests** list as **Pending** (yellow)
- Notification fires to the school admin/principal

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"Now from the staff side. When a teacher or other staff member needs to take leave, they sign in to 4SCH and open **My Leave → Request Leave**.

The leave-type dropdown shows their **remaining balance** beside each option, so they always know how many days they have left of each type — no second-guessing.

They pick the leave type, the from-and-to dates, write a reason, and optionally attach a supporting document like a medical certificate. The form shows a live preview of exactly what they're about to request — for example, *'You are requesting 3 days of Sick Leave from 12 Aug to 14 Aug'*.

When they click **Submit Request**, the leave appears on their **My Leave → Requests** list as **Pending**, and a notification fires to the school admin or principal who approves leave at your school."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "My Leave → Request Leave"
- "Balance shown right next to each type"
- "Submit → notifies approver"

## [05:15-06:15] Approving or Rejecting Leave (60 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Back as School Admin
- Sidebar → **Staff → Leave Requests**
- Filter: **Show Pending only**
- A row → click to open detail panel
- Detail panel shows: staff name, leave type, dates, reason, attached document, balance after this request
- Two primary buttons: **Approve** (green) and **Reject** (red)
- Optional comment textarea
- Click **Approve** with a comment "Approved — get well soon"
- Status flips to **Approved** (green), the row disappears from the Pending filter
- Notification fires back to the staff member
- A quick cut to the **Staff Attendance** page: the approved dates now show automatically as **On Leave** (locked)

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"As the approver, open **Staff → Leave Requests**. Filter to **Pending** so you see what needs action.

Click a row to see the full request — staff name, leave type, dates, reason, any attached document, and the balance they'd have left after this request. That last number is the one to check most often: it stops you accidentally approving more days than someone actually has.

When you've made your decision, click **Approve** or **Reject**, optionally add a comment (which the staff member sees), and submit. The status changes immediately, the staff member is notified, and — this is the magic part — the approved dates are automatically marked as **On Leave** on the daily attendance page. No double-entry."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Staff → Leave Requests"
- "Filter to Pending"
- "Approve / Reject with optional comment"
- "Approval auto-fills the attendance page"

## [06:15-06:45] How This Feeds Payroll (30 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Quick diagram:
  - 👤 Staff submits leave →
  - 👔 Admin approves →
  - 📅 Attendance auto-marks On Leave →
  - 💰 Payroll generation reads it → Paid leave: no impact · Unpaid leave: pro-rates "Affected by Leave" components

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"The reason attendance and leave matter so much is what happens next. When you generate monthly payroll, 4SCH reads each staff member's attendance and approved leave for the month and applies the rules:

- **Paid leave** — no impact on the payslip
- **Unpaid leave** — every salary component flagged as **Affected by Leave** is reduced proportionally for the days off
- **Absent days** (not covered by leave) — treated as unpaid for components flagged as Affected by Leave

That's why getting attendance and leave right matters: payroll inherits all of it automatically."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Attendance + Leave → Payroll"
- "Paid leave = no impact"
- "Unpaid leave / Absent = pro-rated deductions"
