## Production Notes

**Camera/Screen Recording:**
- Record at 1920x1080 resolution minimum
- Maintain consistent cursor speed and click clarity
- Show full timetable grid during walkthroughs
- Zoom in on specific cells when demonstrating details

**Graphics & Animations:**
- Animate conflict warnings in red
- Use green checkmarks for successful operations
- Create smooth transitions between view modes
- Highlight buttons before clicking them

**Audio:**
- Background music: 20-30% volume during voiceover
- Voiceover pace: Professional, conversational (130-150 words per minute)
- Sound effects: Subtle (soft click for button presses, gentle notification sound)

**Text Overlays:**
- Use 24pt minimum font size for readability
- Display step numbers prominently (Step 1, Step 2, etc.)
- Keep overlays on screen for 3-5 seconds
- Use consistent color scheme (school brand colors)

**Timing Cues:**
- Each visual should align exactly with voiceover mentions
- Pause screen for 2-3 seconds on important screens
- Show cursor movement clearly before clicking
