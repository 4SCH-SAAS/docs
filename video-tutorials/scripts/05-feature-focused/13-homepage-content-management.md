**First, Homepage Sliders** - these are rotating visual banners at the top. They're perfect for welcoming students back at term start, promoting school events, celebrating achievements like WAEC results, or announcing important deadlines like fee payments.

### Managing Homepage Sliders (1:30 - 3:30)

**[VISUAL]**
- Navigate to Admin Panel
- Go to Settings → Content Management → Homepage Sliders
- Highlight the slider management interface
- Show list of existing sliders

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Let's start with sliders. To access slider management:

1. Log into your Admin Panel
2. Go to Settings or Content Management
3. Click on 'Homepage Sliders' or 'Slider Management'

You'll see a list of all your sliders. Each shows a thumbnail, title, status, and order number.

**Creating a New Slider:**

Click 'Add New Slider' or 'Create Slider'. You'll need:

**An Image** - preferably 1920 by 600 pixels in landscape format. Keep it under 1 megabyte for fast loading. Use JPG or PNG format. Here's the important part: use high-quality, clear images - blurry images look unprofessional. Include your school's logo and use consistent branding colors.

For example, if you're welcoming students back to school, use an image of smiling students in uniform. If promoting your sports day event, show your sports field or students competing.

**A Title** - keep it short and catchy. Examples from Nigerian schools:
- 'Welcome Back! First Term 2024/2025!'
- 'WAEC Results Out - 98% Pass Rate!'
- 'Inter-House Sports Competition - March 15th'
- 'School Fees Due - Payment Portal Open Now'

**A Description** (optional) - supporting text, keep it brief. Example: 'Register your child now - limited slots available!'

**A Link** (optional) - where should users go when they click? Maybe your fee payment portal, event registration form, or a news article.

**Display Duration** - how long before rotating to the next slider. I recommend 5-7 seconds. Too short and people can't read; too long and they get bored.

**Order/Priority** - set which slider shows first (1), second (2), and so on.

**Status** - mark as Active so it appears on the homepage.

Before publishing, click Preview to see how it looks on desktop and mobile. Test on your phone!

Once you're happy, click Save or Publish. Your slider goes live immediately.

**Best Practice:** Keep 3-5 active sliders rotating. Update them at least monthly. Remove outdated sliders right away - nothing looks worse than a slider from last term still on the homepage!"

### Creating & Posting Announcements (3:30 - 5:15)

**[VISUAL]**
- Navigate to Communication → Announcements
- Show announcements list
- Click "Create Announcement"
- Show the announcement creation form

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Now let's look at announcements - these are how you communicate important information quickly.

**Where to Find Announcements:**
Go to Communication → Announcements, or use the Quick Actions panel on your dashboard.

**Creating an Announcement:**

Click 'Create Announcement' or 'New Announcement'.

**Title** - be clear and specific. Examples:
- 'School Closure - Public Holiday'
- 'Exam Timetable Released'
- 'First Term Fees Payment Reminder'
- 'Parent-Teacher Meeting - Saturday, March 22nd'

**Message** - write your full announcement. Keep it clear and organized:
- Put the most important information first
- Use bullet points for lists
- Include specific dates, times, and locations
- Bold important information
- Keep paragraphs short

Here's an example: 'The school will be CLOSED on Monday, March 17, 2025 for Independence Day. Classes will resume on Tuesday, March 18, 2025. Enjoy the holiday safely!'

**Priority Level** - this is important:
- **High** (Red) - for urgent news that sends push notifications. Use this for closures, emergencies, or critical deadlines
- **Medium** (Yellow) - for important but not urgent information
- **Low** (Green) - for general information that doesn't need notifications

**Target Audience** - who should see this?
- All Users
- Students Only
- Parents Only
- Teachers/Staff Only
- Specific Classes or Sections

For example, post a fee reminder to Parents only. Post exam timetables to Students, Parents, and Teachers.

**Attachments** (optional) - attach PDFs, forms, or documents. For example, attach the full exam timetable PDF.

**Expiry Date** (optional) - set when the announcement should no longer appear. For event announcements, set it to the day after the event.

**Before Sending:**
Click Preview to review your work. Check for typos and clarity. Make sure it looks good on mobile. When ready, click Send or Publish.

**Announcement Best Practices:**
- Proofread everything before sending
- Don't mark everything as High Priority - people stop taking urgent alerts seriously
- Send at good times: weekday mornings (8-10 AM) or evenings (7-9 PM)
- 2-3 announcements per week is ideal. Daily announcements for one topic are fine, but multiple daily for non-urgent items causes announcement fatigue
- Include all necessary details - don't assume people know dates or times"

### Configuring Featured Sections (5:15 - 6:30)

**[VISUAL]**
- Show homepage with featured sections highlighted
- Navigate to Settings → Homepage Settings or Featured Content
- Show configuration options
- Click through featured section examples

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Featured sections are blocks on your homepage that highlight important content. Let's configure them.

**What Are Featured Sections?**

These are customizable areas where you showcase:
- Upcoming events in your school calendar
- Latest news or achievements
- Quick links to important portals
- Important documents or forms

**Configuring Featured Content:**

Go to Settings → Homepage Configuration or Featured Content Settings.

You can usually customize:

**Featured Events** - display your upcoming school events. Parents and students see at a glance when the next important date is. Include the event name, date, and a brief description.

**Latest News** - showcase your most recent announcements or news articles. This keeps your homepage feeling fresh and active.

**Quick Links** - add shortcuts to frequently used resources:
- Fee Payment Portal
- Parent Portal
- Academic Calendar
- Contact Us form
- Student Results

For Nigerian schools, you might add:
- WAEC Registration Portal
- Exam Timetables
- School Holiday Dates
- Admission Form

**Featured Achievements** - display your school's recent wins and celebrations. Did your debate team win a competition? Did you get excellent WAEC results? Feature it! This builds school pride and attracts prospective students and parents.

**Customization Options:**
- Enable or disable each section
- Set how many items to display (e.g., show next 5 events, or latest 3 news items)
- Choose display order
- Set colors and styling to match your school branding

**Best Practice:** Don't feature outdated content. If your featured event was last month, it looks unprofessional. Check featured sections weekly and update them as content changes."

### Updating School Information (6:30 - 7:30)

**[VISUAL]**
- Navigate to Admin Panel
- Go to Settings → School Information or School Details
- Show information fields
- Highlight each section as discussed

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Your school information section is where you maintain your school's contact details and basic information. Keep this accurate and current.

**Where to Update:**
Go to Admin Panel → Settings → School Information or School Details.

**Key Information to Maintain:**

**School Name** - ensure it's spelled correctly and formatted as you want it to appear everywhere in the system.

**Contact Information:**
- Phone number(s) - list all reachable phone numbers
- Email address - the general school email parents and staff use to contact you
- Website - if you have one
- Social Media - Facebook, WhatsApp, Twitter handles for your school

For Nigerian context, many schools list multiple phone numbers because parents might have different providers. Include WhatsApp if available.

**Location & Address:**
- Full address
- Local Government Area (LGA)
- State
- GPS coordinates if available

This is crucial for parents finding your school location, especially for new parents or open day visitors.

**School Hours:**
- Morning classes start time
- Classes end time
- Closing time for admin office

List both during school days and during holidays, if different.

**School Logo & Images:**
- Upload your official school logo
- Upload a school banner image
- Upload school photos for the homepage gallery

Make sure logo is high resolution and includes your school name clearly.

**Additional Information:**
- Principal's name and qualification
- Year founded
- School motto
- Vision and mission statements (if you want to display)
- Brief description of your school

**Important:** When you update any information, click Save immediately. Always proofread - if you misspell your school name or phone number, parents can't contact you!

**Update Schedule:**
- Check school hours at term start - they might change for exams or holidays
- Update principal's name when there's a change
- Refresh photos seasonally
- Verify phone and email monthly"

### Best Practices & Content Strategy (7:30 - 8:15)

**[VISUAL]**
- Show calendar view
- Show content planning document
- Timeline of seasonal content ideas
- Checklist appearing on screen

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Let me share strategies for keeping your homepage fresh and professional.

**Content Calendar:**

Plan ahead. Create a simple monthly content calendar:

**Week 1:** Welcome message or monthly theme slider, announcement of key events

**Week 2:** Promote upcoming event with slider, announce deadlines

**Week 3:** Feature achievements or student wins with slider, post event updates

**Week 4:** Preview next month, recap and thank you announcement

**Seasonal Content Ideas:**

**Term Start (September, January, April):**
- 'Welcome Back' slider with happy students
- Announcement about term calendar and class schedules
- Featured section with academic calendar
- Reminder about required school fees

**Mid-Term (October, February, May):**
- Progress updates slider
- Announce upcoming exams or events
- Feature student achievements
- Post about extra lessons or support programs

**Exam Period (November, March, July):**
- Post exam timetables immediately
- Create 'Good Luck' announcement
- Feature exam success tips
- Remind about exam rules

**End of Term (December, March, July):**
- 'Results Release' announcement with celebration
- Share top achievers and statistics - Chioma Okonkwo: 9 A's, Ibrahim Musa: 8 A's
- Holiday greeting slider
- Preview next term

**Special Events:**
- Independence Day (March 29th) - patriotic slider
- Christmas/New Year - festive greetings
- Easter - appropriate messages
- Sports Day - event promotion
- Open Day - special announcements
- Graduation - celebration and thank you

**Quick Reminders:**

**DO:**
- ✅ Keep content fresh - update at least monthly
- ✅ Use high-quality images
- ✅ Proofread everything - typos hurt credibility
- ✅ Test on mobile devices
- ✅ Include specific dates and times
- ✅ Get feedback from staff about what should be featured

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Leave outdated sliders active - nothing looks worse
- ❌ Overcrowd with too many items - keep it clean
- ❌ Use copyrighted images without permission
- ❌ Forget important deadlines
- ❌ Set everything as high priority
- ❌ Ignore typos and errors

**Pro Tip:** Batch prepare content. Create 3-4 sliders at once for the month ahead. Save seasonal content to reuse next year. Get students or communications staff to help create content - it increases engagement!"
