# CA on Mobile Apps — Quick Guide

**Video Duration:** 5-6 minutes  
**Target Audience:** Teachers, Students, and Parents using mobile apps  
**Difficulty Level:** Beginner  
**Prerequisites:** Mobile app installed, school admin has configured CA on an exam

---

## Video Objectives

By the end of this short video, viewers will know:

1. How teachers enter CA marks from the Staff Teacher app
2. How students/parents view CA breakdowns in the Parent-Student app
3. How marks are validated against CA totals
4. Where to find help if the CA dropdown doesn't appear

---

## Script

### INTRO (0:00 - 0:30)

**[Screen: Split screen — teacher app + parent app]**

**Narrator:**
"Welcome to a quick guide on using Continuous Assessment — or CA — features on the 4SCH mobile apps. Whether you're a teacher entering marks or a parent viewing your child's results, this video shows you everything in under six minutes."

---

### PART 1: TEACHERS — Entering CA Marks (0:30 - 2:30)

**[Screen: Tap Staff Teacher app icon]**

**Narrator:**
"Teachers, open the **Staff Teacher app** and tap **Academics**."

**[Screen: Academics menu → Offline Exam Result]**

"Select **Offline Exam Result**."

**[Screen: Filter selection — class, exam, subject]**

"Choose your class section, the exam, and a subject. If the exam has CA configured, a new dropdown appears: **Assessment Type**."

**[Screen: Assessment Type dropdown showing CA1 (20%) - 20 marks, CA2 (20%) - 20 marks, Exam (60%) - 60 marks]**

"Tap the dropdown to see your options — typically CA1, CA2, and Exam. Each shows its weightage and total marks."

**[Screen: Select CA1 → header updates to "Total Marks: 20"]**

"Select **CA1**. The header updates to show **Total Marks: 20** — the maximum for CA1."

**[Screen: Student list with input fields]**

"The student list appears. Tap each student's input and enter their score. The app prevents you from entering more than 20."

**[Screen: Try entering 25 → error message]**

"Try to type 25 and you'll see an error — protecting you from data entry mistakes."

**[Screen: Save Draft / Submit buttons]**

"When finished, tap **Save as Draft** to keep editing later, or **Submit & Publish** to finalize."

**[Screen: Switch dropdown to CA2 → students cleared, fresh entry]**

"To enter CA2 marks, just switch the dropdown. Your CA1 marks are safely stored. Enter CA2 marks, submit, and you're done."

:::tip
CA marks for different types are completely independent. You can submit CA1 today and CA2 next week.
:::

---

### PART 2: STUDENTS & PARENTS — Viewing CA Breakdown (2:30 - 4:30)

**[Screen: Parent app icon → Tap to open]**

**Narrator:**
"Students and parents, open the **Parent Student app**."

**[Screen: Main menu → Exams → Results]**

"Tap **Exams**, then **Results**. You'll see a list of published exams."

**[Screen: Tap an exam → result screen opens]**

"Tap any exam to view the result."

**[Screen: Subject rows with grades and totals]**

"For each subject, you see the overall marks and grade. But there's a hidden gem."

**[Screen: Tap "View CA Breakdown" under a subject → card expands]**

"Tap **View CA Breakdown** under any subject. The card expands to show how the grade was calculated."

**[Screen: Expanded card showing CA1 18/20, CA2 16/20, Exam 44/60 with colored progress bars]**

"You see each component — CA1, CA2, Exam — with marks, weightage, and a colour-coded progress bar."

**[Screen: Highlight green/blue/orange/red colours]**

"Colours tell the story at a glance:
- **Green:** Excellent, 75% or above
- **Blue:** Good, 60–74%
- **Orange:** Average, 40–59%
- **Red:** Needs improvement, below 40%"

**[Screen: Term Total row at bottom of breakdown]**

"The bottom row shows the **Term Total** — your aggregate score for the subject."

**[Screen: Tap "Hide CA Breakdown" → card collapses]**

"Tap again to collapse. Simple and powerful."

---

### PART 3: PUSH NOTIFICATIONS (4:30 - 5:00)

**[Screen: Phone showing notification "Results Available: Mid-Term"]**

**Narrator:**
"When the school publishes results, both students AND parents receive a push notification. Tap the notification to jump straight to the result."

**[Screen: Notification opens result screen]**

"No need to manually check — the app tells you when there's something new."

---

### PART 4: TROUBLESHOOTING (5:00 - 5:30)

**[Screen: Common issues with checkmark fixes]**

**Narrator:**
"A few quick tips if something doesn't work:"

| Issue | Solution |
|---|---|
| **CA dropdown doesn't appear** | The exam may not have CA configured. Ask the school admin to set it up from the web. |
| **No notification arrived** | Check device notification permissions in app settings. |
| **Marks won't save** | Check your internet connection. The app shows an error if save fails. |
| **CA Breakdown not visible** | The exam may not have CA. Result still shows correctly without it. |
