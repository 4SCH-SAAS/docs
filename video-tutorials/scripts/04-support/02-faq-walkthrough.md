## [00:20-01:30] Account & Access Questions (70 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Account icons, key symbols, login illustration

### "How many users can have access?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"4SCH supports unlimited users based on your school's actual population. You create accounts for:
- All staff members (teachers, administrators, coordinators)
- All students currently enrolled
- Parents of enrolled students
- Support staff (librarians, finance officers)

Each user gets one account and can log in from multiple devices simultaneously. There's no per-user fee - you pay a fixed monthly fee based on your student count."

### "Can a user have multiple roles?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Currently, each user has one primary role. However, administrators can assign specialized roles:
- A teacher can also be a class coordinator
- A teacher can also be a librarian
- An administrator can override and perform teacher functions

Contact your administrator to request role changes."

### "How long does account setup take?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Administrators can create individual accounts in seconds. For bulk uploads of students or staff, the system processes up to 5,000 records in a few minutes.

New users receive welcome emails with login instructions. First-time users must set a new password on login."

## [01:30-02:45] Data & Privacy Questions (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Privacy lock icons, data security visuals, GDPR/compliance badges

### "Is my data secure?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"4SCH uses industry-standard security:
- All data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/SSL)
- Data encrypted at rest (AES-256)
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Automatic daily backups stored in secure data centers
- Compliance with international data protection standards

Your school data is stored in secure servers. We never sell user data to third parties."

### "Who can see my information?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Privacy depends on your role:
- Teachers see only their assigned students' academic data
- Parents see only their own child's information
- Administrators see school-wide data but not private messages
- Students see only their own data and school announcements

Financial data access is limited to finance officers and administrators. Messages between users are private."

### "Can data be deleted?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Data retention depends on type:
- Student academic records are kept for 7+ years (check your education authority requirements)
- Attendance records are kept for 5+ years
- Financial/fee records are kept per accounting standards (usually 7 years)

You can request data export anytime. Deleting accounts removes user login but archives their academic records for compliance.

Contact your administrator or support@4sch.com for data deletion requests."

### "What about GDPR/data protection laws?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"4SCH complies with GDPR, CCPA, and other global data protection regulations. We:
- Only collect necessary data
- Get proper consent before processing
- Allow users to access and download their data
- Delete data upon request (with legal holds for educational records)
- Report any breaches within required timeframes

Your school is the data controller - 4SCH is the processor. Your school's privacy policy applies."

## [02:45-04:00] Feature & Functionality Questions (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Feature screens, integration icons, offline indicator

### "Can 4SCH work offline?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"The web version requires internet. However, the mobile app supports offline mode:
- View previously loaded data (attendance, grades, timetables)
- Mark attendance offline
- Write notes offline

Changes sync automatically when back online. Large data downloads (like full class lists) must be done while online first."

### "How do I integrate 4SCH with other systems?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"4SCH supports:
- CSV/Excel import-export for bulk data transfer
- Email notifications (sent automatically from 4SCH or from your school's own email account)
- SMS gateway integration for notifications
- Mobile app sync with web platform
- Calendar integration (iCal format)

If your school needs a custom integration with another system you already use, get in touch with support@4sch.com — the team will talk you through what's possible on your plan."

### "Can teachers create assignments if students don't have devices?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Yes! 4SCH is flexible:
- Teachers create digital assignments in the app
- Students can submit responses (for those with devices)
- Teachers maintain traditional submission methods (paper, in-person)
- Both can be recorded in the system
- Parents view all submissions and feedback

This makes 4SCH work in low-tech or mixed-device environments."

### "How many students can be in one class?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"4SCH handles class sizes from 1 to 500+ students. Larger classes (100+) might experience slower performance when filtering data. Performance is best with classes under 150 students.

For very large schools, administrators can assign multiple teachers or class coordinators to one class to improve management."

## [04:00-05:15] Billing & Subscription Questions (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Pricing chart, payment methods, invoice

### "How is 4SCH priced?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"4SCH uses per-student pricing:
- You pay for the number of students enrolled
- Unlimited staff accounts included
- Billing based on enrollment count each month
- Discounts available for annual subscriptions
- No setup fees or hidden charges

As student numbers change (admissions/transfers), billing adjusts automatically."

### "What payment methods are accepted?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Schools can pay via:
- Bank transfer/wire transfer
- Credit/debit card (Visa, Mastercard)
- Mobile money (in Nigeria - MTN, Airtel, Glo)
- Checks (with additional processing time)

Invoices are provided monthly or on your billing cycle. Most schools pay monthly."

### "What if we can't afford the subscription?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"We offer:
- Educational discounts for non-profit schools
- Flexible billing arrangements
- Free trial period (usually 30 days)
- Monthly-to-annual discounts
- Discounts for multiple schools in an organization

Contact sales@4sch.com to discuss your school's situation. We work with schools across all budget levels."

### "Can we cancel anytime?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Month-to-month subscriptions can be cancelled with 30 days notice. Annual subscriptions have a 30-day cancellation window after purchase.

After cancellation, you get a 30-day grace period to export your data. After that, all data is archived per legal requirements."

## [06:30-07:45] Technical & System Questions (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Device icons, browser logos, system requirements

### "What devices and browsers are supported?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Web version works on:
- Any modern browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge
- Windows, Mac, Linux computers
- Tablets (iPad, Android tablets)

Mobile apps available for:
- iOS (iPhone/iPad) - iOS 12+
- Android - Android 6.0+

Older devices/browsers may work but aren't officially supported. We recommend updating to current versions for security."

### "What internet speed do I need?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Minimum: 1 Mbps (for basic functions)
Recommended: 5+ Mbps (for smooth experience with files/videos)

4SCH works on 4G mobile networks. WiFi provides the most stable experience. Most operations use minimal data - roughly 1-5 MB per hour of use."

### "Is there a mobile app?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Yes! Free 4SCH apps available on:
- Google Play Store (Android)
- Apple App Store (iOS)

The app has all core features and works offline for viewing data. Some advanced features (like bulk imports) require the web version.

Sync is automatic - changes made on web appear in app within seconds when online."

### "Can multiple people use the same account?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Not recommended. Each user should have their own account for:
- Security (tracking who did what)
- Notifications (targeted to individual)
- Data privacy (seeing only appropriate information)
- Audit trails (compliance requirement)

Sharing accounts violates most school policies. Administrators can create additional accounts free of charge."
