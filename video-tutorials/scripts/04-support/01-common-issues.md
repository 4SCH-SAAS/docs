**Duration:** 9 minutes  
**Target Audience:** Users experiencing problems with 4SCH  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Identify common 4SCH issues
- Learn troubleshooting steps for each issue
- Know when to contact support
- Prevent common problems in the future

## [00:20-01:00] Login Issues (40 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Login page with error messages, password reset screen

### "Forgot Your Password?"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"The most common issue is a forgotten password. Click 'Forgot Password?' on the login page. Enter your email address and you'll receive a password reset link. Check your inbox and spam folder.

Click the link and create a new password following the requirements:
- At least 8 characters
- Mix of uppercase and lowercase letters
- At least one number
- At least one special character (!@#$%)"

**[VISUAL]** Password reset form being filled in

### "Invalid Credentials"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If you're getting 'Invalid credentials', make sure you're:
- Using the correct email or username (case-sensitive username)
- Typing the password correctly
- Entering your school's specific domain if required

Try logging in from an incognito browser window to rule out caching issues."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Check Email/Username Spelling"
- "Remember: Username is Case-Sensitive"
- "Clear Browser Cache if Needed"

---

## [01:00-02:00] Account Access Issues (60 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Account locked icon, permission denied message

### "Your Account is Locked"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"After multiple failed login attempts, your account temporarily locks for security. Wait 15-30 minutes and try again. If the problem persists, contact your administrator - they can unlock your account immediately."

### "Permission Denied"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If you see 'Permission Denied' or can't access a feature, your role may not have that permission. Different roles have different feature access. Contact your school administrator to verify your role or request additional permissions."

**[VISUAL]** Permission settings being adjusted

### "Session Expired"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If you're inactive for 2 hours, your session automatically expires for security. Simply log in again. Your work is saved - any unsaved changes might be lost. Save regularly, especially when working with data."

---

## [02:00-03:15] Data Display Issues (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Dashboard screens with missing data, refresh button, loading spinners

### "Data Not Loading / Blank Dashboard"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If your dashboard is blank or data isn't showing:

First, refresh the page (F5 or Ctrl+R on Windows, Command+R on Mac).

Check your internet connection - 4SCH requires a stable connection.

Clear your browser cache: Go to Settings > Clear Browsing Data > select 'Cached Images and Files' > Clear.

Try a different browser to rule out browser-specific issues.

If using mobile app, try force-closing and reopening the app."

**[VISUAL]** Browser refresh and cache clearing steps

### "Attendance/Grades Not Showing"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If specific data like attendance or grades isn't visible:
- For Teachers: Make sure you're viewing the correct class and date range
- For Admins: Check that you haven't applied filters hiding the data
- For Parents: Your child may not have taken the assessment yet

Check the filter settings in the top-right of the data table."

---

## [03:15-04:30] Performance Issues (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Slow loading animation, timeout error

### "Page Loading Slowly"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If 4SCH is running slowly:

Check your internet speed - you need at least 2 Mbps for smooth operation.

Close other browser tabs and applications using internet.

Avoid peak hours (typically morning in school) when many users are online.

If you're on mobile, make sure you're on WiFi or have strong 4G signal.

Clear your browser cache to remove old files slowing you down."

**[VISUAL]** Speed test being performed, browser tabs being closed

### "Page Timeout / Connection Lost"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If you get a timeout or connection error:
- Check if you're still connected to the internet
- Try refreshing the page
- If on mobile, try switching between WiFi and cellular data
- Wait a few minutes and try again

If timeouts happen frequently, contact your school IT or support@4sch.com with your browser information."

---

## [04:30-05:45] Mobile App Issues (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Mobile app screens, installation process

### "App Won't Install / Update"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If you can't install or update the 4SCH mobile app:

Check you have enough storage space (4SCH needs about 150MB).

Make sure your device OS is up to date:
- iPhone: iOS 12 or later
- Android: Android 6.0 or later

Try uninstalling and reinstalling the app.

If you still have issues, go to the app store and check for user reviews - if many report the same issue, check your device compatibility."

### "App Keeps Crashing"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If the app crashes:
- Force close the app (not just minimize)
- Clear the app's cache: Settings > Apps > 4SCH > Storage > Clear Cache
- Restart your device
- Check your app is fully updated

If crashes continue, uninstall and reinstall."

**[VISUAL]** App cache being cleared on a mobile device

### "Not Receiving Notifications"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If you're not getting notifications:
- Check app Settings > Notifications to ensure they're enabled
- Go to your device Settings and verify the app has notification permission
- Make sure your internet connection is active
- Log out and log back in to refresh connections"

---

## [05:45-07:00] Data Entry Issues (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Form filling, error messages, validation

### "Can't Save / 'Invalid Data' Error"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If you see an error when saving:
- Check all required fields (marked with red asterisks) are filled
- Make sure email addresses are in correct format (example@domain.com)
- Phone numbers should be digits only, no special characters
- Dates must be in the specified format shown in the field
- Names shouldn't include numbers or special characters"

**[VISUAL]** Form fields with validation errors highlighted

### "File Upload Failed"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If you're uploading a file (like attendance or student list) and it fails:
- Verify the file format matches requirements (usually CSV or Excel)
- Check file size - it should be under 10MB
- Make sure column headers match the template exactly
- Ensure no cells are empty in required fields
- Download the template from the system and use it as your example"

---

## [07:00-08:15] Sync & Offline Issues (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Sync icons, offline mode indicator, cloud storage

### "Mobile App Not Syncing"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If your mobile data isn't syncing with the web:
- Check your internet connection
- Manually refresh by pulling down on the screen
- Check for app updates in your app store
- Log out and log back in
- Force close and reopen the app

Sync happens automatically when you're online and have made changes."

### "Offline Mode Issues"
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"4SCH mobile works offline for some features like viewing cached data and creating offline notes. When online, changes automatically upload.

If offline features aren't working:
- Go online to download the data first
- Check Settings > Offline to ensure offline mode is enabled
- Ensure you downloaded data while online"

---

## [08:15-09:00] When to Contact Support (45 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Support contact options, help desk interface

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"If you've tried all troubleshooting steps and still have issues:

Email: support@4sch.com - provide your email, school name, and detailed description

In-App Help: Click the Help icon on your dashboard and use 'Contact Support' form

Your School Administrator: They can help with permission, access, and account issues

When contacting support, include:
- What you were trying to do
- What error you received (screenshot if possible)
- What steps you've already tried
- Your browser and device information

Support typically responds within 24 business hours."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Email: support@4sch.com"
- "Use Contact Form in App"
- "Tell Your Administrator"
- "Include Error Details"

## [08:15-09:00] Prevention Tips (45 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Best practices checklist, save icon, backup

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Prevent issues by:
- Regularly updating your browser and app
- Saving your work frequently (don't rely on auto-save)
- Using strong, unique passwords
- Keeping your profile information current
- Clearing browser cache monthly
- Avoiding multiple simultaneous logins on the same account
- Backing up important reports regularly"

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Keep Software Updated"
- "Save Frequently"
- "Use Strong Passwords"
- "Clear Cache Monthly"
