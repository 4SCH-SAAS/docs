## [02:00-03:15] Email Support (75 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Email interface, contact form, support ticket

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"For issues you can't solve yourself, email our support team at support@4sch.com."

### When to Email Support
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Email support for:
- Technical issues you've tried troubleshooting
- Account or permission problems
- Data issues or inconsistencies
- Feature requests
- Billing questions
- Data export/deletion requests
- Any issue involving personal information

We typically respond within 24 business hours (sometimes same day)."

### How to Email Effectively
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"For faster resolution, include:
1. Your email and school name
2. What you were trying to do
3. What happened (exact error message if possible)
4. Screenshot of the issue
5. Steps you've already tried
6. Your device and browser information
7. When the issue started

Specific details help us solve your problem faster."

**[VISUAL]** Example email being composed with all required details

### What to Include in Screenshots
**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"When taking screenshots:
- Capture the error message fully
- Include any red error text
- Show enough context (menu, buttons visible)
- Hide sensitive information (passwords, full student names)

Don't email passwords - our team never asks for passwords via email."

## [05:15-06:00] Quick Reference: Support Options (45 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Comparison table of support channels

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Here's a quick guide to choosing the right support channel:

**For immediate answers (under 5 minutes)**: Use in-app help or Help Center website. These are available 24/7 and cover 95% of common questions.

**For quick help (5-30 minutes)**: Ask your school administrator - they can usually resolve account/permission issues immediately.

**For detailed help (several hours to 24 hours)**: Email support@4sch.com with details. Good for technical issues, data problems, or feature requests.

**For community perspectives (varies)**: Post in the user community forum to learn how others solved similar issues.

**For urgent issues**: Call your school administrator, who can escalate to priority support if needed."
