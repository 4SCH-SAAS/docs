  - Priority setting (Normal, High, Urgent - affects notification color/urgency)
  - Attachment upload (documents, images, PDFs)
  - Schedule posting (Immediate or Schedule for later date/time)
  - Preview button
  - Publish button
- Announcement appears in:
  - Admin notification center
  - Parent mobile app with notification alert
  - Student portal
  - Teacher dashboard
  - Email notification sent to all recipients

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Announcements broadcast important information to everyone. Go to Communications and create a new announcement. Write your title and message using the rich text editor. Add images, links, and attached documents.

Choose who receives it: all school users, or specific groups like parents or teachers. Set priority level. High-priority announcements show with red flags and urgent alerts. Important announcements might warrant SMS as well.

Schedule announcements for later if you prefer. Type your message now, schedule it for tomorrow morning, and it automatically publishes and notifies everyone at that time. This is perfect for pre-planning.

Preview how your announcement looks to recipients. Check formatting, clarity, and tone. Once satisfied, publish. The system immediately:
- Sends app notifications to all recipients
- Sends emails to those who have email notifications enabled
- Displays in their message centers
- Archives for future reference and search"

- Message composition interface showing:
  - Message text field with formatting toolbar
  - Emoji picker
  - Attachment button (images, files)
  - @mention feature (e.g., "@all", "@absent today", "@not submitted assignment")
  - Recipients list showing all students in class
  - Checkbox to include student parents
  - Send and Schedule options
- Message preview
- Send confirmation

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Teachers send class messages to keep students and parents informed. Open your class, go to Messages, and click 'Send Message'.

Type your message in the composer. Use formatting: bold for emphasis, bullet points for instructions, emojis for friendliness. Attach files - a study guide, sample solutions, or a reading list.

The @mention feature is powerful. Type '@all' to notify everyone in class, '@absent today' to message only students who were marked absent, or '@not submitted' to remind students who haven't turned in an assignment.

Choose who receives the message: students only, or include parents. If you're announcing an assignment, let parents know. If it's a casual reminder about tomorrow's lesson, just send to students.

Use the Schedule feature to send messages at specific times - maybe a reminder one hour before class starts. Preview your message for tone and clarity, then send. Students and parents receive instant notifications."

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"One-on-one messaging creates private, focused conversations. Teachers message parents about individual student progress, concerns, or achievements. Open the parent's chat from your contacts.

All previous messages appear in a scrollable history. Type your new message and send. The message appears immediately in their inbox with a notification. You see read receipts showing when they've read your message.

Parents reply, and their message appears in your chat thread. This conversation history is permanent - scroll back months to review what you've discussed. Nothing gets lost in email or forgotten.

These one-on-one chats are perfect for sensitive discussions: 'Chioma's been struggling with math lately. Can we discuss support at home?' The parent sees the message, reads it privately, and responds thoughtfully.

You can search your entire conversation history to find specific topics. And toggle notifications per conversation - maybe you want alerts for certain parents but not others."

## [05:30-07:00] Message Approvals & Moderation (90 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Settings > Communication Policies
- Message approval workflow configuration:
  - Enable message approval toggle
  - Set which message types need approval (Student to Teacher, Teacher to Parent, All Messages)
  - Approve timeout (e.g., urgent messages auto-publish after 24 hours)
  - Inappropriate content detection settings
- Approval Queue showing pending messages:
  - Message preview
  - Sender and recipient info
  - Flagged reason (if auto-detected as inappropriate)
  - Approve button (checkmark)
  - Reject with reason (X with comment)
  - Report option
- Rejection template for common reasons:
  - "Language - please rephrase"
  - "Off-topic - keep to school-related matters"
  - "Contains personal contact info - use school communication only"
- Message history showing approved/rejected status
- Statistics on moderation: approval rate, rejection reasons

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"For schools with younger students or strict communication policies, set up message approvals. Messages from students to teachers, or teachers to parents, can be reviewed before sending.

Configure the approval system in Communication Policies. Choose which message types require approval. Optionally set an auto-publish timeout - urgent messages publish automatically after 24 hours even if not yet approved.

The system has basic inappropriate content detection. Words flagged as potentially inappropriate get a warning during composition and may go to the approval queue.

Administrators review pending messages. Click approve to send the message, or click reject with a reason. If rejected, the sender gets feedback: 'Message contains personal contact info. Please use school communication only.' They can revise and resubmit.

This moderation prevents cyberbullying, protects privacy, and maintains professional communication standards while still enabling authentic interactions."

## [07:00-08:30] Notification Settings & Message History (90 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- User Settings > Notifications
- Communication notification preferences:
  - Toggle for announcements
  - Toggle for group messages
  - Toggle for one-on-one messages
  - Toggle for discussion replies
  - Notification method: In-App, Email, SMS, All
  - Quiet hours setting (e.g., no notifications 9 PM to 7 AM)
  - Critical messages always notify (overrides quiet hours)
- Message Center/Inbox showing:
  - All messages in chronological order
  - Filter tabs: All, Announcements, Discussions, Personal, Unread
  - Search bar for finding specific messages
  - Mark as read/unread
  - Archive conversations
  - Delete option (with confirmation)
  - Favorite important messages with star icon
- Message search results showing:
  - Highlighting search terms
  - Message context and sender
  - Quick reply from search result

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Everyone controls their own notification preferences. Go to Settings > Notifications to customize how you receive messages.

Toggle each message type on or off. Want announcements but not every group message? Choose that. Select notification method: show in the app only, send emails, send SMS, or all three.

Set quiet hours. Teachers might not want notifications during sleep time. Enable quiet hours, say 9 PM to 7 AM, and the app won't notify during those times - messages arrive silently and you see them when you open the app.

Critical messages - marked urgent by administrators - always notify regardless of quiet hours. This ensures important emergency information reaches everyone.

The Message Center shows all your communications in one place. Search for specific topics: 'Search for "exam"' finds all messages mentioning exams across all conversations. Filter by message type - just see announcements, or just personal messages from parents.

Archive old conversations to clean up your inbox while keeping history searchable. Star important messages to keep them at the top. The system maintains full history indefinitely."
