# Online Exams - Student's Complete Guide

**Video Duration:** 10-12 minutes  
**Target Audience:** Students  
**Difficulty Level:** Beginner  
**Prerequisites:** Basic 4SCH app/web knowledge

---

## Video Objectives

By the end of this video, students will be able to:
1. Access and start online exams
2. Navigate through questions effectively
3. Manage time during exams
4. Submit exams correctly
5. View their results
6. Handle common issues

---

## Script

### INTRO (0:00 - 0:30)

**[Screen: Student with phone/tablet, studying]**

**Narrator:**
"Hey students! Taking an online exam for the first time? Don't worry - we'll walk through everything step by step. By the end of this video, you'll know exactly how to access exams, answer questions, manage your time, and submit with confidence. Let's make your first online exam stress-free!"

---

### SECTION 1: Before You Start (0:30 - 2:00)

**[Screen: Checklist animation]**

**Narrator:**
"Before starting any online exam, make sure you have these five things ready:"

**[Visual: Checkmarks appearing]**

**1. Stable Internet**
"WiFi is better than mobile data. Test your connection before exam time."

**2. Fully Charged Device**
"Whether phone, tablet, or laptop - charge to 100% before starting."

**3. Exam Key**
"This is a 6-digit number your teacher will share. Write it down!"

**4. Quiet Environment**
"Find a quiet spot with no distractions."

**5. Enough Time**
"Make sure you have the full exam duration available. Don't start if you only have 10 minutes free for a 60-minute exam!"

---

### SECTION 2: Accessing Your Exam (2:00 - 4:00)

**[Screen: Split screen - Mobile app and Web portal]**

**Narrator:**
"You can take exams on your phone using the 4SCH Student App, or on a computer using the web portal. Let me show you both."

#### On Mobile App

**[Screen: App demonstration]**

"1. Open the 4SCH Student App
2. Tap the 'Exams' tab at the bottom
3. Switch to 'Online Exams' tab
4. You'll see a list of your exams"

**[Screen: Exam status badges]**

"Exams have different statuses:
- Blue badge 'Upcoming' - not started yet
- Green badge 'Ongoing' - available to attempt now
- Gray badge 'Completed' - deadline passed or you've submitted"

#### On Web Portal

**[Screen: Browser demonstration]**

"1. Go to ngs.4sch.com in your browser
2. Login with your credentials
3. Click Exams, then Online Exams
4. Same list appears"

---

### SECTION 3: Starting the Exam (4:00 - 5:30)

**[Screen: Exam details page]**

**Narrator:**
"Before clicking 'Start Exam', check these details:"

**[Screen: Highlight each detail]**

"- Subject name - make sure it's the right exam
- Duration - how long you have
- Total questions - how many to answer
- Total marks - maximum score
- End date - the deadline"

**[Screen: Start Exam button]**

"Ready? Click 'Start Exam'."

**[Screen: Exam key entry screen]**

"Now enter the 6-digit exam key your teacher gave you. Type carefully - no spaces!"

**[Screen: Key validation]**

"If you get 'Invalid Exam Key' error, double-check the number with your teacher and try again."

**[Screen: Timer starts]**

"Once the key is accepted, the timer starts immediately! You're in the exam now."

---

### SECTION 4: Answering Questions (5:30 - 7:30)

**[Screen: Question interface]**

**Narrator:**
"Here's what you see on each question:"

**[Screen: Label each element]**

"- Question number at the top
- The question text (might include images)
- Answer options
- Marks for this question
- Timer counting down"

#### Single Answer Questions

**[Screen: Radio button demonstration]**

"For single answer questions, you'll see circles next to options. Tap or click one option. Only one can be selected."

**[Screen: Changing answer]**

"Changed your mind? Just click a different option. The previous selection disappears."

#### Multiple Answer Questions

**[Screen: Checkbox demonstration]**

"For multiple answer questions, you'll see checkboxes - you can select more than one option."

**[Screen: Important callout]**

"IMPORTANT: You must select ALL correct answers to get marks. If a question has answers A and C, and you only select A, you get zero marks!"

#### Questions with Images

**[Screen: Image question example]**

"Some questions have diagrams or charts. Tap the image to view it larger. Pinch to zoom on mobile."

---

### SECTION 5: Navigating & Time Management (7:30 - 9:00)

**[Screen: Navigation buttons]**

**Narrator:**
"Use Next and Previous buttons to move between questions. You don't have to answer in order."

**[Screen: Strategy demonstration]**

"Smart strategy: Answer easy questions first, then come back to difficult ones."

**[Screen: Timer focus]**

"Keep an eye on the timer! It's usually at the top of the screen."

**[Screen: Time remaining color change]**

"When time is running low (last 5-10 minutes), the timer often turns red. That's your warning!"

**[Screen: Time management tips]**

"Pro tip: If you have 60 minutes for 30 questions, that's 2 minutes per question. Pace yourself!"

---

### SECTION 6: Submitting Your Exam (9:00 - 10:30)

**[Screen: Review answers]**

**Narrator:**
"Before submitting, review your answers if time allows. Scroll through all questions to make sure nothing is blank."

**[Screen: Submit button]**

"When ready, click 'Submit Exam' button."

**[Screen: Confirmation dialog]**

"You'll see a confirmation: 'Are you sure you want to submit?' This is your last chance!"

**[Screen: Click confirm]**

"Click 'Yes' or 'Confirm'."

**[Screen: Auto-submit warning]**

"IMPORTANT: When time expires, the exam auto-submits with whatever you've answered. Don't wait until the last second!"

**[Screen: Submission success]**

"You'll see a success message. Your exam is submitted!"

---

### SECTION 7: Viewing Results (10:30 - 11:30)

**[Screen: Results display]**

**Narrator:**
"The best part - instant results! Right after submission, you'll see:"

**[Screen: Result breakdown]**

"- Marks obtained
- Total marks
- Percentage
- Pass/Fail status (if applicable)"

**[Screen: Detailed results]**

"To see detailed results later, go to Exams → Online Exam Results and click on the exam name."

---

### CONCLUSION (11:30 - 12:00)

**[Screen: Success tips]**

**Narrator:**
"Remember:
✓ Check internet and battery before starting
✓ Enter the exam key correctly
✓ Read questions carefully
✓ Watch the timer
✓ Submit before time expires

You've got this! Good luck on your exams!"

**[Screen: Help resources]**

"Having issues? Check our troubleshooting guide linked below. Happy studying!"
