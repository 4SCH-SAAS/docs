# Online Exams - Teacher's Complete Guide

**Video Duration:** 12-15 minutes  
**Target Audience:** Teachers  
**Difficulty Level:** Beginner to Intermediate  
**Prerequisites:** Basic 4SCH login knowledge

---

## Video Objectives

By the end of this video, teachers will be able to:
1. Create an online exam from scratch
2. Build and manage a question bank
3. Add questions to exams (multiple methods)
4. Schedule and publish exams
5. Monitor student participation
6. View and analyze results

### SECTION 3: Creating Your First Exam (5:30 - 8:30)

**[Screen: Exams → Online Exams → Create Online Exam]**

**Narrator:**
"Now that we have questions, let's create an exam. Navigate to Online Exams and click 'Create Online Exam'."

**[Screen: Exam creation form]**

#### Filling Exam Details

**Title**
"Give your exam a clear, descriptive title. Instead of 'Test 1', use something specific like 'Mathematics Chapter 5: Quadratic Equations - Mid-Term Quiz'."

**Class & Section**
"Select your class - you'll only see classes you're assigned to teach. Choose 'Class 10'."

**[Screen: Section multi-select]**

"Now select sections. You can choose one or multiple. Let's select Section A and Section B."

**Subject**
"Choose the subject - Mathematics."

**Session Year**
"This is usually auto-selected to the current academic year."

**Start Date & Time**
"When should students be able to access this exam? Let me set it for next Monday, 10:00 AM."

**[Screen: Calendar picker demonstration]**

**End Date & Time**
"When's the deadline? I'll set Friday 5:00 PM. Students can attempt anytime between start and end."

"How long should students have? This is the actual test duration. For 20 questions, I'll give 40 minutes - that's 2 minutes per question."

**[Screen: Highlight duration vs. deadline difference]**

**Important distinction:**
"Notice the difference: Duration is how long they get ONCE they start. The date range is WHEN they can start. A student starting on Wednesday at 2 PM will have 40 minutes from 2 PM."

### SECTION 7: Best Practices & Tips (14:30 - 15:30)

**[Screen: Tips appearing as icons]**

**Narrator:**
"Before we wrap up, here are some pro tips for effective online exams:"

**Tip 1: Build Question Banks Early**
"Don't create questions the night before the exam. Build your bank throughout the semester as you teach each topic."

**Tip 2: Test Your Exam**
"Create a test exam with a few questions and attempt it yourself using a test student account. Verify everything works."

**Tip 3: Mix Difficulty Levels**
"Include easy questions to build confidence, medium for most students, and hard to challenge top performers."

**Tip 4: Time Generously**
"Students need more time than you think. Give 1.5 to 2 minutes per question, plus extra for reviewing."

**Tip 5: Share Exam Key at Exam Time**
"Don't share the exam key days in advance. Share it just before or at the start of the exam to prevent unauthorized access."

**Tip 6: Communicate Clearly**
"Announce exam rules clearly - one attempt only, no pausing, auto-submit on time expiry."

**Tip 7: Be Available**
"During the exam, be available via messaging or phone in case students face technical issues."

**Tip 8: Review and Improve**
"After each exam, note what worked and what didn't. Refine your question bank continuously."
