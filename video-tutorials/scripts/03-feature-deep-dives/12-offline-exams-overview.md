### SECTION 2: The Complete Workflow (2:30 - 5:00)

**[Screen: Animated timeline showing the journey]**

**Narrator:**
"Let's walk through the complete journey of an offline exam, from creation to results."

#### Phase 1: Exam Setup (Admin)

**[Screen: Admin creating exam]**

"**Step 1: Create Exam Structure**

An administrator creates the exam - let's say 'First Term Examination - March 2025' for Class 10. They set:
- Exam name
- Class
- Start date (when exams begin)
- End date (when exams finish)
- Publish status (initially set to 'No' - results hidden)"

**[Screen: Timetable creation interface]**

"**Step 2: Build the Timetable**

For each subject in the exam, the admin adds:
- Mathematics: March 15, 9 AM - 12 PM, 100 marks, passing 35
- English: March 17, 9 AM - 11:30 AM, 80 marks, passing 28
- Science: March 19, 9 AM - 12 PM, 100 marks, passing 35

And so on for all subjects."

**[Screen: Published timetable view]**

"Students can now see their exam schedule - time to start studying!"

#### Phase 2: Exam Conduct (School)

**[Screen: B-roll of exam hall, students writing]**

"**Step 3: Conduct the Exams**

On the scheduled dates, students take their exams the traditional way - pen, paper, exam hall. This happens outside the 4SCH system."

#### Phase 3: Evaluation (Teachers)

**[Screen: Teacher at desk with answer papers]**

"**Step 4: Teachers Evaluate Papers**

Teachers take the answer sheets home or to the staff room and grade them manually. This is where expertise and judgment come in - evaluating essays, checking calculations, assessing diagrams."

**[Screen: Mark entry interface]**

"**Step 5: Enter Marks into System**

Once evaluation is complete, teachers login to 4SCH and enter the marks. For each student:
- Roll number and name appear
- Teacher enters obtained marks
- System validates (can't exceed total marks)
- System calculates pass/fail automatically
- Teacher clicks Save"

**[Visual: Marks being entered, validation happening]**

"The system is smart - it won't let you enter 105 marks for a 100-mark exam!"

#### Phase 4: Results (Admin & Students)

**[Screen: Admin reviewing results]**

"**Step 6: Admin Reviews Results**

The admin checks that all subjects have marks entered, reviews for any anomalies, and verifies the data."

**[Screen: Publish button being clicked]**

"**Step 7: Publish Results**

When everything looks good, the admin clicks 'Publish Results.' Instantly, results become visible to students and parents."

**[Screen: Student viewing results on phone]**

"**Step 8: Students View Results**

Students receive notification, login to their app or web portal, and view their performance - overall percentage, grade, subject-wise breakdown, pass or fail status."

**[Screen: Happy student showing results to parent]**

"Parents can monitor their child's performance through the parent portal."

- Automatic pass/fail calculation

#### Automatic Calculation

**[Screen: Calculation animation]**

"The system does the math automatically:

For each student:
1. Subject percentage = (Obtained / Total) × 100
2. Subject grade = Based on percentage
3. Subject pass/fail = Obtained >= Passing marks

Overall:
1. Total obtained = Sum of all subjects
2. Overall percentage = (Total obtained / Total possible) × 100
3. Overall grade = Based on percentage
4. Overall status = Pass if ALL subjects passed, else Fail"

**[Screen: Example calculation]**

"Example: Chukwudi scored 75 in Math (100), 65 in English (80), 80 in Science (100), and 55 in Social (80).
Total: 275 out of 360 = 76.39% = Grade A = Pass"

#### Publication Control

**[Screen: Publish/Unpublish toggle]**

"Administrators control when results go live. Need to make corrections? Unpublish, edit marks, and republish. Complete control!"

- Multiple choice questions
