# Video Script: Holidays & School Calendar

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Target Audience:** School Admins responsible for the academic calendar
**Learning Objectives:**
- Add, edit, and remove holidays for the current session year
- Understand the three guard-rails 4SCH applies to prevent calendar mistakes
- Know how holidays ripple through Staff Attendance, Student Attendance, Timetable, Reports, and Notifications
- Adopt a sensible workflow that adds the whole year's holidays in one sitting

## [00:50-02:00] Adding a Holiday (70 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Click **Add Holiday** → form slides open with two fields:
  - **Date** (date picker)
  - **Title** (text)
- Pick 14 Apr (a date in current session year) → type "Good Friday" → click **Save**

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"To add a holiday, click **Add Holiday** at the top of the page. The form has just two fields:

- **Date** — the day of the holiday. It must fall within the current session year.
- **Title** — a short, clear name like 'Good Friday' or 'Founder's Day'. This appears on the calendar and on student and parent dashboards.

Click **Save**, and the holiday is created instantly — visible immediately on every calendar view across the platform."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**

## [02:00-03:00] Guard-Rails (60 sec)

**[VISUAL]** Three split-screen scenarios:

**Scenario 1: Date outside session year**
- Pick a date in the next academic year → click Save
- Red error appears: "The selected date must fall within the current session year"

**Scenario 2: Date with existing staff attendance**
- Pick today (which already has attendance saved)
- Red error: "Cannot add holiday on a date with existing staff attendance records"

**Scenario 3: Date with approved leave**
- Pick a date where 3 staff are already on approved leave
- Yellow warning: "3 staff have approved leave for this date. Continue?"
- **Continue** button (blue) and **Cancel** (grey)

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"4SCH protects you from common mistakes with three guard-rails:

If you try to add a holiday on a date outside the current session year, you'll see a clear error message — either change the date, or extend the session year in Settings.

If staff attendance has already been recorded for the date you're trying to declare a holiday, 4SCH blocks the add. You can't retroactively turn a normal working day into a holiday. The fix is to first delete the attendance entries for that date, then add the holiday.

If approved leave already exists for that date, 4SCH warns you with the staff count but lets you proceed. This is useful for shared holidays that overlap with planned leave — the warning gives you a chance to coordinate with the affected staff first."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Outside session year → blocked"
- "Existing attendance → blocked"
- "Approved leave → warning, you can proceed"

## [03:00-03:45] Editing and Deleting (45 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Holiday list → hover a row → edit and delete icons appear on the right
- Click **edit** → form opens pre-filled → change date from 14 Apr to 15 Apr → Save → row updates
- Hover a different row → click **delete** → confirmation modal → Confirm → row disappears

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"To edit a holiday, hover the row and click the small edit icon. Change the date or title, and click Save. The same guard-rails apply.

To delete a holiday, hover the row and click the delete icon. Confirm, and the date returns to being a normal working day.

A practical tip: if a public holiday moves to a different day, **edit the existing holiday's date** rather than deleting and re-creating it. Editing preserves any calendar references already in flight, like upcoming notifications."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Edit · Delete · Both respect the guard-rails"
- "Tip: edit → don't delete + recreate"

---

## [03:45-04:30] How Holidays Affect Other Modules (45 sec)

**[VISUAL]** A short montage:
- Staff Attendance page → 25 Dec shows a grey "Holiday" pill instead of buttons
- Student Attendance page → 25 Dec shows "Holiday" instead of the attendance form
- Timetable → 25 Dec periods are visually marked
- Reports → "School Days" total excludes holiday count
- Notification preview: "Upcoming Holiday — Christmas Day (Wed 25 Dec)" arriving 2 days before on a parent's phone

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"Holidays don't sit alone — they ripple across the platform.

On the **Staff Attendance** page, the holiday date is marked Holiday and skipped from the expected-headcount calculation.

On the **Student Attendance** page, the date is not eligible for attendance entry. Teachers see Holiday instead of the attendance form.

On the **Timetable**, periods on a holiday date are visually marked so teachers and students know not to expect class.

In **Reports & Analytics**, holiday days are excluded from School Days totals, so attendance percentages remain fair.

And if you turn on the **Upcoming Holiday reminder template** in Settings, parents and staff get an in-app and email reminder one or two days before each holiday."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Holidays ripple through 5 modules automatically"
- "Reminders configurable in Settings → Notification Templates"

---

## [04:30-05:00] Wrap-up & Practical Tips (30 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Three tip cards:
  - 🗓️ **Add the full year in one sitting** at the start of each session
  - 🕐 **Half-day breaks ≠ holidays** — adjust the timetable instead
  - 📨 **Coordinate with the accountant** if a holiday falls in unpaid leave
- 4SCH logo + outro music

**[VOICEOVER]:**
"Three quick tips to wrap up.

One — add every known holiday at the start of each session year. Doing it in one sitting is much faster than reacting to dates as they approach.

Two — if a 'half day' is genuinely a working day with classes ending early, don't add it as a holiday — adjust the timetable for that day instead. Holidays in 4SCH are full-day events.

Three — if a holiday falls in the middle of a staff member's unpaid leave, the day is still counted as a holiday and doesn't reduce payroll. The Holidays plus Staff Attendance plus Payroll integration takes care of this automatically — see the **Staff Attendance & Leave** video for the full picture."

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
- "Next: Staff Attendance & Leave"
