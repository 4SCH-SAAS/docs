**[VISUAL]**

  - Late arrival analysis: students frequently arriving late, time patterns

  - Assignment submission rate per class (bar chart)
  - Online platform usage (logins, active hours, features used)
  - Communication participation (message count per teacher, parent response rate)
  - Exam participation and online submission
  - Study material downloads
- Generate custom attendance report:
  - Filter by class, date range, student
  - Compare periods (this month vs last month)
  - Identify patterns (e.g., Mondays have higher absence)

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Attendance Analytics track student engagement with school. The system shows overall attendance rate, trends over time, and which classes need improvement.

See attendance by status: how many showed up, how many were absent, how many arrived late. When absences spike, you can investigate the cause - was there illness? Holiday? Construction?

The Chronic Absentees list identifies students regularly missing school. This early warning system helps intervene before students fall too far behind academically. You can contact families, provide support, or adjust their care plan.

Late arrival patterns tell a story. If many students arrive late on Mondays, maybe there's a transportation issue. Track this systematically rather than relying on general impressions.

Beyond attendance, track engagement with the platform itself. Assignment submission rates show which classes and students actively participate in learning activities. Download and use of study materials correlates with academic performance.

Communication metrics reveal parent involvement. If parents aren't reading messages or responding to teachers, that's valuable information for improving parent engagement strategies.

Export detailed attendance records for compliance - many regulatory bodies require documented attendance proof. 4SCH provides comprehensive, auditable records."

**[VISUAL]**
- Financial Dashboard showing:

  - Date range selector
  - Fee type filter
  - Payment method filter
  - Student/class filter
  - Visualization options
- Export options:
  - PDF for printing/sharing with board
  - Excel for further analysis
  - CSV for accounting software integration

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Financial Analytics show how money flows through your school. Track total collections, payment methods used, and trends over time.

The system shows which payment methods parents prefer. If online payments are trending up, that's useful information for promoting digital payment options. It increases convenience and reduces handling of cash.

Outstanding fees list tells you exactly who owes money and for how long. You can prioritize follow-up: someone three months behind needs immediate attention, while someone five days late might just need a reminder.

Collection rate by class reveals which sections have payment challenges. Class 4 might have lower collections due to a higher proportion of financially struggling families - useful context for planning support or discussing affordability with families.

The system projects future collections based on current trends. If you're on track to exceed targets, great. If you're falling short, you have time to implement strategies like fee reminders, payment plans, or scholarship adjustments.

Financial reports are essential for administrative and board discussions. Export to PDF for board meetings or Excel for accountants. The system integrates with accounting software through CSV export, eliminating duplicate data entry."

## [06:30-08:15] Teacher & Staff Performance Metrics (105 sec)

**[VISUAL]**
- Teacher Performance Dashboard showing:
  - Teacher list with key metrics:
    - Subjects taught
    - Classes assigned
    - Student count
    - Assignment submission rate (% of assigned assignments with student submissions)
    - Average marks given (shows if a teacher grades harder or easier)
    - Student attendance in their classes
    - Communication frequency (messages sent, response time to parent messages)
    - Online activity (logins, lesson uploads, engagement with platform)
  - Comparison tools: this teacher vs. department average vs. school average
  - Performance trends over time (assignment submission rate chart showing improvement)
- Staff productivity metrics:
  - Administrative staff: ticket resolution time, user management efficiency
  - Transportation staff: trip completion rate, safety incident reports
  - Accounting staff: fee processing time, reconciliation accuracy
- Recognition opportunities:
  - Highlight high performers
  - Identify support needs for struggling staff
  - Plan professional development based on gaps

**[VOICEOVER]:**  
"Teacher analytics provide insights into classroom effectiveness. Track how many assignments each teacher creates and how many students submit them. High submission rates indicate engaged students and clear expectations.

Compare teachers' grading patterns. This isn't to single anyone out, but to ensure consistency. If one teacher gives mostly A's and another mostly C's for similar work, it suggests different standards. Discuss to ensure fairness.

Monitor how often teachers use the platform - logging in regularly, uploading materials, communicating with parents. Consistent engagement correlates with better student outcomes.

Track student attendance in specific teachers' classes. Some teachers have excellent attendance while students skip others' classes. This data-driven feedback helps teachers understand their impact and adjust engagement strategies.

Response times to parent messages matter. Teachers who respond within hours build stronger relationships with families. Teachers slow to respond might need training on time management or communication expectations.

Use this data positively. Recognize high-performing teachers, provide support to those struggling, and plan professional development based on identified gaps. Teachers appreciate data-driven feedback and opportunities for growth."

Pre-built templates answer common questions: 'Which students need intervention?' showing those with multiple risk factors. Run these templates regularly to catch problems early.

These custom reports are your secret weapon for data-driven decision-making. Instead of guessing at problems, you have facts. Instead of hoping things improve, you measure and adjust."
