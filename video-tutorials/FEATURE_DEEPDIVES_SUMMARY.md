## Technical Specifications

### Video Requirements
- Resolution: 1080p minimum (1440p preferred)
- Frame rate: 30fps
- Codec: H.264 (MP4)
- Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps

### Audio Requirements
- Sample rate: 48 kHz
- Bit depth: 16-bit
- Bitrate: 192 kbps
- Format: AAC
- Quality: Professional, noise-free

### Caption Requirements
- Format: SRT or VTT
- Sync: Perfect match with voiceover
- Accuracy: 100% verbatim
- Readability: 17-20 chars/line

### Thumbnail Requirements
- Dimensions: 1280×720 px
- Format: PNG or JPG
- Text: Bold, readable
- Consistency: Unified design
