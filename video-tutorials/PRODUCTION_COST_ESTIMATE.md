## 💰 Detailed Cost Breakdown

### 1. Pre-Production ($3,500 - $6,000)

#### Script Review & Refinement
- **Task:** Review 21 scripts with stakeholders, incorporate feedback
- **Time:** 40 hours @ $50-75/hr
- **Cost:** $2,000 - $3,000

#### Storyboarding
- **Task:** Visual planning for complex sequences (5-7 videos need detailed storyboards)
- **Time:** 20 hours @ $50/hr
- **Cost:** $1,000 - $1,500

#### Demo Environment Setup
- **Task:** Create clean test database with sample data for all roles
- **Time:** 20 hours @ $50-75/hr
- **Cost:** $1,000 - $1,500

---

### 2. Voiceover ($4,200 - $9,240)

#### Professional Voice Artist
- **Rate:** $200-400 per finished hour (industry standard)
- **Total Duration:** 231 minutes = 3.85 hours finished audio
- **Recording Time:** ~12-15 hours (includes retakes, direction)
- **Cost:** $3,500 - $7,500

#### Audio Editing & Mastering
- **Task:** Noise reduction, normalization, timing adjustments
- **Time:** 15 hours @ $50-75/hr
- **Cost:** $750 - $1,500

#### Studio Time (if needed)
- **Rate:** $100-150/hour for professional studio
- **Time:** 8-10 hours
- **Cost:** $800 - $1,500
- **Alternative:** Remote recording (included in artist rate)

**Recommended:** Hire experienced e-learning voice artist with home studio ($200-250/hr finished)

---

### 3. Video Production ($8,400 - $21,000)

#### Screen Recording
- **Task:** Record all screen sequences, demonstrations across devices
- **Time:** 60 hours @ $60-80/hr
- **Cost:** $3,600 - $4,800

#### Video Editing
- **Task:** Cut, transitions, timing, on-screen text, annotations
- **Time:** 80 hours @ $60-100/hr (shorter videos = 2-3 hrs each, longer = 4-6 hrs)
- **Cost:** $4,800 - $8,000

#### Motion Graphics & Animations
- **Task:** Intro/outro animations, callout boxes, transitions, icons
- **Time:** 40 hours @ $75-120/hr
- **Cost:** $3,000 - $4,800

#### Color Grading & Final Polish
- **Task:** Consistent look, brand colors, final QA
- **Time:** 20 hours @ $50-75/hr
- **Cost:** $1,000 - $1,500

#### Revisions (2 rounds per video)
- **Task:** Incorporate feedback, make changes
- **Time:** Included in editing hours above

**Tools Needed:**
- Adobe Premiere Pro or Final Cut Pro (subscription: $50/month)
- After Effects for animations (subscription: $50/month)
- Screen recording software like Camtasia ($300 one-time) or ScreenFlow ($130)

## 💵 Cost-Saving Options

### Budget-Friendly Approach ($15,000 - $20,000)

1. **Use Freelancers from Fiverr/Upwork** - Nigerian talent often 30-50% lower rates
2. **AI Voiceover** - ElevenLabs or Murf ($30/month) instead of professional ($4,000 savings)
3. **Template-Based Editing** - Use Camtasia templates, less custom animation ($2,000 savings)
4. **Auto-Generated Captions** - YouTube auto-captions with manual review ($1,000 savings)
5. **In-House Project Management** - Use existing team member ($2,000 savings)
6. **Royalty-Free Music** - YouTube Audio Library (free) ($500 savings)

**Budget Total:** ~$16,000 - $22,000

---

### Premium Approach ($45,000 - $60,000)

1. **Celebrity/Recognizable Voice** - Nigerian educator or influencer
2. **Full Animation Studio** - Custom motion graphics for all videos
3. **Multiple Languages** - Add Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo versions
4. **Professional Actors** - On-camera segments for intros
5. **Marketing Campaign** - Paid promotion, influencer partnerships
