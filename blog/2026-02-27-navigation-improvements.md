## 🎯 What's New

### Improved Main Navigation

We've restructured the main navigation bar to help you find what you need faster:

#### 1. **"Blog" is now "What's New"**
The old "Blog" tab has been renamed to **"What's New"** to better reflect its purpose—keeping you updated on new features, improvements, and important announcements.

#### 2. **New "Guides" Dropdown Menu**
Access role-specific documentation instantly with our new Guides dropdown:
- 🎓 **For School Admins** - School setup, management, and administration
- 👥 **For Teachers** - Class management, assignments, and grading
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **For Parents** - Monitor your child's progress and pay fees
- 📚 **For Students** - Access assignments, timetables, and results
- 💼 **For Support Staff** - Non-teaching staff workflows
- 🚌 **For Drivers** - Transportation management
- 💰 **For Accountants** - Financial management and reporting
- 📖 **All Documentation** - Complete documentation home

#### 3. **Quick Support Access**
A new **"Support"** link in the main navigation takes you directly to our FAQ and help resources.

#### 4. **Register Your School**
New schools can now easily find the registration link prominently displayed in the top navigation.

### Improved Contact & Support Page

The [Contact Support](/support/contact-support) page has been completely revamped with:

- **Multiple Contact Channels** - Email, phone, WhatsApp, and social media
- **Role-Based Support** - Different instructions for admins vs. teachers/parents/students
- **Response Time SLAs** - Know when to expect help based on issue priority
- **Before You Contact** - Checklist to help us resolve your issue faster
- **Self-Service Resources** - Links to FAQ, troubleshooting, and guides

### School Registration Process

New schools can now find detailed registration instructions in the [Getting Started](/intro/getting-started) guide:

1. Visit [www.4sch.com](https://www.4sch.com)
2. Register your school
3. Check your email (including spam folder)
4. Wait for our team to review and reach out

## 🎨 Why These Changes?

- **Better Discoverability** - Role-based guides help you find relevant documentation instantly
- **Clear Labeling** - "What's New" accurately describes release notes and updates
- **Improved Support** - Multiple pathways to get help when you need it
- **Modern Communication** - Social media and WhatsApp for faster engagement
- **Growth Focus** - Prominent school registration encourages new signups
