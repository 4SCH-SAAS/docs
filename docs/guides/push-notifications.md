# Push Notifications & Notification Box

This guide explains the notification system in 4SCH — what notifications you'll
receive, how to manage them, and how to ensure push notifications are working
on your device.

> **Audience:** All users (Students, Parents, Teachers, School Admins).

---

## What Are Notifications?

4SCH uses two types of notifications to keep you informed:

### 1. In-App Notifications (Notification Box)
- Live in a **Notification Box** accessible from a bell icon in the apps
- Persistent — they stay until you delete them
- Show full title and message
- Tap to navigate to the relevant page

### 2. Push Notifications
- Pop up on your device's lock screen / notification tray
- Even if the app is closed
- Tap to open the app at the relevant page
- Need to be enabled in your device settings

You'll typically receive **both** for important events.

## What Notifications You'll Receive

Notifications cover key school events. What you receive depends on your role:

### Students
- 📚 Exam timetable published
- 📊 Results published (with grades and CA breakdown)
- 📝 New assignment created
- 📢 School announcement
- 💰 Fee invoice generated (if applicable)
- 📅 Event reminder

### Parents/Guardians
Everything students get, plus:

- 👨‍👩‍👧 Child-specific notifications (attendance, behaviour, etc.)
- 💳 Fee payment reminders for your children

### Teachers
- 📋 New leave request to approve
- ⏰ Timetable changes affecting you
- 📈 Result publishing tasks
- 📢 Admin announcements

### School Admins
Everything teachers get, plus:

- 💼 Subscription invoice generated
- ⚠️ Subscription expiring soon
- 🔔 System alerts

## Finding Your Notification Box

### Mobile App (Parent/Student)
- Open the **Parent Student** app
- On the **home screen**, look for the 🔔 **bell icon** in the top-right
- Tap it to open the Notification Box

### Mobile App (Staff Teacher)
- Open the **Staff Teacher** app
- The bell icon is in the **header** of the home screen
- Tap to open notifications

### Web (Student Portal)
- Login at your school's student portal
- Click **Notifications** in the left sidebar

### Web (School Admin)
- Login to the admin panel
- Click the notification bell icon in the top navigation

## Managing Your Notification Box

You can manage individual notifications and bulk actions.

### Marking as Read

- **Tap a notification** in the list — it's marked as read automatically
- Read notifications appear lighter/greyed out
- Unread notifications appear bolder

### Mark All Read

- Look for **"Mark all read"** button at the top of the notification box
- Tap to mark every notification as read at once

### Deleting Notifications

- **Mobile:** Swipe left on a notification → tap **Delete**
- **Web:** Click the **×** button next to a notification

### Clear All

- Tap **"Clear all"** at the top to delete all your notifications at once
- Some apps offer **"Clear read only"** to keep unread ones

> **Important:** Deletion is per-user. If your child also received the same
> notification, they still have it in their notification box.

---

## Auto-Cleanup

To keep your notification box manageable, 4SCH automatically deletes:

- Notifications you've **manually deleted**
- Notifications **older than 90 days** (even if you didn't delete them)

This happens automatically — you don't need to do anything.

## Enabling Push Notifications on Your Device

For push notifications to reach your device's lock screen / notification tray,
they must be enabled in your device settings.

### iOS (iPhone/iPad)

1. Open **Settings**
2. Scroll to **Parent Student** (or Staff Teacher) app
3. Tap **Notifications**
4. Enable **Allow Notifications**
5. Choose alert style (Banner, Alert, etc.)
6. Enable **Sounds** and **Badges** if desired

### Android

1. Open **Settings → Apps**
2. Find **Parent Student** (or Staff Teacher)
3. Tap **Notifications**
4. Enable **Allow notifications**
5. Configure individual notification categories if needed

### First Launch

When you first open the 4SCH mobile app:

- The app asks for notification permission
- Tap **Allow** to enable push notifications

If you accidentally tapped Deny, follow the steps above to enable.

---

## Push Notifications on Web

Web browsers can also show push notifications:

### Chrome / Edge / Firefox

1. When asked, tap **Allow** when the browser prompts
2. To check / re-enable:
   - Click the lock icon in the address bar
   - Find **Notifications** → set to **Allow**

### Safari (macOS)

1. Safari → **Preferences → Websites → Notifications**
2. Find your school's URL → set to **Allow**

## Troubleshooting

### I'm not receiving push notifications

**Check 1:** Device notifications enabled?
- Follow the steps above for iOS / Android / browser

**Check 2:** App in background?
- iOS may pause notifications if app is closed for long periods. Open it occasionally to refresh.

**Check 3:** Internet connection?
- Push notifications require internet. Check Wi-Fi or mobile data.

**Check 4:** Battery saver / Do Not Disturb?
- Battery savers may delay notifications. Whitelist 4SCH apps in your battery settings.
- Check your phone isn't in Do Not Disturb mode.

**Check 5:** Notification permissions revoked?
- iOS / Android sometimes reset permissions after an update. Re-enable them.

### Notification box is empty

**Check 1:** Refresh the page
- Mobile: Pull down to refresh
- Web: Press F5 / Refresh button

**Check 2:** Are you logged in as the right person?
- Notifications are per-user — make sure you're logged in as the intended user.

**Check 3:** Is the event recent?
- If you haven't received any school events recently, the box may genuinely be empty.

### I tapped a notification but it doesn't open the right page

**Check 1:** App version
- Update to the latest version of the app — older versions may not support deep linking.

**Check 2:** Already logged out?
- The app may have logged you out. Log back in, then tap the notification again.

### I'm missing notifications others received

**Check 1:** Your role and child links
- Notifications are role-specific. Parents only get notifications for their linked children.
- If you have multiple children, verify all are linked to your account.

**Check 2:** Subscription notifications (school admins)
- Subscription-related notifications go only to school administrators.

### My child got the notification but I didn't (or vice versa)

This is a **known good behaviour**! Each user receives their own copy of
notifications. If your child deletes their notification, it doesn't delete
yours. Similarly, if you delete yours, theirs still appears.

## Notification Frequency

You don't get spammed:

- Events you trigger yourself (e.g., admin publishing a result) don't notify you
- Bulk actions create one notification per recipient (not one per recipient × event)
- Auto-cleanup keeps the notification box tidy

If you find any notification too frequent, ask your school admin to review
the school's notification settings.

---

## Privacy & Data

Notifications:

- Are sent only to users authorised to receive them
- Don't contain sensitive data in the title/body (just enough to identify the event)
- Are stored in your school's database (multi-tenant isolation)
- Are not shared with third parties
