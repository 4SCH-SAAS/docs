Welcome! This guide is for non-teaching staff members who support school operations. Whether you're an admission officer, secretary, front desk staff, or other support role, you'll find instructions for your daily tasks here.

## Common Tasks for Non-Teaching Staff

### Managing Visitors

If you work at the front desk, you may need to register visitors.

**How to Register a Visitor:**
1. Go to **Visitors** or **Front Desk** (if available)
2. Click **Register Visitor**
3. Fill in:
   - Visitor's Name
   - Purpose of Visit (Meeting with Principal, Parent Inquiry, Vendor, etc.)
   - Person to Meet
   - Time In
   - ID Type and Number (for security)
   - Phone Number
4. Click **Save**

**When Visitor Leaves:**
1. Find the visitor in the list
2. Click **Check Out**
3. Enter Time Out
4. Click **Save**

**Benefits:**
- Security tracking
- Visitor history for reference
- Reports on school visitors

### Handling Admissions & Inquiries

If you're an admission officer or handle inquiries, here's your workflow.

#### Recording an Inquiry

When a parent calls or visits asking about admission:

**Step 1: Record the Inquiry**
1. Go to **Admissions** → **Inquiries**
2. Click **New Inquiry**
3. Fill in:
   - Parent/Guardian Name
   - Phone Number
   - Email Address
   - Child's Name
   - Child's Age
   - Desired Class
   - Current School (if transferring)
   - How they heard about your school
4. Click **Save**

**Step 2: Follow Up**
- The system can remind you to follow up
- Call or message parent with information
- Schedule school tour if requested
- Send admission forms

#### Processing a New Admission

When a student is accepted:

**Step 1: Collect Documents**
- ✅ Completed admission form
- ✅ Birth certificate
- ✅ Previous school report cards
- ✅ Transfer certificate (if applicable)
- ✅ Passport photos
- ✅ Parent IDs
- ✅ Medical records

**Step 2: Verify Information**
- Check all required documents are provided
- Verify information is consistent
- Ensure previous school records are authentic

**Step 3: Create Student Profile**
1. Go to **Students** → **Add Student**
2. Fill in all required information:
   - Personal details
   - Class assignment
   - Parent/guardian information
   - Emergency contacts
   - Medical information
3. Click **Save**

**Step 4: Generate Admission Number**
- System auto-generates or you assign manually
- Format example: "2024/0456"
- Give to parent for reference

**Step 5: Upload Documents**
1. Go to student profile → **Documents**
2. Upload scanned copies:
   - Birth certificate
   - Photos
   - Medical records
   - Previous reports
3. Label each document clearly

**Step 6: Generate Invoice**
1. Work with accounts office or admin
2. Generate fee invoice for new student
3. Include:
   - Admission/Registration fee
   - First term tuition
   - Uniform (if applicable)
   - Books (if applicable)

**Step 7: Issue Welcome Pack**
- Admission letter
- Student handbook
- Timetable
- School calendar
- Parent login credentials for 4SCH
- Uniform requirements
- Book list

:::tip Keep Track
Use the **Admissions Pipeline** feature (if available) to track inquiries from initial contact → admission test → document submission → enrollment. This helps you follow up and not lose potential students.
:::

### Managing Student Records

You may be responsible for keeping student records up-to-date.

#### Updating Student Information

**When to Update:**
- Parent changes phone number or address
- Student has medical issue to record
- Parent requests update to emergency contact
- Name correction

**How to Update:**
1. Go to **Students** → Find the student
2. Click on student name
3. Click **Edit**
4. Update the necessary information
5. Click **Save**

**Important Changes Requiring Admin Approval:**
- Class change
- Date of birth
- Admission number
- Withdrawal/Transfer

For these, inform your admin before making changes.

#### Adding Documents to Student Files

**Common Documents:**
- Birth certificate (at admission)
- Medical reports
- School letters (suspension, commendation, etc.)
- Competition certificates
- Transfer certificates

**How to Upload:**
1. Open student profile
2. Go to **Documents** tab
3. Click **Upload Document**
4. Select file
5. Add description: "Medical Report - January 2024"
6. Click **Upload**

**Organizing Documents:**
- Use clear, consistent naming
- Include dates in descriptions
- Separate by category (Medical, Academic, Administrative)

### Communication Tasks

#### Sending School Messages

You may send messages on behalf of the school.

**Common Messages:**
- Fee payment reminders
- Event invitations
- Document collection notices
- General announcements

**How to Send a Message:**
1. Go to **Messaging** → **New Message**
2. Select **Recipients:**
   - All Parents
   - Specific Class
   - Individual Parents
3. Write your message clearly
4. Attach files if needed (PDF, images)
5. Click **Send**

**Sample Messages:**

**Fee Reminder:**
> "Dear Parents,
>
> This is a reminder that Second Term fees are due by January 31st. Please make payment at the school office or online via your 4SCH portal.
>
> For inquiries, contact the Accounts Office.
>
> Thank you."

**Document Collection:**
> "Dear Parents of JSS 3 Students,
>
> WAEC registration is ongoing. Please submit the following to the school office by Friday:
> - 2 passport photographs
> - Birth certificate (photocopy)
>
> Office hours: 8am - 3pm, Monday to Friday.
>
> Thank you."

#### Responding to Parent Inquiries

**When Parents Contact the School:**

**Common Questions:**
- "What are your school fees?"
- "Do you have space in JSS 2?"
- "How do I pay fees online?"
- "Can I schedule a tour?"

**How to Respond:**
1. Check **Messages** or **Inbox**
2. Reply promptly (within 24 hours)
3. Be professional and friendly
4. Provide accurate information
5. Escalate to admin if you don't know the answer

**Response Example:**
> "Dear Mrs. Okafor,
>
> Thank you for your interest in our school. We currently have space available in JSS 2.
>
> School fees for JSS classes are:
> - Tuition: ₦75,000 per term
> - Development Levy: ₦15,000 per term
> - Other fees: ₦10,000 per term
> Total: ₦100,000 per term
>
> We would be happy to schedule a tour for you. Please let us know your preferred date and time.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]
> [School Name] Admissions Office"

### Generating Reports

Depending on your role, you may need to generate various reports.

**Common Reports:**
- Student enrollment summary
- Admission statistics
- Visitor log
- Daily attendance summary
- Outstanding fees list (if you assist with fees)

**How to Generate a Report:**
1. Go to **Reports**
2. Select the report type
3. Choose filters:
   - Date range
   - Specific classes
   - Other criteria
4. Click **Generate**
5. View online or download (PDF/Excel)

**Using Reports Effectively:**

**Enrollment Report:**
- Track enrollment trends
- Identify classes near capacity
- Plan for next term admissions

**Admission Statistics:**
- How many inquiries converted to admissions?
- Which marketing channels work best?
- What's the typical timeline from inquiry to enrollment?

**Daily Attendance:**
- Print for admin review
- Identify patterns (low attendance days)
- Follow up on chronic absenteeism

## Role-Specific Tasks

### Front Desk / Receptionist

**Your Key Responsibilities:**
- Welcome visitors
- Answer phone calls
- Direct inquiries to appropriate staff
- Register visitors
- Manage appointment scheduling
- Handle walk-in inquiries

**Daily Routine:**

**Morning (8:00 AM - 12:00 PM):**
1. ✅ Check messages and emails
2. ✅ Review scheduled appointments
3. ✅ Greet students and staff arriving
4. ✅ Handle parent inquiries
5. ✅ Register any visitors
6. ✅ Answer phone calls promptly

**Afternoon (12:00 PM - 4:00 PM):**
1. ✅ Continue managing visitors
2. ✅ Process admission inquiries
3. ✅ Update visitor log
4. ✅ Coordinate with admin on urgent matters
5. ✅ Prepare end-of-day report (if required)

**Communication Tips:**
- Answer phone: "Good morning, [School Name], how may I help you?"
- Be warm and professional
- Get names and contact info for follow-up
- Don't promise what you can't deliver (e.g., "The principal will definitely call you back")
- Take detailed messages for staff members

## Best Practices for Non-Teaching Staff

### Professionalism

**Always:**
- ✅ Dress appropriately (follow school dress code)
- ✅ Arrive on time
- ✅ Maintain a positive attitude
- ✅ Be courteous to everyone (parents, students, visitors, colleagues)
- ✅ Keep your workspace organized

**Never:**
- ❌ Share confidential student or staff information
- ❌ Gossip about students, parents, or colleagues
- ❌ Use your phone excessively for personal matters
- ❌ Be rude or dismissive, even if stressed

### Confidentiality

**Information You Must Keep Confidential:**
- Student grades and performance
- Disciplinary records
- Medical information
- Family situations
- Financial information
- Staff personal matters

**If Parents Ask About Other Students:**
> "I'm sorry, but I can't share information about other students. Please speak with the class teacher or principal if you have concerns."

### Time Management

**Prioritizing Tasks:**

**Urgent & Important:**
- Handle immediately (emergency calls, urgent admin requests)

**Important but Not Urgent:**
- Schedule dedicated time (reports, follow-ups)

**Urgent but Not Important:**
- Delegate if possible or handle quickly

**Neither Urgent nor Important:**
- Do when you have free time or eliminate

**Use To-Do Lists:**
- Daily task list
- Weekly goals
- Monthly projects
- Check off completed items

### Customer Service Excellence

**The First Impression:**
- You're often the first contact parents have with the school
- Your professionalism reflects on the entire school
- A warm welcome can make a huge difference

**Dealing with Difficult Parents:**

**If a Parent is Upset:**
1. Listen without interrupting
2. Stay calm and professional
3. Acknowledge their feelings: "I understand you're frustrated"
4. Explain what you can do to help
5. If you can't resolve it, involve admin: "Let me get the principal for you"
6. Never argue or take it personally

**Sample Response:**
> "I understand your concern, Mr. Okafor. Let me see how I can help. [Pause to check/research] I need to consult with the principal on this matter. May I have them call you back within the hour?"

## Technology Tips

### Using 4SCH Efficiently

**Keyboard Shortcuts** (if available):
- Learn common shortcuts to work faster
- Ask your admin for a shortcut reference guide

**Browser Tips:**
- Bookmark frequently used pages
- Keep multiple tabs open for different tasks
- Clear cache if system seems slow

**Mobile Access:**
- Download 4SCH mobile app (if available)
- Check messages even when away from desk
- Mark urgent items for follow-up

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

**"I can't log in"**
1. Check your username and password
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try a different browser
4. Contact admin to reset password

**"Page is loading slowly"**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Close unnecessary browser tabs
3. Clear browser cache
4. Restart your computer
5. Report to admin if problem persists

**"I don't see the menu I need"**
- Your role may not have access to that feature
- Contact admin to request access if needed
