## <span className="heading-icon"><DashboardIcon size={18} /></span> Your Parent Dashboard {#parent-dashboard}

When you log into 4SCH, you'll see your **Parent Dashboard**—your home base for tracking your child's education.

- Recent attendance summary
- Latest grades and test scores
- Outstanding fees and payment status

## <span className="heading-icon"><AttendanceIcon size={18} /></span> Checking Your Child's Attendance {#checking-attendance}

One of the most important things you can monitor is whether your child is attending school regularly.

### How to Check Attendance

**Option 1: From Your Dashboard**
- Look at the attendance summary on your child's card
- You'll see today's status and overall attendance percentage

**Option 2: Full Attendance Report**
1. Click on your child's name
2. Go to the **Attendance** tab
3. You'll see a calendar or list showing:
   - ✅ **Present** — Attended that day
   - ❌ **Absent** — Did not attend
   - ⏰ **Late** — Arrived late
   - 🏥 **Excused** — Absent with permission

### Understanding Attendance Notifications

**You'll receive notifications when:**
- Your child is marked absent
- Your child arrives late to school
- Attendance drops below a certain percentage (if configured)

**Notification methods:**
- Push notification to your phone (if app is installed)
- SMS message
- Email
- In-app notification

:::note What to Do If Attendance Is Wrong
If your child attended but is marked absent:
1. Check with your child first—were they in class on time?
2. Message the class teacher explaining the situation
3. The teacher or admin can correct the record

Always address attendance issues quickly to avoid misunderstandings.
:::

### Why Attendance Matters

Schools often have minimum attendance requirements:
- Usually 75-80% attendance required
- Poor attendance affects exam eligibility
- Consistent absences may trigger school intervention

**Track your child's attendance percentage** shown on their profile. If it drops below 85%, investigate why and work with the school to improve it.

## <span className="heading-icon"><GradesIcon size={18} /></span> Viewing Grades & Results {#viewing-grades}

Stay informed about your child's academic performance.

### How to Check Grades

1. Click on your child's name from your dashboard
2. Go to the **Grades** or **Results** tab
3. Select the term/semester you want to view

### What You'll See

**Recent Test Scores:**
- Individual test and quiz results
- Subject-by-subject breakdown
- Percentage or letter grade
- Teacher comments (if provided)

**Overall Performance:**
- Average grade per subject
- Class rank or position (if school publishes this)
- Comparison to previous terms
- Areas of strength and weakness

**Report Cards:**
- Full term report cards
- Download as PDF for printing
- Includes attendance summary and teacher remarks

### Understanding the Grading System

Your school uses a specific grading system. Common systems include:

**Percentage-Based:**
- 70-100% = A (Excellent)
- 60-69% = B (Very Good)
- 50-59% = C (Good)
- 40-49% = D (Pass)
- Below 40% = F (Fail)

**Or your school may use a different scale.** Check with the school for their specific grading criteria.

### When Are Grades Posted?

Teachers publish grades after:
- Each test or assessment
- Midterm exams
- End of term exams

**You'll receive a notification** when new grades are published.

### What to Do If You're Concerned About Grades

**If grades are lower than expected:**
1. **Talk to your child first** — Are they understanding the material? Do they need help?
2. **Review the specific tests** — Which subjects or topics are problematic?
3. **Message the teacher** — Ask for specific feedback and suggestions
4. **Request extra help** — Ask if the school offers tutoring or study groups
5. **Monitor progress** — Check if improvement happens over the next assessments

:::tip Parent-Teacher Communication
When messaging about grades, be specific:
- ❌ "Why is my child failing?"
- ✅ "I noticed my child scored 45% on the last Math test. Can we discuss what topics they're struggling with and how I can help at home?"
:::

### Downloading Report Cards

At the end of each term:
1. Go to your child's **Grades** section
2. Look for "Download Report Card" or "Term Report"
3. Click to download as PDF
4. Save or print for your records

Some schools require a printed report card to be signed and returned.

## <span className="heading-icon"><FinanceIcon size={18} /></span> Paying School Fees {#paying-school-fees}

4SCH makes it easy to track and pay your child's school fees.

### Viewing Fee Invoices

1. From your dashboard, click on **Fees** or **Payments**
2. You'll see all invoices for your child(ren):
   - **Paid** — Already settled
   - **Pending** — Due for payment
   - **Overdue** — Past the due date
   - **Partial** — Partially paid

### Understanding Your Fee Structure

Typical fee breakdown:
- **Tuition Fee** — Main academic fee
- **Development Levy** — School infrastructure
- **Resource/Activities Fee** — School-defined charge (if applicable)
- **Sports Fee** — Athletics and sports programs
- **Exam Fee** — External exam registration
- **Transport Fee** — School bus (if applicable)
- **Feeding Fee** — School meals (if applicable)

**Your school may have different fee categories.** Click on any invoice to see the detailed breakdown.

### Complete Workflow: Paying School Fees End-to-End

This comprehensive workflow guides you through the entire fee payment process, from receiving an invoice to getting your receipt.

#### Phase 1: Understanding Your Fee Invoice (2-3 minutes)

**When you receive a fee notification:**

You'll get notified via:
- 📱 Push notification: "New fee invoice for Sarah - Term 2 fees"
- 📧 Email: "School Fee Invoice - Due March 31, 2026"
- 💬 SMS: "Fee reminder for Sarah Wilson"

**Step 1: Access the invoice**
1. Open 4SCH app or log in to the web portal
2. Go to **Fees** section from dashboard
3. Or click the notification to go directly to fees

**Step 2: Review invoice details**

Click on the pending invoice to see:

```
Invoice #: INV-2026-0234
Student: Sarah Wilson - Grade 5A
Term: Second Term 2026
Issue Date: February 1, 2026
Due Date: March 31, 2026
Status: PENDING
```

**Fee breakdown:**
| Fee Type | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Tuition Fee | KSh 25,000 |
| Development Levy | KSh 3,000 |
| Sports Fee | KSh 1,500 |
| Resource Fee | KSh 2,000 |
| **Total Amount Due** | **KSh 31,500** |

**Step 3: Check payment options**

Look for:
- **Full Payment:** Pay entire amount (KSh 31,500)
- **Installment Payment:** Pay in parts (if allowed by school)
  - Example: 3 installments of KSh 10,500 each
  - Installment 1 due: March 10
  - Installment 2 due: March 20
  - Installment 3 due: March 31

**Step 4: Check for discounts**
- Early payment discount: "Pay before Feb 28 - Get 5% off"
- Sibling discount: "Second child: 10% off"
- Financial aid: "Scholarship applied: -KSh 5,000"

**Updated total after discounts: KSh 28,000**

:::tip Understanding Your Invoice
- **Due Date:** Pay before this date to avoid late fees
- **Overdue:** Late fee may apply (e.g., 5% penalty)
- **Partial Payment:** Some schools allow partial payment
- **Previous Balance:** Check if you have any outstanding fees from previous terms
:::

#### Phase 2: Choosing Your Payment Method (1 minute)

**Available payment methods (varies by school):**

**Option 1: Online Payment (Recommended - Instant confirmation)**
- ✅ Credit/Debit Card (Visa, Mastercard)
- ✅ Mobile Money (M-Pesa, MTN, Airtel)
- ✅ Bank Transfer (instant or 24-hour processing)
- ✅ PayPal or other e-wallets

**Option 2: Offline Payment (Manual confirmation needed)**
- 💰 Cash at school office
- 🏦 Cheque deposit
- 🏧 Bank branch deposit

**Recommendation:**
- **Online payment:** Instant receipt, no school visit needed
- **Cash payment:** Use if you don't have cards/mobile money

**Cost comparison:**

| Method | Transaction Fee | Processing Time |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|
| Card Payment | 2.5% (KSh 700) | Instant |
| M-Pesa | 1% (KSh 280) | Instant |
| Bank Transfer | KSh 0-100 | 1-24 hours |
| Cash at office | KSh 0 | Instant (but requires visit) |

**Best choice for KSh 28,000 invoice:**
- **M-Pesa:** Lower fee (KSh 280), instant confirmation
- **Bank Transfer:** No fee if same-day, but may take hours

#### Phase 5: What If Payment Fails? (Troubleshooting)

**Common issues and solutions:**

**Issue 1: "Insufficient funds"**
- Check M-Pesa balance: Dial *234# or check app
- Top up your M-Pesa account
- Try again

**Issue 2: "Transaction timeout"**
- Network issue - wait 2 minutes
- Check if money was deducted from M-Pesa
- If deducted but payment not reflected:
  - Wait 30 minutes for processing
  - Contact school if still pending after 1 hour
  - Keep M-Pesa SMS as proof

**Issue 3: "Payment gateway error"**
- Try different payment method
- Clear browser cache (web) or restart app
- Try again in 10 minutes
- Contact school IT support if persistent

**Issue 4: Money deducted but payment shows "pending"**
- **Don't pay again!**
- Take screenshot of:
  - M-Pesa confirmation SMS
  - 4SCH payment status
- Message school accountant with:
  ```
  Subject: Payment Verification Needed
  
  Hi, I paid KSh 28,000 for Sarah Wilson (Grade 5A)
  via M-Pesa on Feb 15, 2026 at 10:30 AM.
  
  M-Pesa Transaction Code: MPE2026021567890
  
  Payment shows as "pending" in 4SCH.
  Please verify and confirm.
  
  Thank you.
  ```
- School will reconcile within 24 hours

**📅 Timing Strategies:**
1. **Pay immediately after salary:** Avoid spending it elsewhere
2. **Set reminders:** 5 days before due date
3. **Use auto-debit:** Some schools offer this option
4. **Pay in person on payroll days:** Avoid weekend bank closures

**🔒 Security Practices:**
1. **Always download receipt:** Proof of payment
2. **Keep M-Pesa SMS:** Additional proof
3. **Screenshot confirmation:** Just in case
4. **Check invoice status:** Verify it shows "PAID"

**🚨 Avoid These Mistakes:**
1. ❌ Paying to wrong account
2. ❌ Not keeping receipts
3. ❌ Paying twice when first payment pending
4. ❌ Missing due date and paying late fees
5. ❌ Not verifying student name on invoice

## <span className="heading-icon"><MessagingIcon size={18} /></span> Messaging Teachers & School {#messaging-teachers}

### When to Message Teachers

**Good reasons to message:**
- Ask about your child's progress
- Inform teacher about absences or late arrivals
- Ask for clarification on assignments
- Discuss behavioral concerns
- Request parent-teacher meeting
- Ask about extra help opportunities

**When to call instead:**
- Urgent safety concerns
- Medical emergencies
- Serious behavioral incidents
- Time-sensitive issues

### How to Send a Message

**Option 1: From Your Child's Profile**
1. Go to your child's profile
2. Click **Message Teacher**
3. Select which teacher (class teacher, subject teacher)
4. Write your message
5. Click **Send**

**Option 2: From Messaging Section**
1. Go to **Messages** → **New Message**
2. Select recipient (teacher's name)
3. Write and send

### Writing Effective Messages

**Good Message Example:**
> **Subject:** Question About Math Homework
>
> Dear Mr. Okafor,
>
> My son mentioned he's having difficulty with the algebra homework assigned yesterday. Could you please clarify what we should focus on for questions 5-8?
>
> Also, are there any additional practice problems you'd recommend?
>
> Thank you,  
> Mrs. Adekunle

**Why This Works:**
- Polite and respectful
- Specific about the issue
- Clear question
- Shows parent engagement

**Poor Message Example:**
> "Why is the homework so hard?"

This is too vague and sounds confrontational.

### Response Times

Teachers typically respond within:
- **24 hours during weekdays**
- **Longer on weekends or holidays**

If it's urgent, consider calling the school office instead.

### Reading School Announcements

The school admin and teachers send announcements about:
- School holidays and closures
- Upcoming events (sports day, parent-teacher meetings)
- Policy changes
- Fee deadlines
- Health and safety updates

**You'll find announcements:**
- On your dashboard
- In the **Announcements** or **News** section
- Via notifications (if enabled)

:::tip Don't Miss Important Updates
Enable push notifications so you don't miss urgent announcements about:
- School closures due to weather
- Emergency changes to schedule
- Important meetings

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### "I can't log in"

**Try these steps:**
1. **Check your username** — It may be your email, phone number, or a username provided by school
2. **Reset your password** — Click "Forgot Password" on login screen
3. **Check with school** — Contact the school office to verify your account is active

### "I don't see my child's information"

**Possible reasons:**
- Your account isn't linked to your child yet
- You're logged in with the wrong account
- The school hasn't added your child to the system yet

**Solution:** Contact the school's admin office to link your account properly.

### "Fees show as unpaid but I already paid"

**What to do:**
1. Check your payment receipt/confirmation
2. Message the school bursar with payment details (date, amount, method)
3. They'll verify and update your account

Allow 24-48 hours for manual payments to reflect in the system.

### "I'm not receiving notifications"

**Check these:**
1. **Notification settings** — Are they enabled in your profile?
2. **Email spam folder** — Check if notifications went to spam
3. **Phone number** — Is it correct in your profile?
4. **App permissions** — If using mobile app, check notification permissions in phone settings
