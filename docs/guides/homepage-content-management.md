# Homepage Content Management

The homepage is the first thing students, parents, and staff see when they log into 4SCH. Effective homepage management keeps your school community informed, engaged, and aware of important updates, events, and achievements.

:::info Who Should Read This?
- **School Administrators** - Manage homepage sliders and school-wide announcements
- **Teachers** - Post class-specific announcements (limited)
- **Marketing/Communications Staff** - Keep content fresh and relevant
:::

---

## What You Can Manage

### 1. **Homepage Sliders** 🎨
Visual banners that rotate on the homepage, perfect for:
- Welcome messages and seasonal greetings
- Event promotions (Sports Day, Cultural Festival, etc.)
- Achievement highlights (Exam results, awards)
- Important announcements (School closure, schedule changes)
- Upcoming deadlines (Fee payment, exam registration)

### 2. **School-Wide Announcements** 📢
Text-based notices that appear prominently:
- Emergency notifications
- General updates and news
- Policy changes
- Event information
- Quick reminders

### 3. **Featured Content** ⭐
Highlight important information:
- Latest news articles
- Upcoming events
- Important documents
- Quick links

## Managing Homepage Sliders

### Why Use Sliders?

**Benefits:**
- ✅ **Visual Impact** - Images grab attention better than text
- ✅ **Multiple Messages** - Rotate several important items
- ✅ **Professional Look** - Modern, polished appearance
- ✅ **Engagement** - Keep homepage fresh and interesting
- ✅ **Mobile Friendly** - Look great on phones and tablets

**Best Use Cases:**
- Welcome new students/parents at session start
- Promote school events (Open Day, Sports competitions)
- Celebrate achievements (WAEC results, competitions won)
- Important deadlines (Fee payment, exam registration)
- Seasonal messages (Holiday greetings, term start)

---

### Creating a New Slider

**Step 1: Access Slider Management**
1. Log in to Admin Panel
2. Go to **Settings** or **Content Management**
3. Click **Homepage Sliders** or **Slider Management**

**Step 2: Create Slider**
1. Click **Add New Slider** or **Create Slider**
2. Fill in slider details

**Step 3: Upload Slider Image**

**Image Requirements:**
- **Recommended Size:** 1920 x 600 pixels (16:9 landscape ratio)
- **Minimum Size:** 1200 x 400 pixels
- **Maximum File Size:** 2MB (preferably under 1MB for fast loading)
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Quality:** High resolution but optimized for web

**Tips for Great Slider Images:**
- Use school brand colors
- Include school logo
- Text should be large and readable
- High contrast (dark text on light background or vice versa)
- Avoid too much text - keep it punchy
- Test on mobile devices

**Step 4: Add Slider Content**

**Title:**
```
Enter a short, catchy title
Example: "Welcome to First Term 2024/2025!"
         "WAEC Results Out - 98% Pass Rate!"
         "Sports Day - March 15th"
```

**Description/Subtitle (Optional):**
```
Add supporting text if needed (keep it brief)
Example: "We wish all our students success this term"
         "Congratulations to our exceptional students"
         "Register your child now - limited slots"
```

**Link (Optional):**
- Where should users go when they click?
- Examples:
  - Event registration page
  - Fee payment portal
  - News article
  - Gallery album
  - External website

**Link Type:**
- Internal (within 4SCH system)
- External (opens in new tab)

**Step 5: Set Display Options**

**Order/Priority:**
- Number 1 = Shows first in rotation
- Number 2 = Shows second, etc.
- Lower numbers = higher priority

**Status:**
- ✅ **Active** - Slider is live and visible
- ⏸️ **Inactive** - Slider is hidden (draft or archived)

**Display Duration:**
- How long slider shows before rotating to next
- Recommended: 5-7 seconds
- Too short: Users can't read
- Too long: Users get bored

**Target Audience (if available):**
- All Users
- Students Only
- Parents Only
- Teachers/Staff Only

**Step 6: Preview & Publish**
1. Click **Preview** to see how it looks
2. Test on mobile view
3. If satisfied, click **Save** or **Publish**
4. Slider goes live immediately (or as scheduled)

### Managing Existing Sliders

**View All Sliders:**
1. Go to **Homepage Sliders**
2. See list of all sliders with:
   - Thumbnail preview
   - Title
   - Status (Active/Inactive)
   - Order number
   - Date created

**Edit a Slider:**
1. Click **Edit** button or pencil icon
2. Update any field (image, title, description, order, etc.)
3. Click **Save Changes**
4. Changes appear immediately on homepage

**Delete a Slider:**
1. Click **Delete** button or trash icon
2. Confirm deletion
3. ⚠️ **Warning:** This is permanent!

**Reorder Sliders:**
- **Method 1:** Drag and drop sliders to reorder
- **Method 2:** Change the order/priority number
- **Method 3:** Use up/down arrows to move sliders

**Activate/Deactivate Slider:**
- Toggle **Active** switch on/off
- Inactive sliders don't show on homepage but are saved
- Useful for seasonal content you'll reuse

---

### Slider Best Practices

**Content Guidelines:**

**DO:**
- ✅ Use high-quality, clear images
- ✅ Keep text minimal and punchy
- ✅ Update regularly (at least monthly)
- ✅ Align with school events calendar
- ✅ Use consistent branding (colors, fonts, logo)
- ✅ Test on mobile before publishing
- ✅ Include call-to-action if needed ("Register Now", "Learn More")

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Use blurry or low-quality images
- ❌ Overcrowd with too much text
- ❌ Leave outdated sliders active
- ❌ Use copyrighted images without permission
- ❌ Forget to check mobile responsiveness
- ❌ Use offensive or inappropriate content
- ❌ Have too many sliders (3-5 is ideal)

**Timing & Rotation:**

**How Many Sliders?**
- **Ideal:** 3-5 active sliders
- **Minimum:** 2 (for rotation effect)
- **Maximum:** 7 (more = cluttered, slow loading)

**How Long to Display?**
- Important announcement: Keep for 1-2 weeks
- Event promotion: 2-3 weeks before event
- Seasonal greeting: Duration of season/term
- Achievement celebration: 1-2 weeks
- General welcome: Entire term/session

**Update Frequency:**
- Review weekly
- Update at least monthly
- Remove outdated content immediately
- Add new content for major events

## Managing Announcements

### Types of Announcements

**1. School-Wide Announcements**
- Sent to all students, parents, and staff
- For general school news
- Admin only

**2. Class-Specific Announcements**
- Sent to specific class/section
- For class-related news
- Teachers can post

**3. Subject-Specific Announcements**
- Related to a particular subject
- Teachers post to their subject students

---

### Creating School-Wide Announcements

**Step 1: Access Announcements**
1. Log in to Admin Panel
2. Go to **Communication → Announcements**
3. Or from Dashboard → **Quick Actions** → **Post Announcement**

**Step 2: Create Announcement**
1. Click **Create Announcement** or **New Announcement**

**Step 3: Enter Announcement Details**

**Title:**
```
Clear, descriptive title
Example: "School Closure - Public Holiday"
         "Exam Timetable Released"
         "Parent-Teacher Meeting - Saturday"
```

**Message/Description:**
```
Full details of announcement
Example:
"The school will be closed on Monday, March 17th, 2025 for 
Independence Day. Classes will resume on Tuesday, March 18th.

Enjoy the holiday responsibly!"
```

**Formatting:**
- Keep paragraphs short
- Use bullet points for lists
- Bold important information
- Include dates, times, locations clearly

**Step 4: Set Announcement Options**

**Priority/Importance:**
- 🔴 **High** - Urgent (shows red, sends push notification)
- 🟡 **Medium** - Important (shows yellow, may send notification)
- 🟢 **Low** - General info (shows green, no push notification)

**Target Audience:**
- All Users
- Students Only
- Parents Only
- Teachers/Staff Only
- Specific Classes/Sections

**Attachments (Optional):**
- Upload PDFs, images, or documents
- Examples: Exam timetable PDF, event flyer, forms
- Max size: Usually 5-10MB

**Expiry Date (Optional):**
- When should announcement no longer show?
- Example: Event announcements expire after event date
- Leave blank for permanent announcements

**Step 5: Preview & Send**
1. Click **Preview** to review
2. Check for typos and clarity
3. Click **Send** or **Publish**

**Delivery:**
- Appears on homepage immediately
- Sent as push notification (if enabled)
- Emailed to users (if configured)
- Shows in user's announcement feed

### Managing Announcements

**View Announcements:**
- See all active and archived announcements
- Filter by:
  - Date range
  - Target audience
  - Priority
  - Status (Active/Expired)

**Edit Announcement:**
1. Find announcement in list
2. Click **Edit**
3. Update content
4. Click **Save**
5. ⚠️ Note: Notifications won't re-send to users who already saw it

**Delete Announcement:**
1. Click **Delete**
2. Confirm deletion
3. Removes from all users' feeds

**Pin Important Announcement:**
- Pinned announcements stay at top
- Use for critical, ongoing information
- Example: "Fees payment portal maintenance this weekend"

### Announcement Best Practices

**Writing Effective Announcements:**

**DO:**
- ✅ Use clear, simple language
- ✅ Put most important info first
- ✅ Include specific dates, times, locations
- ✅ Provide action steps if needed
- ✅ Proofread before sending
- ✅ Use appropriate priority level
- ✅ Send timely (not last-minute unless emergency)

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Use ALL CAPS (seems like shouting)
- ❌ Write overly long messages (keep concise)
- ❌ Mark everything as "High Priority"
- ❌ Send too frequently (announcement fatigue)
- ❌ Include unverified information
- ❌ Forget to include key details

**Timing Guidelines:**

**How Far in Advance:**
- Major events: 2-4 weeks
- Regular events: 1-2 weeks
- Deadlines: 1-2 weeks, with reminders
- Emergency: Immediately
- General news: As it happens

**Frequency:**
- **Good:** 2-3 announcements per week
- **Acceptable:** Daily for different topics
- **Too Much:** Multiple daily for non-urgent items

**Best Times to Send:**
- **Weekday mornings:** 8-10 AM (parents checking before work)
- **Weekday evenings:** 7-9 PM (family time, check phones)
- **Avoid:** Very late night, very early morning
- **Weekend:** OK for non-urgent announcements

## Troubleshooting

### Slider Not Showing

**Check:**
- Is slider status **Active**?
- Is image uploaded correctly?
- Is order/priority set?
- Are there any other active sliders?
- Clear browser cache

**Try:**
- Re-upload image (check file size and format)
- Change slider order
- Deactivate other sliders temporarily
- Check permissions

---

### Announcement Not Received

**Check:**
- Target audience settings correct?
- Push notifications enabled in user settings?
- Email notifications configured?
- Announcement not expired?

**Try:**
- Resend announcement
- Check user notification preferences
- Verify email addresses
- Test with one user first

---

### Image Quality Issues

**Problem:** Blurry or pixelated images

**Solutions:**
- Use higher resolution source image
- Maintain recommended dimensions (1920x600)
- Don't stretch small images
- Export at 80-90% quality (JPG)
- Use PNG for images with text

---

### Slow Homepage Loading

**Problem:** Too many or large sliders

**Solutions:**
- Limit to 3-5 active sliders
- Compress images (use tools like TinyPNG)
- Keep file sizes under 1MB
- Use JPG instead of PNG for photos
- Remove inactive/old sliders
