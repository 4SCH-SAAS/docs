# School Gallery Management

The school gallery is a powerful tool for building school spirit, engaging parents, documenting achievements, and creating a vibrant school community. Showcase sports days, cultural events, academic achievements, field trips, and daily school life through organized photo and video albums.

:::info Who Should Read This?
- **School Administrators** - Manage gallery settings and permissions
- **Teachers** - Upload class event photos
- **Marketing/Communications Staff** - Document and promote school activities
- **Parents & Students** - View and download memories (view-only access)
:::

---

## Why Use the School Gallery?

**Benefits:**
- ✅ **Engagement** - Parents love seeing their children's school activities
- ✅ **Documentation** - Preserve school history and achievements
- ✅ **Marketing** - Showcase school activities to prospective parents
- ✅ **Community Building** - Strengthen school spirit and pride
- ✅ **Communication** - Visual updates are more engaging than text
- ✅ **Memories** - Create lasting memories for students and families

**Common Use Cases:**
- Sports day and competitions
- Cultural festivals and performances
- Graduation ceremonies
- Field trips and excursions
- Class activities and projects
- Award ceremonies
- Special assemblies
- Guest speaker visits
- School facilities and infrastructure
- Student achievements

---

## Gallery Structure

### Albums Organization

The gallery is organized into **albums**, similar to photo albums or folders.

**Album Categories:**
1. **Events** - Sports Day 2024, Cultural Festival, Science Fair
2. **Academic Activities** - Library Week, Math Competition, Science Lab
3. **Ceremonies** - Graduation 2024, Prize Giving Day, Assembly
4. **Field Trips** - Zoo Visit, Museum Tour, Factory Visit
5. **Sports** - Inter-House Sports, Football Match, Swimming Gala
6. **Arts & Culture** - Drama Performance, Music Concert, Art Exhibition
7. **Facilities** - School Buildings, Laboratories, Library, Playground
8. **Special Occasions** - Independence Day, Christmas, Easter

**Organization Best Practices:**
- Organize by session year (e.g., "Sports Day 2024", "Sports Day 2025")
- Use clear, descriptive album names
- Separate major events into individual albums
- Group related smaller events if needed
- Archive old session albums (keep accessible but separate)

## Creating Albums

### Step 1: Access Gallery Management

1. Log in to Admin Panel
2. Go to **Gallery** → **Manage Gallery**
3. Or from Dashboard → **Content** → **Gallery**

### Step 2: Create New Album

1. Click **Create Album** or **New Album**
2. Fill in album details

**Album Name:**
```
Use clear, descriptive names with year
Examples:
✅ "Sports Day 2024"
✅ "First Term Graduation 2024/2025"
✅ "Science Week - JSS 2 Projects"
✅ "Field Trip: National Museum - March 2025"

❌ "Photos"
❌ "Event"
❌ "New Album"
```

**Album Description:**
```
Add context and details
Example:
"Annual Sports Day held on March 15, 2025 at the school field. 
Inter-house competitions featuring track events, field events, 
and novelty races. Great participation from all houses!"
```

**Session Year:**
- Select academic session (e.g., 2024/2025)
- This helps organize and filter albums
- Makes it easy to view specific year's content

**Date:**
- Event date (when photos were taken)
- Helps with chronological sorting

**Visibility:**
- **Public** - Visible to all users (students, parents, staff)
- **Private** - Visible only to admin/selected users
- **Students Only** - Only students can view
- **Parents Only** - Only parents can view
- **Staff Only** - Only teachers and staff can view

**Featured Album:**
- ✅ Featured albums show prominently on homepage
- Use for most important/recent events
- Limit to 2-3 featured albums at a time

### Step 3: Save Album

1. Review details
2. Click **Save** or **Create Album**
3. Album is created (empty, ready for photos)

## Uploading Photos & Videos

### Preparing Media Files

**Photo Requirements:**

**Format:**
- ✅ JPG/JPEG (recommended for photos)
- ✅ PNG (for images with text/graphics)
- ⚠️ HEIC/HEIF (convert to JPG first for compatibility)

**Size:**
- **Recommended:** 1920 x 1080 px to 3840 x 2160 px
- **Minimum:** 800 x 600 px
- **Maximum:** 4K (3840 x 2160 px) - larger files upload slower
- **File Size:** Under 5MB per photo (2-3MB ideal)

**Quality Tips:**
- Use good lighting when taking photos
- Hold camera/phone steady (avoid blurry images)
- Take landscape orientation for group photos
- Ensure faces are clearly visible
- Avoid overly dark or bright images

**Video Requirements:**

**Format:**
- ✅ MP4 (H.264) - recommended
- ✅ MOV
- ✅ AVI
- ⚠️ Check max file size limits

**Size:**
- **Maximum:** Usually 50-100MB per video
- **Length:** 1-5 minutes ideal (shorter is better for loading)
- **Resolution:** 1080p (Full HD) recommended

**Video Tips:**
- Keep videos short and focused
- Ensure good audio quality
- Use landscape orientation
- Avoid shaky footage
- Add text/subtitles if needed

---

### Uploading Process

**Step 1: Select Album**
1. Go to **Gallery → Manage Gallery**
2. Find the album you created
3. Click **Upload** or **Add Photos**

**Step 2: Select Files**

**Method 1: Drag & Drop**
- Drag photos/videos from your computer
- Drop them into the upload area
- Multiple files can be uploaded at once

**Method 2: Browse Files**
- Click **Browse** or **Choose Files**
- Select photos from your computer
- Hold Ctrl (Windows) or Cmd (Mac) to select multiple

**Bulk Upload:**
- Select up to 20-50 files at once (varies by system)
- Progress bar shows upload status
- Don't close browser until upload completes

**Step 3: Add Photo Details (Optional)**

For each photo, you can add:

**Caption/Title:**
```
Describe what's in the photo
Example: "JSS 2 students at the science fair demonstration"
         "Champion house receiving trophy - Red House wins!"
         "Students visiting the planetarium"
```

**Tags/Keywords:**
```
Help with searching later
Example: "sports, athletics, track, 100m race"
         "science, experiment, chemistry, lab"
```

**Featured Photo:**
- Mark 1-2 photos as "featured" for album cover
- Best quality photos that represent the event

**Step 4: Save & Publish**
1. Review uploaded photos
2. Remove any unwanted images
3. Click **Save** or **Publish**
4. Photos appear in gallery immediately

## Managing Gallery Content

### Organizing Photos

**Reordering Photos:**
- Drag and drop to rearrange
- Or use arrow buttons to move up/down
- Put best photos first

**Setting Album Cover:**
- Select the most representative photo
- Or upload a custom cover image
- Shows as thumbnail in album list

**Bulk Actions:**
- Select multiple photos (checkboxes)
- Delete multiple at once
- Move to different album
- Download selected

---

### Editing Albums

**To Edit an Existing Album:**
1. Go to **Gallery → Manage Gallery**
2. Find album in list
3. Click **Edit** button or pencil icon

**You Can Update:**
- Album name
- Description
- Session year
- Date
- Visibility settings
- Featured status

**Common Edits:**
- Fix typos in album names
- Update descriptions with results/details
- Change visibility after event
- Un-feature old albums

---

### Deleting Content

**Delete Individual Photos:**
1. Open album
2. Select photo(s) to delete
3. Click **Delete** or trash icon
4. Confirm deletion
5. ⚠️ **Permanent** - cannot be undone

**Delete Entire Album:**
1. Go to album list
2. Click **Delete Album**
3. Confirm deletion
4. ⚠️ **All photos in album are deleted**

**When to Delete:**
- Poor quality/blurry photos
- Duplicate images
- Photos taken by mistake
- Outdated content (after archiving)
- Student privacy concerns

## Gallery Best Practices

### Content Guidelines

**DO:**
- ✅ Get permission before photographing students
- ✅ Use high-quality, well-lit photos
- ✅ Capture diverse activities and students
- ✅ Include captions with context
- ✅ Organize chronologically
- ✅ Feature positive moments and achievements
- ✅ Update regularly (weekly or after events)
- ✅ Remove poor quality images

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Upload photos without permission
- ❌ Include embarrassing or unflattering images
- ❌ Post photos showing student injuries or distress
- ❌ Upload copyrighted images from internet
- ❌ Leave albums disorganized or unlabeled
- ❌ Upload extremely large files (slow loading)
- ❌ Include personally identifiable information in captions

---

### Privacy & Safety

**Student Privacy:**
- ✅ Obtain parental consent for photo use
- ✅ Don't tag student names publicly (if system allows tagging)
- ✅ Be cautious with close-up facial photos
- ✅ Respect parents who opt out of photo sharing
- ✅ Avoid photos showing student addresses/locations
- ✅ Don't show student in compromising situations

**Permissions:**
- Have photo release forms signed at enrollment
- Maintain list of students whose parents opted out
- Check permissions before major events
- Respect cultural/religious photography restrictions

**Content Safety:**
- Review all photos before publishing
- Remove any inappropriate content immediately
- Monitor for bullying or negative comments
- Have a takedown policy for reported images

---

### Photography Tips

**For School Events:**

**Before the Event:**
- Charge camera/phone batteries
- Clear memory card/storage space
- Plan key moments to capture
- Assign photographers if needed

**During the Event:**
- Capture arrival and setup
- Photograph opening ceremony
- Get action shots during activities
- Include crowd/audience reactions
- Capture award/prize giving
- Group photos of participants
- Candid moments and smiles
- Closing ceremony

**After the Event:**
- Review and select best photos
- Delete blurry/poor quality images
- Organize by activity/segment
- Edit if needed (brightness, crop)
- Upload promptly while event is fresh

**Composition Tips:**
- **Rule of Thirds** - Don't center everything
- **Get Close** - Fill frame with subject
- **Eye Level** - Shoot from students' height for better perspective
- **Backgrounds** - Avoid cluttered or distracting backgrounds
- **Lighting** - Use natural light when possible
- **Action** - Capture movement and emotions
- **Variety** - Mix wide shots, medium shots, close-ups

**Consistent Album Names:**

Format: [Event Name] - [Date/Session]

Examples:
✅ Sports Day - March 2025
✅ Science Fair - Second Term 2024/2025
✅ Graduation Ceremony - July 2024

❌ Event Photos

```
Rename files for easy organization

DSC_0234.jpg → science_fair_chemistry_demo.jpg
20250315_123045.jpg → graduation_award_ceremony.jpg
```

## Viewing Gallery (User Perspective)

### For Students & Parents

**How to Access:**
1. Log in to Student/Parent Portal or App
2. Go to **Gallery** from main menu
3. Or click gallery icon on homepage

**Browsing Albums:**
- View list of all public albums
- Filter by session year
- Search by album name
- Sort by date (newest first)

**Viewing Photos:**
- Click album to open
- Photos display in grid or slideshow
- Click photo for full-size view
- Swipe left/right to navigate (mobile)
- Use arrow keys (desktop)

**Downloading Photos:**
- Click **Download** button on photo
- Or download entire album (if enabled)
- Saves to device

**Sharing:**
- Some systems allow sharing to social media
- Or share album link with family

**Permissions:**
- View-only access (cannot upload/delete)
- Can only see public albums or those assigned to them

## Advanced Features

### Session Year Filtering

**Why It's Useful:**
- Separate content by academic year
- Easy to find historical events
- Clean interface (don't mix years)

**How to Use:**
1. Assign session year when creating album
2. Users can filter gallery by session
3. Archive old sessions at year-end

---

### Album Access Control

**Public Albums:**
- Visible to all logged-in users
- Good for major school events
- Most common setting

**Private Albums:**
- Only admin can view
- Use for:
  - Staff-only events
  - Pre-publication review
  - Sensitive content

**Role-Based Albums:**
- **Students Only** - Class photos, student activities
- **Parents Only** - Parent meetings, PTA events
- **Staff Only** - Teacher training, staff outings

**Class-Specific Albums:**
- Only specific class can view
- Example: "JSS 2A Science Project"
- Privacy for class activities

---

### Featured Albums

**What They Do:**
- Appear prominently on homepage
- Highlighted in gallery section
- Draw attention to recent/important events

**Best Practices:**
- Feature 2-3 most recent albums
- Update regularly (monthly)
- Un-feature after 2-4 weeks
- Feature diversity of activities

---

### Comments & Engagement

**If Comments Enabled:**
- Users can comment on photos/albums
- Parents share appreciation
- Students reminisce

**Moderation:**
- Review comments regularly
- Remove inappropriate content
- Disable if problematic

**Reactions/Likes:**
- Simple engagement option
- See which photos are most popular
- Less moderation needed than comments

## Gallery Maintenance

### Regular Tasks

**Weekly:**
- Upload photos from recent events
- Review and organize new albums
- Remove poor quality images
- Respond to any photo concerns

**Monthly:**
- Review featured albums (update if needed)
- Check storage space used
- Clean up draft/incomplete albums
- Backup important photos externally

**Termly:**
- Archive previous term's albums
- Plan upcoming events to photograph
- Review privacy permissions
- Create new albums for next term

**Yearly:**
- Archive previous session
- Delete unnecessary duplicates
- Create year-in-review album
- Update photography policies

---

### Storage Management

**Monitor Storage:**
- Check available gallery storage space
- Most systems have limits (e.g., 10GB, 50GB)

**If Running Low:**
- Delete duplicate photos
- Remove poor quality images
- Compress large files before upload
- Archive very old albums offline
- Upgrade storage if needed

**Optimization:**
- Resize photos before upload (don't need 4K images)
- Use JPG instead of PNG for photos
- Compress videos before upload
- Delete RAW camera files (keep JPG only)

---

## Troubleshooting

### Upload Failing

**Check:**
- File size under limit (usually 5-10MB per photo)
- Correct file format (JPG, PNG, MP4)
- Internet connection stable
- Browser is up-to-date
- Enough storage space available

**Solutions:**
- Reduce file size (compress image)
- Convert to supported format
- Upload fewer files at once
- Try different browser
- Clear browser cache

---

### Photo Not Displaying

**Check:**
- Upload completed successfully
- Album is published (not draft)
- Photo visibility settings correct
- Browser cache (refresh/clear)

**Solutions:**
- Re-upload photo
- Check photo file isn't corrupted
- Verify album visibility settings
- Try viewing in incognito/private window

---

### Slow Loading

**Causes:**
- Too many high-resolution photos
- Large video files
- Slow internet connection
- Too many albums loading at once

**Solutions:**
- Optimize images before upload
- Limit videos per album
- Use lazy loading (loads as you scroll)
- Reduce number of featured albums

---

### Permission Denied

**Problem:** User reports can't view album

**Check:**
- Album visibility settings
- User role permissions
- Session year filter active
- Album published (not draft)

**Solution:**
- Adjust album visibility
- Verify user has correct role
- Check session year assignment
- Ensure album is published
