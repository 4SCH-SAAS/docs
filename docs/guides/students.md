## <span className="heading-icon"><DashboardIcon size={18} /></span> Your Student Dashboard {#student-dashboard}

When you log into 4SCH, you'll see your **Student Dashboard**. This is your home page for everything school-related.

### What's on Your Dashboard

**Today's Schedule:**
- Your classes for today with times
- Teachers for each subject
- Room numbers (if available)

**Upcoming Deadlines:**
- Assignments due this week
- Upcoming tests and exams
- Projects and submissions

**Recent Updates:**
- New grades posted
- Messages from teachers
- School announcements

**Quick Stats:**
- Your attendance percentage
- Latest grade average
- Pending assignments

:::tip Start Your Day Here
Check your dashboard every morning before school to see:
- What classes you have today
- Any homework due today
- New messages or announcements
:::

## <span className="heading-icon"><DashboardIcon size={18} /></span> Viewing Your Timetable {#viewing-your-timetable}

Your timetable shows all your classes for the week.

### How to Check Your Timetable

1. Go to **Timetable** from the main menu
2. You'll see your weekly schedule
3. Each box shows:
   - Subject name (e.g., Mathematics, English)
   - Teacher's name
   - Time (e.g., 9:00 AM - 10:00 AM)
   - Room/venue (if applicable)

### Understanding Your Timetable

**Different views:**
- **Week View** — See the whole week at once
- **Day View** — Focus on just today's classes
- **List View** — See classes as a simple list

**Color coding:**
- Each subject usually has a different color
- This makes it easy to spot classes quickly

### Exam Timetable

When exams are coming:
1. Go to **Timetable** → **Exams**
2. You'll see:
   - Exam date and time
   - Subject being tested
   - Duration (e.g., 2 hours)
   - Venue/room

**Download your exam timetable** so you can study the right subjects at the right time.

:::tip Study Smart
Print your exam timetable and stick it on your wall at home. This helps you plan your study time properly.
:::

## <span className="heading-icon"><GradesIcon size={18} /></span> Assignments & Homework {#assignments}

Your teachers will post assignments for you to complete and submit online.

### Viewing Your Assignments

**From Your Dashboard:**
- See assignments due soon
- Click on any assignment to view details

**From Assignments Page:**
1. Go to **Assignments** from the main menu
2. You'll see all your assignments organized by:
   - **Upcoming** — Due soon
   - **Overdue** — You missed the deadline
   - **Submitted** — You've already turned them in
   - **Graded** — Teacher has graded your work

### Understanding an Assignment

When you click on an assignment, you'll see:

**Assignment Details:**
- **Title** — What it's called (e.g., "Chapter 5 Math Problems")
- **Subject** — Which class it's for
- **Teacher** — Who assigned it
- **Instructions** — What you need to do
- **Due Date** — When to submit by
- **Total Points** — How many marks it's worth

**Attached Files:**
- Worksheets to download
- Reference materials
- Rubrics (grading criteria)

### Submitting Your Assignment

**Step 1: Do the Work**
Complete the assignment according to the instructions.

**Step 2: Prepare Your Submission**

If it's a **file upload**:
- Save your work as a PDF, Word document, or image
- Name it properly: "YourName_Subject_Assignment.pdf"
- Example: "JohnOjo_Math_Chapter5.pdf"

If it's a **text entry**:
- Type your answer directly into the submission box

**Step 3: Submit**
1. Click **Submit Assignment**
2. Choose your file (if file upload) or type your answer
3. Review what you're submitting
4. Click **Submit** to send it to your teacher

**Step 4: Confirmation**
You'll see a confirmation that your assignment was submitted successfully.

:::caution Important
Once you submit, you usually can't edit it! Make sure you've:
- ✅ Completed all questions
- ✅ Checked your spelling and grammar
- ✅ Attached the right file
- ✅ Submitted before the deadline
:::

### Checking Your Assignment Grade

After your teacher grades your work:
1. Go to **Assignments**
2. Look for assignments marked **Graded**
3. Click on the assignment
4. You'll see:
   - Your score (e.g., 15/20)
   - Your percentage (e.g., 75%)
   - Teacher's feedback/comments

**Read the feedback carefully!** Your teacher's comments help you improve next time.

### What If You Miss the Deadline?

**If you're going to be late:**
- Message your teacher before the deadline
- Explain why (illness, family emergency, etc.)
- Ask if you can submit late

**Some teachers allow late submissions with a penalty** (e.g., 10% deduction per day late).

:::tip Time Management
Don't wait until the last minute! Submit assignments at least a day before the deadline. This gives you a buffer if something goes wrong (internet issues, computer problems, etc.).
:::

## <span className="heading-icon"><GradesIcon size={18} /></span> Checking Your Grades {#checking-your-grades}

See how you're performing in all your subjects.

### How to View Your Grades

1. Go to **Grades** or **Results** from the main menu
2. Select the term/semester
3. You'll see your grades for all subjects

### What You'll See

**For Each Subject:**
- Test scores (CA1, CA2, Quiz, etc.)
- Exam scores (Midterm, Final Exam)
- Your total/average
- Grade letter (A, B, C, etc.) or percentage

**Overall:**
- Your average across all subjects
- Your position in class (if school shows this)
- Comparison to previous terms

### Understanding Your Grades

**Continuous Assessment (CA):**
- Tests during the term
- Assignments and projects
- Class participation
- Usually 30-40% of final grade

**Exams:**
- Midterm exam
- Final/end-of-term exam
- Usually 60-70% of final grade

**Total Grade:**
CA + Exam = Your final grade for that subject

### Grade Scale

Most schools use something like:
- **A (70-100%)** — Excellent
- **B (60-69%)** — Very Good
- **C (50-59%)** — Good
- **D (40-49%)** — Pass
- **F (Below 40%)** — Fail

*(Your school may use a different scale)*

### Downloading Your Report Card

At the end of term:
1. Go to **Grades** or **Results**
2. Look for **Download Report Card**
3. Click to save as PDF
4. Print it if your parents need to sign it

### What to Do If You're Not Happy with Your Grades

**Don't panic! Here's what to do:**

1. **Identify the problem**
   - Which subjects are you struggling with?
   - Was it just one bad test, or is it ongoing?

2. **Review your mistakes**
   - Look at which questions you got wrong
   - Understand why you made those mistakes

3. **Ask for help**
   - Talk to your teacher during break or after class
   - Ask your parents if you need a tutor
   - Form study groups with classmates

4. **Make a plan**
   - Study more for that subject
   - Do extra practice problems
   - Review your notes regularly

5. **Track improvement**
   - Check if your next test score is better
   - Keep working until you see progress

:::tip Stay Positive
One bad grade doesn't define you! What matters is that you learn from it and improve. Many successful students had setbacks and came back stronger.
:::

## <span className="heading-icon"><AttendanceIcon size={18} /></span> Your Attendance Record {#attendance}

Track whether you're attending school regularly.

### Why Attendance Matters

**Good attendance is important because:**
- You learn more when you're present in class
- Many schools require 75-80% attendance
- Poor attendance can affect your exam eligibility
- Teachers notice and appreciate consistent attendance

### Checking Your Attendance

1. Go to **Attendance** from the main menu
2. You'll see:
   - **Today's status** — Present, Absent, or Late
   - **This week** — How many days you attended
   - **This term** — Overall attendance percentage
   - **Calendar view** — Visual showing which days you were present/absent

**Color coding:**
- ✅ **Green** — Present
- ❌ **Red** — Absent
- ⏰ **Yellow** — Late
- 🏥 **Blue** — Excused absence

### Understanding Attendance Percentage

Your attendance percentage = (Days present ÷ Total school days) × 100

**Example:**
- Total school days: 100
- Days you attended: 85
- Your attendance: 85%

**What's good attendance?**
- **90-100%** — Excellent! Keep it up
- **80-89%** — Good, but room for improvement
- **75-79%** — Borderline, try not to miss more days
- **Below 75%** — Concerning, you may face consequences

### What If You Need to Miss School?

**If you're sick or have an emergency:**

1. **Tell your parents immediately**
2. **Parent should contact the school** (call or message)
3. **Bring a note when you return** (from parent or doctor if you were sick)
4. **Your absence may be marked as "Excused"**

**Excused absences** usually don't count against you as harshly as unexcused absences.

### Catching Up After Absence

When you miss school:
1. **Check 4SCH for assignments** you missed
2. **Message classmates** to find out what was covered
3. **Talk to your teachers** to get notes or extra help
4. **Submit late work** as soon as possible

:::caution Warning
Don't skip school without a good reason! Attendance is tracked and poor attendance can:
- Lower your grades
- Get you suspended from exams
- Result in disciplinary action
- Affect your ability to graduate
:::

## School Announcements

Stay updated with what's happening at school.

### Where to Find Announcements

**On Your Dashboard:**
- Recent announcements show at the top
- Click "View All" to see older announcements

**In the Announcements Section:**
- Go to **Announcements** or **News**
- See all messages from:
  - School administrators
  - Your teachers
  - School activities/clubs

### Types of Announcements You'll See

- 📅 **School events** — Sports day, cultural week, holidays
- 📚 **Academic reminders** — Exam dates, report card collection
- 🏫 **Policy changes** — New rules or procedures
- 🎉 **Celebrations** — Student achievements, birthdays
- ⚠️ **Important alerts** — School closures, emergency information

### Making Sure You Don't Miss Important Info

**Enable notifications** so you get alerts for:
- Exam schedule releases
- School closures
- Urgent updates

**Check announcements daily** — just like you check social media!

## Tips for Students Using 4SCH

### Daily Habits

**Every morning:**
- ✅ Check your dashboard for today's schedule
- ✅ See what assignments are due this week
- ✅ Read any new announcements

**Every evening:**
- ✅ Check if any new grades were posted
- ✅ See if teachers sent messages
- ✅ Review tomorrow's timetable

### Before Exams

**2 weeks before:**
- Download exam timetable
- Check which topics will be covered
- Access past papers and practice

**1 week before:**
- Review all your grades to identify weak areas
- Focus study time on subjects you're struggling with
- Make sure you have all study materials

**Day before each exam:**
- Confirm exam time and venue
- Prepare materials (pen, pencil, calculator)
- Get good sleep!

### Staying Organized

**Use 4SCH to:**
- Set reminders for assignment deadlines
- Track which assignments you've completed
- Monitor your grade progress
- Keep all school documents in one place

**Create a routine:**
- Check 4SCH at the same time every day
- Submit assignments early when possible
- Respond to teacher messages promptly

:::tip Success Strategy
Successful students check 4SCH daily, submit assignments on time, and communicate with teachers when they need help. Make 4SCH part of your daily routine!
:::

## Privacy & Your Account

### What Information Can Others See?

**Your classmates CAN'T see:**
- ❌ Your grades
- ❌ Your attendance details
- ❌ Your personal messages

**Your teachers CAN see:**
- ✅ Your grades (in their subjects)
- ✅ Your attendance (in their classes)
- ✅ Your assignment submissions

**Your parents CAN see:**
- ✅ Everything about you (grades, attendance, fees)

### Keeping Your Account Secure

**Password Safety:**
- Don't share your password with friends
- Use a password you can remember but others can't guess
- Change it if you think someone knows it

**Log Out:**
- Always log out if using a shared computer
- Don't stay logged in on friends' phones
