# Driver Guide

Welcome! As a transportation driver in the 4SCH system, you use the mobile app to manage your daily trips, track students, mark attendance, and communicate with parents and school administrators. This guide will help you use all the features available to you.

:::info Who is this guide for?
This guide is for **school transportation drivers** who use the 4SCH Staff Mobile App (Android/iOS) to manage student transportation.
:::

## Getting Started

### Logging In

**On the mobile app:**
1. Download **4SCH Staff App** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app
3. Enter your school code (ask your coordinator if you don't know it)
4. Enter your registered email/mobile and password
5. Tap **Login**

## Managing Daily Trips

### Starting a Trip

**To begin your route:**
1. From the dashboard, tap **Start Trip** on the scheduled trip
2. Select trip type:
   - **Morning Pickup** - Going to school
   - **Afternoon Drop** - Returning home
3. Review the route and student list
4. Tap **Start** to begin

<Screenshot
  src="/img/screens/drivers/start-trip.svg"
  alt="Start Trip Screen"
  caption="Start Trip - Begin your route"
/>

:::caution Important
Always start the trip when you begin your route. This allows parents to track the bus in real-time.
:::

### Live Trip Tracking

Once a trip is started:
- Your location is shared in real-time with parents and school
- Parents can see estimated arrival time at their stop
- School can monitor trip progress
- You can mark students as picked up/dropped off

**Trip Status Indicators:**
- 🟢 **In Progress** - Trip is active
- 🟡 **At Stop** - Stopped at a pickup/drop point
- 🔴 **Delayed** - Running behind schedule
- ✅ **Completed** - Trip finished

### Marking Student Attendance

At each stop, you need to mark attendance:

**Pickup (Morning Trip):**
1. Arrive at the pickup point
2. Tap the stop on your route
3. Mark each student:
   - ✅ **Present** - Student boarded the bus
   - ❌ **Absent** - Student didn't show up
   - 🏥 **On Leave** - Pre-informed leave
4. Tap **Continue** to next stop

<Screenshot
  src="/img/screens/drivers/student-attendance.svg"
  alt="Student Attendance Marking"
  caption="Mark Attendance - Track who's on board"
/>

**Drop-off (Afternoon Trip):**
1. Arrive at the drop point
2. Tap the stop
3. Mark each student as **Dropped**
4. Confirm parent/guardian pickup if required
5. Tap **Continue** to next stop

:::tip Best Practice
Always confirm student identity before marking them present. Safety is priority!
:::

### Viewing Your Route

**To see your assigned route:**
1. Go to **Routes** from the menu
2. You'll see:
   - List of all stops in order
   - Student names at each stop
   - Pickup/drop times
   - Stop addresses with map view

<Screenshot
  src="/img/screens/drivers/route-details.svg"
  alt="Route Details"
  caption="Route Details - All stops and students"
/>

**Route Information Includes:**
- Stop name and address
- Number of students at each stop
- Scheduled pickup/drop time
- Distance to next stop
- Total route distance and estimated time

### Ending a Trip

**To complete your trip:**
1. After the last drop-off, tap **End Trip**
2. Confirm all students have been dropped
3. Add any notes or incidents (optional)
4. Tap **Complete**

The trip will be marked as completed and logged in the system.

**To report a problem:**

2. Select issue type:

   - **Student Issue** - Student behavior, emergency

In case you need to contact a parent:
1. Go to the student's profile
2. Tap **Contact Parent**

   - **Call** - Direct phone call
   - **Message** - In-app message (if enabled)

**When to Contact Parents:**
- Student not at pickup point

- Late arrival notification

## Best Practices

### Daily Checklist

✅ **Before Starting Your Day:**
- Check your schedule and route
- Verify vehicle condition
- Review student list for any new additions
- Check for route changes or announcements

✅ **During Trips:**
- Start trip in the app when you begin
- Mark attendance accurately at each stop
- Be punctual and follow the schedule
- Report delays or issues immediately
- Maintain student safety at all times

✅ **After Completing Trips:**
- End trip in the app
- Report any incidents or issues
- Check for next day's schedule
- Ensure all students were safely dropped

### Safety Guidelines

🚸 **Student Safety:**
- Always verify student identity before boarding
- Ensure students are seated before moving
- Check all seats before leaving each stop
- Never leave students unattended
- Use emergency procedures if needed

🚗 **Driving Safety:**
- Follow all traffic rules
- Maintain safe speed
- Use seat belts and safety equipment
- Avoid phone use while driving (except hands-free)
- Report road hazards or route issues

### Attendance Accuracy

✓ **Mark attendance correctly:**
- Present only if student actually boards
- Absent if student doesn't show up after waiting
- Never mark attendance in advance
- Double-check before confirming each stop

### Communication Tips

💬 **Professional Communication:**
- Be polite and respectful with parents
- Respond to coordinator messages promptly
- Report issues clearly and accurately
- Keep school informed of delays
- Maintain student confidentiality

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Problem: Can't start a trip**
- ✓ Check if you have internet connection
- ✓ Ensure trip is scheduled for today
- ✓ Verify you're assigned to this route
- ✓ Contact coordinator if issue persists

**Problem: GPS not working**
- ✓ Enable location services in phone settings
- ✓ Grant location permission to the app
- ✓ Restart the app
- ✓ Check if GPS is enabled on your device

**Problem: Student not in my list**
- ✓ Refresh the student list
- ✓ Check if route was recently changed
- ✓ Contact coordinator to verify assignment
- ✓ Don't board unassigned students without approval

**Problem: Can't mark attendance**
- ✓ Ensure trip is started
- ✓ Check internet connection
- ✓ Refresh the screen
- ✓ Try again or report to coordinator

**Problem: App not loading**
- ✓ Check internet connection (WiFi or mobile data)
- ✓ Close and reopen the app
- ✓ Update app to latest version
- ✓ Clear app cache in phone settings
- ✓ Reinstall the app if needed

## FAQs

**Q: What if a student is not at the pickup point?**
A: Wait for the scheduled time (usually 2-3 minutes). If student doesn't appear, mark them as absent and continue. The parent will be notified.

**Q: Can I change my route?**
A: No, routes are assigned by the coordinator. If you need a route change, contact your coordinator.

**Q: What if I'm running late?**
A: Report the delay immediately using "Report Issue" so parents and school can be notified.

**Q: Do I need internet during the trip?**
A: Yes, internet is needed for live tracking and attendance marking. Ensure you have mobile data.

**Q: What if the app crashes during a trip?**
A: Reopen the app and continue marking attendance. The trip will resume from where it was. If it doesn't, contact the coordinator.

**Q: Can parents see my phone number?**
A: Only if the school administrator enables it. Otherwise, they can only message through the app.

**Q: How do I know if there's a route change?**
A: You'll receive a notification and the route will be updated in the Routes section. Always check before starting trips.

**Q: What if I need emergency leave?**
A: Call the school immediately, then apply for leave in the app. The coordinator will arrange a replacement driver.
