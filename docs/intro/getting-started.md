# Getting Started with 4SCH

import Link from '@docusaurus/Link';

Whether you're new to 4SCH or helping your school transition from paper records or another system, this guide will walk you through the first steps.

## New to 4SCH? Register Your School

**4SCH** is a modern enterprise School Management System designed to elevate service delivery for schools, enabling schools to much more effectively orchestrate a very productive teaching and learning process using modern technology.

### Registration Process

**Step 1:** Go to the website: [www.4sch.com](https://www.4sch.com)

**Step 2:** Register Your School if you and your school are new to 4SCH SMS

**Step 3:** Check your email inbox (check spam folder if you didn't see an email from 4SCH). If no mail is found, you may have entered your email incorrectly—contact us.

**Step 4:** If you receive an email acknowledging receipt of your registration, it means we have received and are reviewing your school application. If our team requires more info from your school, you will be contacted.

:::tip Need Help with Registration?
Contact our support team:
- **Email:** info@ng.4sch.com
- **Phone/WhatsApp:** +234 704 151 8843
- **Follow us:** @ng4sch on social media
:::

## Your First Login

After your school subscribes to 4SCH, you'll receive login credentials via email or SMS.

**Step 1: Access the System**
1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Safari recommended)
2. Go to your school's 4SCH URL (e.g., `yourschool.4sch.com`)
3. Enter the username and password provided
4. You'll be asked to change your password on first login

**Step 2: Understand Your Dashboard**

What you see depends on your role:
- **Administrators** see school-wide statistics, pending tasks, and quick access to settings
- **Teachers** see today's classes, attendance reminders, and recent assignments
- **Parents** see their children's recent activity, upcoming events, and fee status
- **Students** see their timetable, assignments due, and recent grades

:::tip First Time Using 4SCH?
Don't worry if things look unfamiliar. Click around and explore—you can't break anything! Each section of the dashboard has help text explaining what it does.
:::

## What to Do First (By Role)

### If You're a School Administrator

Your main job is to set up the school structure so everyone else can use the system.

**Week 1: Basic Setup**
1. ✅ Create the current academic session (e.g., 2024/2025)
2. ✅ Add terms/trimesters within the session
3. ✅ Add all classes your school offers (e.g., Primary 1-6, JSS 1-3, SSS 1-3)
4. ✅ Add sections/streams if you have multiple classes (e.g., JSS 1A, JSS 1B)

**Week 2: Staff and Subjects**
1. ✅ Add all teachers and assign them roles
2. ✅ Add subjects taught at each level
3. ✅ Assign teachers to their subjects and classes
4. ✅ Add non-teaching staff (bursar, secretary, front desk staff)

**Week 3: Students and Parents**
1. ✅ Add students (manually or by importing from Excel)
2. ✅ Add parent/guardian accounts for each student
3. ✅ Link students to their parents in the system

**Week 4: Final Configuration**
1. ✅ Set up fee structures and payment methods
2. ✅ Create the school timetable
3. ✅ Send welcome messages to all users
4. ✅ Train teachers on basic features

### If You're a Teacher

Your administrator has already set up the system. You just need to learn how to use it for your daily work.

**Your First Day**
1. ✅ Log in and check your dashboard
2. ✅ Review your assigned classes and subjects
3. ✅ Familiarize yourself with the class list
4. ✅ Check the school timetable

**Your First Week**
1. ✅ Mark attendance for your classes
2. ✅ Create your first assignment
3. ✅ Enter grades for a recent test
4. ✅ Send a message to parents

**Common Questions**
- *How do I mark attendance?* Go to Classes → Select your class → Mark Attendance
- *Where do I enter grades?* Go to Gradebook → Select subject → Enter marks
- *Can I message all parents at once?* Yes! Use Messaging → Select class group

## Common Setup Challenges (and Solutions)

### "I can't log in"
- **Check your username:** It might be your email, staff ID, or admission number
- **Reset your password:** Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen
- **Contact your admin:** They can verify your account is active

### "I don't see my classes" (Teachers)
- Your admin needs to assign you to classes in the system
- Contact your school admin to add you to the correct subjects

### "I can't see my child's information" (Parents)
- Your account may not be linked to your child yet
- Contact the school office to link your account properly

### "The system is slow"
- Check your internet connection
- Try closing other browser tabs
- Use Chrome or Firefox for best performance
- Contact your admin if the problem persists

## Moving from Paper to Digital

If your school previously used registers and notebooks, here's how 4SCH replaces them:

| Old Method | 4SCH Equivalent |
|------------|-----------------|
| Attendance register | Digital attendance marking (saves automatically) |
| Grade book | Online gradebook (calculates averages automatically) |
| Report cards | Generated automatically from entered grades |
| Fee receipts | Digital receipts (parents can download anytime) |
| Notice board | School announcements (sent to all phones instantly) |
| Teacher-parent meetings | In-app messaging (anytime communication) |

:::note
You don't have to stop using paper completely right away. Many schools run both systems side-by-side for one term while everyone gets comfortable with 4SCH.
:::
