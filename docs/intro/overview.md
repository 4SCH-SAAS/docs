# What is 4SCH?

import Link from '@docusaurus/Link';
import DataIcon from '@site/src/components/icons/Data';

**4SCH** is a complete school management system that helps your school run smoothly every day. Whether you're a school owner, administrator, teacher, parent, or student, 4SCH gives you the tools you need to stay organized and connected.

## Who is 4SCH for?

**School Administrators & Owners**  
Manage student records, staff, fees, academic calendars, and see reports on how your school is performing—all from one dashboard.

**Teachers**  
Take attendance, grade assignments, communicate with parents, and manage your classes without juggling multiple notebooks or systems.

**Parents**  
Check your child's attendance, view grades, pay school fees online, and message teachers—all from your phone or computer.

**Students**  
See your timetable, submit assignments, check your grades, and stay updated with school announcements.

## What can 4SCH do?

4SCH brings everything your school does into one place:

- **Student & Staff Records** — Keep all information organized and easy to find
- **Attendance Tracking** — Mark and monitor who's present each day
- **Grading & Report Cards** — Enter marks, calculate grades, and generate reports automatically
- **Timetable Management** — Create and share class schedules with everyone
- **Fee Management & Payments** — Track fees, send invoices, and accept online payments
- **Messaging & Announcements** — Send updates to teachers, parents, and students instantly
- **Reports & Insights** — Understand trends in attendance, academics, and finances

## Why use 4SCH?

**Save Time**  
Stop doing the same task twice. Enter information once and it's available everywhere it's needed.

**Stay Connected**  
Parents know what's happening at school. Teachers can reach parents instantly. Everyone stays informed.

**Work from Anywhere**  
Access 4SCH from your phone, tablet, or computer. No need to be in the school office.

**Reduce Errors**  
Automatic calculations mean fewer mistakes in grades, fees, and reports.

4SCH is built on **eSchool SaaS**, a proven school management platform. This means:

- **Regular Updates** — You benefit from continuous improvements and new features
- **Reliable & Secure** — Tested by schools worldwide with strong data protection
- **Cloud-Based** — No servers to maintain; access from anywhere with internet
- **Scalable** — Works for small schools and large institutions with multiple branches

## Understanding User Roles

4SCH gives different access levels based on what each person needs to do:

| Role | What They Can Do | Example Tasks |
|------|------------------|---------------|
| **Super Admin** | Full system control, school setup, billing | Create academic sessions, add new schools |
| **School Admin** | Manage school operations, staff, and settings | Add teachers, configure fees, view reports |
| **Teacher** | Classroom management and student assessment | Mark attendance, grade assignments, message parents |
| **Parent/Guardian** | Monitor child's progress and pay fees | Check grades, view attendance, pay invoices |
| **Student** | Access learning materials and view progress | Submit assignments, check timetable, see grades |
| **Non-Teaching Staff** | Administrative and support tasks | Handle admissions, manage transport, process payroll |

:::note Common Question
**Can one person have multiple roles?**  
Yes! For example, a teacher can also be an admin, or a parent might work as a staff member. Each person logs in once and sees all their roles.
:::

## System Requirements

To use 4SCH, you need:

**Internet Connection**  
- Minimum: 2G/3G mobile data or basic broadband
- Recommended: 4G or WiFi for faster loading

**Device**  
- **Computer:** Any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
- **Phone/Tablet:** Android 6.0+ or iOS 12+ with browser or mobile app

**Account**  
- Your school admin will create your account and send you login details
- You'll need an email address or phone number to receive notifications

:::tip For Schools with Limited Internet
4SCH works on basic internet connections. Pages are designed to load quickly even on slower networks. Consider having a dedicated WiFi connection in the school office for admin tasks.
:::
