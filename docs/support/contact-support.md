## Getting Support by Role

### School Administrators
- Contact your 4SCH success manager directly
- Submit a ticket via **Admin Console → Support**
- For urgent issues, use the emergency contact listed in **Admin Console → Support → On-call**

### Teachers, Parents & Students
1. **First:** Reach out to your school office—they can help with most issues
2. **If needed:** The school office can escalate your issue to 4SCH support with proper context
3. **Emergency:** For critical issues (like login problems during exams), contact your school admin

## Response Times

| Priority | Response Time | Examples |
|----------|--------------|----------|
| **Critical** | Within 2 hours | System down, data loss, security issues |
| **High** | Within 24 hours | Feature not working, exam-related issues |
| **Medium** | Within 48 hours | General questions, minor bugs |
| **Low** | Within 5 days | Feature requests, enhancement suggestions |

:::note Business Hours
Our support team operates Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM (West Africa Time). Critical issues are monitored 24/7.
:::

## Before Contacting Support

Help us help you faster by having this information ready:

1. **Your Details**
   - School name
   - Your role (Admin, Teacher, Parent, Student)
   - Your username or email

2. **Issue Description**
   - What were you trying to do?
   - What happened instead?
   - When did this start?
   - Does it happen every time?

3. **Screenshots** (if applicable)
   - Error messages
   - Unexpected behavior
   - Missing data

## Appendix: Data Import Templates

Need to import data? Use the built-in templates from **Admin Console → Import**.

### Typical CSV Headers

**Students:**
```
student_id, first_name, last_name, class, section, date_of_birth, guardian_email
```

**Staff:**
```
staff_id, first_name, last_name, email, role, subject
```

**Subjects:**
```
subject_code, subject_name, class, elective
```

**Enrollments:**
```
student_id, class, section, session, term
```

:::tip
Download the template from the import section to ensure your CSV matches our format exactly.
:::
