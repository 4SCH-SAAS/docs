# 4SCH Parent Quick Reference Card

**Print this card and keep it handy!** — All essential tasks in one place

---

## 🎯 QUICK START (Your First Steps)

1. **Log in** to 4SCH app or web portal
2. **Select your child** from dashboard
3. **Check 3 things daily:**
   - ✅ Attendance for the day
   - ✅ New messages from teachers
   - ✅ Outstanding fees due

---

## 📍 HOW TO CHECK CHILD'S ATTENDANCE

**From Dashboard:**
1. Open 4SCH → Select child
2. Look at attendance summary card
3. See today's status + percentage

**Full Attendance Report:**
1. Click child's name → **Attendance** tab
2. View calendar/list showing:
   - ✅ Present
   - ❌ Absent
   - ⏰ Late
   - 🏥 Excused (absent with permission)

**⚠️ Attendance Alert:** Schools require 75-80% minimum. If below 85%, contact the school.

---

## 📊 VIEW RESULTS & REPORT CARDS

**Check Test Scores & Grades:**
1. Go to **Grades** or **Results** tab
2. Select term/semester
3. See subject-by-subject breakdown
4. View test scores and percentage

**Download Report Card:**
1. **Grades** tab → Find report card section
2. Click **Download Report Card**
3. Save as PDF (print if needed)

**Understanding Grades:**
- 70-100% = A (Excellent)
- 60-69% = B (Very Good)
- 50-59% = C (Good)
- 40-49% = D (Pass)
- Below 40% = F (Fail)

## 💰 PAY SCHOOL FEES

### **STEP 1: View Your Invoice**
1. Go to **Fees** section
2. See invoice details:
   - Amount due
   - Due date
   - Fee breakdown (tuition, development levy, etc.)
3. Check for discounts (early payment, siblings)

### **STEP 2: Choose Payment Method**

**Best for Nigeria:**

| Method | Fee | Speed | Best For |
|--------|-----|-------|----------|
| **Paystack** | 1% (lowest) | Instant | Quick, cheap payment |
| **Flutterwave** | 1.4% | Instant | Alternative option |
| **Bank Transfer** | NGN 0-100 | 1-24 hrs | No fees (if patient) |
| **Cash at School** | NGN 0 | Instant | No cards needed |

### **STEP 3: Pay Online (Paystack Example)**

**Via 4SCH App:**
1. Click **Pay Now** on invoice
2. Select payment method: **Paystack**
3. Enter your Paystack phone number
4. Select amount (full or partial payment)
5. Click **Confirm**

**On Your Phone:**
1. Paystack prompt appears
2. Enter your **Paystack PIN**
3. Wait for confirmation

**✅ Payment complete!** Receipt auto-generates.

### **STEP 4: Verify Payment & Get Receipt**
1. Check invoice status: Should change to **PAID** ✓
2. Click **View Receipt** or **Download Receipt**
3. Save PDF to phone/computer
4. Keep as proof of payment

**💡 Money-Saving Tips:**
- Pay early = get discount (5-10%)
- Use Paystack = lowest fees (1%)
- Set reminders 5 days before due date
- Pay immediately after salary (avoid spending)

### **STEP 5: Paying in Installments (If Allowed)**
1. Choose **3 Installments** option
2. System splits fee into equal parts
3. Each installment has own due date
4. Pay each one like a regular invoice
5. Get reminders before each due date

⚠️ **Important:** Missing installment deadlines = late fees!

## 👨‍🏫 CONTACT TEACHERS

**When to Message:**
- ✅ Ask about child's progress
- ✅ Explain absences/late arrivals
- ✅ Ask about assignments
- ✅ Request parent-teacher meeting
- ❌ Don't message for urgent emergencies (CALL SCHOOL instead)

**How to Message:**
1. Go to child's profile
2. Click **Message Teacher**
3. Select teacher (class or subject teacher)
4. Write clear message
5. Click **Send**

**Response Time:** Usually within 24 hours on weekdays

**Good Message Example:**
> "Hi Mr. Okafor, Chidi struggled with yesterday's algebra homework (questions 5-8). Could you clarify these topics? Any extra practice problems? Thank you."

## ❓ COMMON QUESTIONS ANSWERED

**Q: How do I switch between my children?**
A: Click on dashboard → select different child from dropdown or tabs

**Q: Can I pay fees for multiple children at once?**
A: Check if school offers "Bulk Payment" option. Otherwise, pay each child separately.

**Q: When will my payment show in 4SCH?**
A: Online payment (Paystack/Flutterwave) = instant. Cash at office = within 24 hours. Bank transfer = 1-24 hours.

**Q: What if I see grades that seem wrong?**
A: Don't panic. Message the teacher politely asking for clarification. Teachers review before posting.

**Q: How often should I check 4SCH?**
A: At least once daily (evening is best) to catch attendance issues and new messages.

**Q: Can other parents see my child's information?**
A: No. You can ONLY see your own child's data. Privacy protected by school.

**Q: What's the minimum attendance percentage?**
A: Usually 75-80%. Your child may not sit exams if below this.

**Q: Can I get a receipt for my payment?**
A: Yes! Always download the PDF receipt after paying. Keep it for your records.

## 🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING

### **Can't Log In?**
- ✅ Check username (email, phone, or username from school)
- ✅ Click **Forgot Password** → reset password
- ✅ Contact school office if account not found

### **Payment Failed - "Insufficient Funds"**
- Check Paystack balance: Dial **\*234#** or open app
- Top up Paystack account
- Try payment again

### **Payment Failed - "Transaction Timeout"**
- **Don't pay again!**
- Check: Did money come out of Paystack?
- If YES: Wait 30 minutes, then check 4SCH
- Still pending? Contact school with Paystack SMS proof
- School will verify and update within 24 hours

### **Fees Show Unpaid But I Already Paid**
- Screenshot your Paystack confirmation SMS
- Screenshot the 4SCH payment screen showing "pending"
- Message school bursar with both screenshots
- Include: date, amount, payment method
- School verifies within 24 hours

### **App Won't Open / App Crashes**
- Restart phone
- Close app completely and reopen
- Check internet connection (WiFi or mobile data)
- Clear app cache (phone settings)
- Reinstall app if still broken

### **Not Receiving Notifications?**
- Go to **Profile** → **Notification Settings**
- Turn ON: Attendance, Fees, Grades, Announcements
- Check phone settings: Allow notifications
- Check email spam folder
- Verify phone number in profile is correct

### **Don't See Child's Information**
- Verify you're logged into correct account
- Contact school admin to link your account to child
- School may not have added child to system yet

## 🔐 SECURITY TIPS

**Protect Your Account:**
- ✅ Use STRONG password (letters + numbers + symbols)
- ✅ Don't share password with anyone (not even child)
- ✅ Log out on shared computers/phones
- ✅ Never give school your Paystack/bank PIN
- ✅ Always download receipts as proof

**Safe Payment Practices:**
- ✅ Only use payment methods in 4SCH app
- ✅ Don't send money to personal accounts
- ✅ Verify student name on invoice before paying
- ✅ Keep all payment receipts and SMS confirmations
- ✅ Don't pay twice if first payment shows "pending"
