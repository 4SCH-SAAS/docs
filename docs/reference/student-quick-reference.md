## 📝 DAILY TASKS

### ✅ Check Your Assignments

**Steps:**
1. Log in to your school portal
2. Go to **"Assignments"** or **"Coursework"**
3. Look for your subject folders
4. Click on each assignment to see:
   - What you need to do
   - When it is due
   - How many marks it is worth
5. Write down due dates in your notebook or phone

**💡 Pro Tip:** Check assignments every morning before school starts!

---

### 🕐 View Your Timetable

**Steps:**
1. Click on **"My Timetable"** or **"Schedule"** in the menu
2. View your classes for today and this week
3. Note the **class time** and **teacher's name**
4. If online, look for the **meeting link**

**⚠️ Important:** Always check your timetable 10 minutes before class starts!

---

### 📤 Submit Your Work

**Steps:**
1. Go to the assignment
2. Click **"Upload"** or **"Submit Work"**
3. Choose your file (Word, PDF, or image)
4. Add any comments if needed
5. Click **"SUBMIT"** button
6. **Wait for the green tick ✓** - this means it was received

**❌ Common Mistake:** Clicking submit but not seeing the confirmation. Always scroll down to check!

**📌 File Tips:**
- Use clear names: `Chukwudi_Mathematics_Assignment1.pdf`
- Keep files under 25MB
- Save as PDF for best results

## 🎯 TAKING ONLINE EXAMS

### Before the Exam
- ✅ Test your internet connection (use WiFi, not mobile data)
- ✅ Close all other browser tabs and apps
- ✅ Have a glass of water nearby
- ✅ Tell family members not to disturb you
- ✅ Log in **5 minutes early**

### During the Exam
1. **Read each question carefully** - Don't rush!
2. **Use the timer** - It counts down on your screen
3. **Skip difficult questions** - Come back to them later
4. **Don't close the browser** - You'll lose everything!
5. **Click NEXT to move** between questions

### After the Exam
- Wait for the "Your exam has been submitted" message
- **DO NOT close the page immediately**
- Wait 30 seconds to be sure
- Take a screenshot if possible
- Log out properly

### ⏱️ Timer Tips
- 60 minute exam = spend 1 min per mark (use this guide)
- Set your own phone alarm for 5 minutes before the end
- Questions at the end are usually easier - save time for them!

## 📊 VIEWING RESULTS & REPORT CARDS

### Check Assignment Marks
1. Go to **"My Grades"** or **"Marks"**
2. Click the subject
3. See your mark and teacher's feedback
4. 📌 **Teacher comments are important!** - Read them carefully

### View Your Report Card
1. Click **"Reports"** in the menu
2. Select the term/term (Term 1, Term 2, etc.)
3. See all your subjects and marks
4. Look for your position in class
5. **Download and print** if your school asks for it

### Understanding Your Marks
- **A (80-100)**: Excellent! ⭐⭐⭐
- **B (70-79)**: Very good! ⭐⭐
- **C (60-69)**: Good, but study harder
- **D (50-59)**: Need to improve - ask teacher for help
- **F (Below 50)**: Meet with teacher immediately

## 👨‍🏫 CONTACTING TEACHERS

### Send a Message
1. Click **"Messages"** in the menu
2. Find your teacher's name
3. Click **"New Message"**
4. Write professionally:
   - Start with "Good morning Sir/Ma"
   - State your problem clearly
   - Ask your question
   - End with "Thank you"
5. Click **SEND**

### Example Message
> Good morning Sir,
>
> I have a question about the Mathematics assignment due on Friday. Can you explain question 5 again? I don't understand how to calculate the percentage.
>
> Thank you, Sir.
>
> Chukwudi Okafor
> JSS 2 Green

### When to Contact Teachers
- ❓ You don't understand a lesson
- 📝 You can't access an assignment
- 📊 You want to know why you got low marks
- ⏰ You'll miss a deadline (tell them before!)
- 🆘 You have technical problems

**⏱️ Response Time:** Teachers usually reply within 24 hours. Don't send the same message 5 times!

## ❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

### Q: I Forgot My Password
**What to do:**
1. Go to the **"Login"** page
2. Click **"Forgot Password?"**
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your email (including spam folder)
5. Click the link in the email
6. Create a NEW password
7. Try logging in again

**If no email comes:**
- Wait 10 minutes (sometimes slow)
- Check spam/junk folder
- Contact your school's **IT Support**
- Go to school and tell a teacher

---

### Q: My Assignment Won't Upload
**Try this:**
1. ✓ Check your internet connection (move closer to WiFi)
2. ✓ Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
3. ✓ Make sure file is smaller than 25MB
4. ✓ Try refreshing the page (press F5)
5. ✓ Delete browser cookies and try again
6. ✓ Use a different computer if available

**Still not working?**
- Take a screenshot showing the error
- Email the IT support with the screenshot
- Go submit the work to your teacher in person
- Don't wait until the deadline!

---

### Q: The Exam Timer Issues
**My timer disappeared:**
- Don't panic! Refresh the page (F5)
- The timer still counts down, just not visible
- Continue answering questions
- Submit your work

**Timer moved too fast:**
- Contact your teacher immediately
- Explain what happened
- They may give you extra time
- Have screenshots as proof

**I ran out of time:**
- Submit what you have
- Email your teacher explaining
- Don't leave answers blank if you know them

---

### Q: I Can't See My Grades
**Solutions:**
1. Make sure you're in the right **class/form**
2. Refresh the page
3. Try logging out and back in
4. Use a different browser
5. Contact your teacher - marks may not be posted yet

---

### Q: Class Link Won't Work
**Try:**
1. Copy and paste the link carefully
2. Make sure you're logged in first
3. Try the link on your phone if computer doesn't work
4. Email teacher the error message
5. Join using the subject name in your timetable

## 🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING TIPS

### Internet Problems
| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Page loads slowly | Move closer to WiFi router |
| Connection drops | Have a backup (mobile data) ready |
| WiFi keeps disconnecting | Restart your router (turn off, wait 2 mins, turn on) |
| Video won't play | Use lower video quality (ask teacher) |

### Account Problems
| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't log in | Check caps lock, try forgot password |
| Logged out suddenly | Password changed? Check email for security alerts |
| Account locked | Contact IT support immediately |
| Wrong name/class shown | Email teacher to fix your profile |

### Assignment Problems
| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't find assignment | Refresh page, check right subject folder |
| Submitted but not showing | Wait 5 minutes, refresh, try again |
| Can't open document | Try different browser or download it |
| File won't upload | Reduce file size, use PDF format |

### Exam Problems
| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't start exam | Check time (don't start early), refresh page |
| Questions loading slowly | Be patient, don't click twice |
| Audio/video won't play | Ask teacher for extension, use different device |
| Accidentally closed browser | Log back in immediately, you may have time left |

## ✨ STUDENT SUCCESS TIPS

✅ **DO:**
- Check your portal every day
- Read teacher feedback on your work
- Start assignments early, don't wait until the last day
- Ask questions when you don't understand
- Keep your password safe

❌ **DON'T:**

- Submit work that isn't yours
- Disable your webcam during exams
- Upload files with virus (scan first!)
- Send rude messages to teachers
- Leave assignments until the deadline

## 📱 QUICK TIPS FOR MOBILE

**Using your phone to access the portal?**
- ✓ Use a recent browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge)
- ✓ Turn phone to landscape for better view
- ✓ Don't use the phone for video exams (small screen)
- ✓ Keep at least 500MB free storage
- ✓ Mobile data can be slow - use WiFi when possible
